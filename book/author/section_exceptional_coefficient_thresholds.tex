\subsection{Fixed exceptional orders and coefficient thresholds}
\label{sec:exceptional-coefficient-thresholds}

The fixed divisor in \cref{ex:exceptional-semipositive-fiber-series} absorbs a range of local integrability conditions. Increasing the selecting coefficient eventually requires additional vanishing at the point blown up. Both this change and the loss of positive reference mass can be computed from the same series.

Retain the blowup $\rho:B\to\mathbb P^q$, $q\geq2$, its exceptional divisor $E$, the resolved projection $\tau$, forms $\alpha,\gamma,\kappa$, and the quotient metric of curvature $\eta$ from that example. Write $x=\rho(E)$ for the point blown up. Let $k,e\geq1$ be integers and equip
\[
 A_e=\rho^*\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^q}(k)\otimes\mathcal O_B(eE),
 \qquad \theta=k\alpha+e\eta
\]
with the product metric. Take $X=B\times\mathbb P^1$ and its projection $\pi$, a marked fiber point $o$, and $R=\|\pi^*s_E\|^2$, $T=\|s_o\|^2$ as before. For positive integers $p,b$ and a real $c>0$, put
\[
 \varphi_c=\frac cp\log(R^p+T^b),\quad
 \omega_c=c\pi^*\kappa+(cb/p+1)\omega_{\mathbb P^1},\quad
 a=\frac pb,
\]
and let $V_m(c)$ be the descended space selected by $\mathcal I(m\varphi_c)$ in $H^0(B,A_e^m)$.

\Needspace{14\baselineskip}
\begin{proposition}\label{prop:exceptional-coefficient-series}\index{Holomorphic section!exceptional coefficient threshold}
For every $c>0$, $\varphi_c\in\PSH(X,\omega_c)$ and the exact selected space is
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 V_m(c)=s_E^{em}\rho^*H^0\bigl(\mathbb P^q,\mathcal O(km)\otimes\mathfrak m_x^{v_m}\bigr),\\
 v_m=\max\{\lfloor m(c-e)-a\rfloor,0\},\qquad m\geq1,
 \end{gathered}
\]
where $\mathfrak m_x$ is the ideal of $x$. For $0<c\leq e+k$, put
\[
 d=\max\{c,e\},\quad \delta=d-e,\quad
 \psi_d=d\log\|s_E\|^2,\quad \ell_m=\lfloor m\delta\rfloor.
\]
Then $\psi_d\in\PSH(B,\theta)$ and
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 \mathrm{MA}_\theta(\psi_d)=\bigl((k-\delta)\alpha+\delta\gamma\bigr)^q,
 \qquad \int_B\mathrm{MA}_\theta(\psi_d)=k^q-\delta^q.
 \end{gathered}
\]
The reference spaces satisfy
\[
 \begin{split}
 W_m(c)&:=H^0\bigl(B,A_e^m\otimes\mathcal I(m\psi_d)\bigr)\\
   &=s_E^{em}\rho^*H^0\bigl(\mathbb P^q,\mathcal O(km)\otimes\mathfrak m_x^{\ell_m}\bigr)
   \subseteq V_m(c).
 \end{split}
\]
For $0<c\leq e$, both spaces equal $H^0(B,A_e^m)$ for every $m$. For $e<c<e+k$, their exact defect and leading dimension are
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 \dim V_m(c)-\dim W_m(c)
    =\binom{\ell_m+q-1}{q}-\binom{v_m+q-1}{q}=O(m^{q-1}),\\
 \dim V_m(c)=\frac{k^q-(c-e)^q}{q!}m^q+O(m^{q-1}).
 \end{gathered}
\]
Here $\binom{q-1}{q}=0$. At $c=e+k$ the displayed reference has zero mass and
\[
 \dim V_m(e+k)
   =\frac{(\lceil a\rceil+1)k^{q-1}}{(q-1)!}m^{q-1}+O(m^{q-2}).
\]
For $c>e+k$, the selected space is zero exactly when
\[
 m(c-e-k)\geq a+1.
\]
In particular it vanishes for every $m\geq\lceil(a+1)/(c-e-k)\rceil$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The norm identity in \cref{ex:exceptional-semipositive-fiber-series} writes $R^p+T^b$ over the common denominator for $\mathcal O(p,b)$ on $\mathbb P^q\times\mathbb P^1$, pulled back to $X$. Its numerator is a sum of squared holomorphic coefficients. The log-sum Hessian and local psh extension give
\[
 \ddc\varphi_c\geq-c\pi^*\alpha-(cb/p)\omega_{\mathbb P^1}.
\]
Thus $\omega_c+\ddc\varphi_c\geq c\pi^*\gamma+\omega_{\mathbb P^1}\geq0$.

Hartogs extension as in the preceding example gives every global section of $A_e^m$ uniquely as $s_E^{em}\rho^*s$, with $s\in H^0(\mathbb P^q,\mathcal O(km))$. If $s$ has order $n$ at the point blown up, this section has generic order $em+n$ along $E$. In a pole chart the model is $\varphi_c=(c/p)\log(|z|^{2p}+|w|^{2b})+O(1)$. The generic unit-coefficient argument and \cref{lem:anisotropic-fiber-integrability} require
\[
 em+n+1>mc-a,
 \quad\hbox{equivalently}\quad
 n\geq\max\{\lfloor m(c-e)-a\rfloor,0\}.
\]
Conversely this divisor order gives integrability in every pole chart; the selector is locally bounded elsewhere. Pullback of the point ideal has order $n$ on $E$, as seen from the blowup coordinates in \cref{ex:modified-point-fiber-series}. This proves the exact formula for $V_m(c)$.

For $0\leq\delta\leq k$, Poincar\'e--Lelong gives
\[
 \theta+\ddc\psi_d=(k-\delta)\alpha+\delta\gamma+d[E]\geq0.
\]
The non-pluripolar measure discards $E$ and equals the top power of the smooth residual. Its class is $k\rho^*c_1(\mathcal O(1))-\delta[E]$. The mixed intersection numbers vanish and $E^q=(-1)^{q-1}$, as computed in \cref{ex:modified-point-fiber-series}, so its mass is $k^q-\delta^q$. The divisor ideal is $\mathcal O_B(-\lfloor md\rfloor E)$. Since $em$ is an integer, its condition on $s$ is exactly $n\geq\ell_m$. This yields the formula for $W_m(c)$ and its inclusion. If $c\leq e$, both point orders are zero.

For an integer $0\leq j\leq km+1$, homogeneous polynomials of degree $km$ realize every jet of total degree below $j$ at the marked point. The number of those jets is $\binom{j+q-1}{q}$. Hence
\[
 h^0\bigl(\mathbb P^q,\mathcal O(km)\otimes\mathfrak m_x^j\bigr)
   =\binom{km+q}{q}-\binom{j+q-1}{q}.
\]
For $e<c<e+k$, $0\leq v_m\leq\ell_m\leq km$ and $\ell_m-v_m\leq\lceil a\rceil$. Subtraction gives the exact defect. Each additional jet degree has $O(m^{q-1})$ monomials, giving its bound; $v_m=m(c-e)+O(1)$ gives the leading dimension.

At $c=e+k$, $v_m=\max\{km-\lceil a\rceil,0\}$. For large $m$, the allowed monomials have point orders $km-\lceil a\rceil,\ldots,km$. Each of these $\lceil a\rceil+1$ degrees has leading count $k^{q-1}m^{q-1}/(q-1)!$, proving the displayed asymptotic. The residual there is $k\gamma$, whose top power is zero. For $c>e+k$, a nonzero polynomial has point order at most $km$. Thus $V_m(c)=0$ exactly when $v_m>km$, which is equivalent to $m(c-e-k)-a\geq1$, including equality.
\end{proof}

For $0<c<e+k$ the constructed reference has positive mass, and the section comparison answers the sufficient-condition part of \cref{qu:fiber-selected-approximation} for this family. At the threshold the reference mass vanishes and section growth drops by one degree; beyond it the selected spaces eventually disappear. These computations concern this fixed exceptional-divisor family, not necessary conditions for arbitrary selecting potentials.
