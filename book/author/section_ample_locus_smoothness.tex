\section{Smooth equations on the ample locus}
\label{sec:ample-locus-smoothness}

Let $X$ now be K\"ahler and let $\alpha$ be a big closed class, with smooth representative $\theta$. Write $\operatorname{Amp}(\alpha)=X\setminus E_{\mathrm{nK}}(\alpha)$. The full-mass solution for a smooth positive volume form has minimal singularities. A strict analytic current supplies a barrier on the ample locus, where the interior estimate of \cref{cor:big-lp-interior-smoothness} gives smoothness.

Let $\operatorname{vol}(\alpha)$ be the classical volume and $\mathcal E(X,\theta)$ its full-mass class.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:big-class-ample-smoothness}\index{Ample locus!smoothness}
Let $\mu$ be a smooth positive volume form with
$\mu(X)=\operatorname{vol}(\alpha)$. The normalized full-mass solution
$\varphi\in\mathcal E(X,\theta)$ of
\begin{equation}\label{eq:big-class-smooth-volume}
 \bigl\langle(\theta+\ddc\varphi)^n\bigr\rangle=\mu,
 \qquad \sup_X\varphi=0,
\end{equation}
is smooth on $\operatorname{Amp}(\alpha)$. The restriction of
$\theta+\ddc\varphi$ to this locus is a K\"ahler form.
\end{theorem}

Existence and uniqueness are the classical closed-big equation theorem recorded in \cref{sec:history-regularity}. We prove the regularity assertion.

\begin{proof}
Fix a K\"ahler form $\omega$ and put $f=\mu/\omega^n$.
Compactness and smoothness give $f\in L^p(X,\omega^n)$ for every
finite $p>1$. The classical minimal-singularity estimate for this closed-big equation, recorded in \cref{sec:history-regularity}, gives a constant $C>0$ such that
\begin{equation}\label{eq:ample-solution-minimal-bounds}
 V_\theta-C\leq\varphi\leq V_\theta.
\end{equation}
The upper bound follows from the normalization.

Choose a strict analytic current $T$ of \cref{thm:bc-non-kahler-locus}. Since its Bott--Chern class is $\alpha$, it has a potential $\rho$ with analytic singularities and smooth remainder. For some constant $\varepsilon>0$ it satisfies
\[
 T=\theta+\ddc\rho\geq\varepsilon\omega,
 \qquad \{\rho=-\infty\}=E_{\mathrm{nK}}(\alpha).
\]
Thus $\rho$ is smooth on
$\Omega=\operatorname{Amp}(\alpha)$. Subtract a constant so that
$\rho\leq0$, hence $\rho\leq V_\theta$. By
\eqref{eq:ample-solution-minimal-bounds},
\[
 \rho-C\leq\varphi\leq0.
\]
Thus $\varphi$ is locally bounded on $\Omega$, and locality identifies its non-pluripolar equation there with the bounded-potential equation. The density $f$ is globally in $L^p$ and is smooth and strictly positive on $\Omega$. All hypotheses of \cref{cor:big-lp-interior-smoothness} hold for the strict barrier $\rho$, so $\varphi$ is smooth and $\theta+\ddc\varphi>0$ on $\Omega$. Closedness of $\theta$ makes this form K\"ahler.
\end{proof}

The global integrability used here follows from smoothness of $\mu$ on compact $X$. Metric behavior near the omitted set requires further information. We first compare geometric density zeros and radial singularities; the later exponential example shows how a divisorial singularity of the solution can cancel a density pole and leave finite distance.
