\section{The Hopf flag and its lower-dimensional body}

The coordinate divisors on a scalar Hopf manifold reduce a homogeneous section to a nowhere-vanishing section on an elliptic fiber. The final valuation coordinate is therefore zero, and the remaining coordinates fill a simplex whose intrinsic volume gives the section growth.

Use $X_n$ and $L_k$ from \cref{prop:hopf-bergman-exact}, with $n\geq2$ and $k\geq0$. In the covering coordinates $z_1,\ldots,z_n$, choose a point $p$ on the elliptic curve $z_1=\cdots=z_{n-1}=0$. The flag is $Y_0=X_n$ and
\[
 Y_i=\{z_1=\cdots=z_i=0\}/\langle \mathrm{e}^{-1}\rangle
 \quad(1\leq i\leq n-1),\qquad Y_n=\{p\}\subset Y_{n-1}.
\]
Each member is smooth and compact and is a prime divisor in the preceding member. Take $W_m=H^0(X_n,L_k^m)$.

\Needspace{17\baselineskip}
\begin{proposition}\label{prop:hopf-flag-simplex}
For this flag,
\[
 \{\nu(s):0\neq s\in W_m\}
 =\{(a_1,\ldots,a_{n-1},0):a_i\in\mathbb N,
                                  \ \textstyle\sum_i a_i\leq km\}.
\]
Consequently
\[
 \Delta(W_\bullet)=
 \{(x_1,\ldots,x_{n-1},0):x_i\geq0,
                                  \ \textstyle\sum_i x_i\leq k\}.
\]
Its $n$-dimensional volume is zero. When $k>0$, its volume in the coordinate hyperplane $x_n=0$, with the Euclidean measure for which the coordinate lattice has covolume one, is
\[
 \frac{k^{n-1}}{(n-1)!}
 =\lim_{m\to\infty}\frac{\dim W_m}{m^{n-1}}.
\]
For $k=0$ the body is a point and $\dim W_m=1$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
By \cref{prop:hopf-bergman-exact}, sections are homogeneous polynomials of degree $km$. Dividing successively by the least powers of $z_1,\ldots,z_{n-1}$ and restricting leaves a nonzero multiple of $z_n^{km-a_1-\cdots-a_{n-1}}$. This residual section has no zeros on $Y_{n-1}$, so the final valuation is zero. The preceding coordinates are nonnegative and sum to at most $km$.

Conversely every such tuple is realized by the monomial
\[
 z_1^{a_1}\cdots z_{n-1}^{a_{n-1}}
 z_n^{km-a_1-\cdots-a_{n-1}}.
\]
The normalized values lie in the displayed simplex and include all its vertices in degree one. Their closed convex hull is therefore that simplex. For $k>0$, its coordinate-lattice volume is $k^{n-1}/(n-1)!$, the leading coefficient of the same proposition's binomial count. For $k=0$, only constant polynomials remain.
\end{proof}
