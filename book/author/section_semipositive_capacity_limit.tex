\begin{corollary}\label{cor:semipositive-dual-capacity-limit}
Under the hypotheses of \cref{prop:semipositive-dual-exponential-limit}, suppose in addition that the obstacle envelope $P_{\delta\omega}(u_0)$ of \cref{sec:obstacle-envelopes} is not identically $-\infty$ for some real $0<\delta<1$. Then $v_\lambda\to u_0$ in capacity as $\lambda\downarrow0$. In particular this holds if the threshold solution $u_0$ is bounded.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Fix a sequence $\lambda_j\downarrow0$. \Cref{prop:semipositive-dual-exponential-limit} gives $u_0-C\leq v_{\lambda_j}\leq0$ and $v_{\lambda_j}\to u_0$ in $L^1$. For all $j$ with $\lambda_j\leq1$,
\[
 \mathrm{MA}^{\mathrm{ext}}_\omega(v_{\lambda_j})
   =c_{\lambda_j}\mathrm{e}^{\lambda_jv_{\lambda_j}}f\,\mathrm{d}V
   \leq b\mathrm{e}^{C}f\,\mathrm{d}V.
\]
The bounded functions $|\mathrm{e}^{v_{\lambda_j}}-\mathrm{e}^{u_0}|$ tend to zero in $\mathrm{d}V$-measure. Since $f\in L^1$, their integrals against $f\,\mathrm{d}V$ tend to zero, so the error term in \eqref{eq:common-barrier-self-error} tends to zero. We apply \cref{prop:lower-barrier-criterion} with $\theta=\omega$ and $\varphi=u_0$; its additional hypothesis that the $L^1$ limit $u$ satisfies $u\preceq\varphi$, in the notation of \cref{def:singularity-order}, holds trivially, since $u=u_0$. This proves convergence in capacity along every sequence, and hence for the family. Finally, if $u_0$ is bounded, a constant below it is a competitor for $P_{\delta\omega}(u_0)$, so this envelope is not identically $-\infty$.
\end{proof}

In general, the condition on $P_{\delta\omega}(u_0)$ is an additional hypothesis. The next subsection shows that it holds when the Monge--Amp\`ere mass of $\omega$ is invariant, or when the singularity of $u_0$ satisfies a quantitative bound.
