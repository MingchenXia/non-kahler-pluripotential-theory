\section{Minimal singularities on blowups}\label{sec:blowups}

An exceptional divisor can be forced by the background itself. Its coefficient is then part of the minimal ordinary type, while the remainder descends to the target. The density changes by the Jacobian, which determines the loss of integrability.

Let $(Y,h)$ be a connected compact Hermitian $n$-fold, $n\geq2$, let $Z\subset Y$ be a connected smooth center of codimension $r\geq2$, and write $\pi:X=\operatorname{Bl}_ZY\to Y$ with exceptional divisor $E$. Choose a smooth metric on $\mathcal O(E)$, put $L=\log|s_E|^2$, and fix $c>0$. The smooth background
\begin{equation}\label{eq:blowup-background}
 \theta=\pi^*h+c\Theta_E
\end{equation}
can be indefinite, and it is nonclosed when $h$ is nonclosed. Positivity is imposed on $\theta+\ddc u$, not on $\theta$.

\begin{proposition}\label{prop:blowup-representation}\index{Minimal singularity!exceptional poles}
Every $u\in\PSH(X,\theta)$ has, up to an additive constant, a unique $h$-plurisubharmonic target potential $q$ such that
\begin{equation}\label{eq:blowup-representation}
 u=cL+q\circ\pi+C,
 \qquad
 \theta+\ddc u=\pi^*(h+\ddc q)+c[E].
\end{equation}
The exact minimal ordinary type in this class is $cL+O(1)$, and $u$ has this type if and only if $q$ is bounded. There is a strict $\theta$-plurisubharmonic barrier with logarithmic coefficient $c+\delta$ for some $\delta>0$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Put $T=\theta+\ddc u$ and $q=\pi_*(u-cL)$ as a scalar distribution. The latter is locally integrable: off $Z$ it is the inverse-coordinate function, and a smooth target volume pulls back with bounded Jacobian, so its absolute integral is controlled by that of $u-cL$ on $X$. Since $\pi_*[E]=0$,
\[
 S=\pi_*T=h+\ddc q\geq0.
\]
A smooth local strictly plurisubharmonic shift gives a quasi-plurisubharmonic representative of $q$. Its pullback is genuine and positive by \cref{lem:smooth-disk-criterion}: the modification is dominant, so a local plurisubharmonic correction remains genuine after composition.

Off $E$, $T=\pi^*S$. The difference is an order-zero current supported on $E$, and it is closed: both derivatives equal $\pi^*\mathrm{d} h$. To identify it, write $R=T-\pi^*S$. In a chart where $E=\{z=0\}$, order zero gives $zR=\bar zR=0$. Closedness then gives $\mathrm{d}z\wedge R=\mathrm{d}\bar z\wedge R=0$. Thus only its normal $(1,1)$ coefficient remains, a measure on $E$; the tangential equations in $\mathrm{d}R=0$ make that measure a constant multiple of integration on $E$. Connectedness of $E$ identifies one constant $a$ globally, so $R=a[E]$. Its cohomology class is $c[E]$. On a line in an exceptional fiber, $\mathcal O(E)$ has degree $-1$, so $[E]$ is nonzero and $a=c$. The difference between the two scalar potentials in \eqref{eq:blowup-representation} has $\ddc$ zero. Local elliptic regularity makes it pluriharmonic; compactness and connectedness make it constant.

When $h>0$, $q=0$ is admissible and every $h$-plurisubharmonic $q$ is bounded above on compact $Y$. Hence $cL$ is minimal and two-sided bounded comparison with it is exactly boundedness of $q$. For the barrier choose the divisor metric so that $-\Theta_E$ is positive on the vertical tangent bundle of $E\to Z$, using the tautological $\mathcal O(-1)$. In adapted coordinates
\begin{equation}\label{eq:blowup-chart}
 \pi(y,z,w)=(y,z,zw),
\end{equation}
$\pi^*h$ controls the nonvertical directions and $|z|^2|\mathrm{d} w|^2$. For small $\delta>0$, the vertical positivity of $-\delta\Theta_E$ dominates its mixed errors by completing the square; away from $E$, compactness allows the same $\delta$. Thus $\pi^*h-\delta\Theta_E>0$, and the potential $(c+\delta)L$ has current $(c+\delta)[E]+\pi^*h-\delta\Theta_E$, dominating a positive Hermitian form. Changing the divisor metric changes this potential only by a smooth correction.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:minimal-blowup-data}\index{Blowup!density integrability}
Fix a smooth positive volume form $\Omega$ on $X$ and a nonzero nonnegative $f\in L^p(X,\Omega)$, $p>1$. There exist $\kappa>0$ and a normalized minimal-type solution of
\begin{equation}\label{eq:minimal-blowup-equation}
 \mathrm{MA}^{\mathrm{ext}}_\theta(u)=\kappa f\Omega,
 \qquad u=cL+q\circ\pi+C,
\end{equation}
where $q$ is H\"older on $Y$. The divisor coefficient is exactly $c$. In \eqref{eq:blowup-chart}, $u-cL=q(y,0)+C+O(|z|^\alpha)$ on smaller charts for some $\alpha>0$, so its limit is constant along each exceptional fiber.

If $\pi_*(f\Omega)=Fh^n$, then $F\in L^s(Y,h^n)$ for every
\begin{equation}\label{eq:pushforward-exponent}
 1<s<\frac{rp}{(r-1)p+1}.
\end{equation}
For a point center, data bounded above and below by positive constants near $E$ attain the strict threshold $s<n/(n-1)$, and for finite $p$ no strictly larger universal exponent than $np/((n-1)p+1)$ can be guaranteed. If $h$ is K\"ahler, the class $[\theta]$ is big and non-nef, has volume $\int_Yh^n$, and $\kappa=\int_Yh^n/\int_Xf\Omega$; the solution is unique among normalized minimal ordinary-type competitors for \eqref{eq:minimal-blowup-equation}. Under $\ddc(h^j)=0$, $1\leq j\leq n-1$, the scalar of the constructed solution has the same integral formula.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
In \eqref{eq:blowup-chart} the complex Jacobian is $z^{r-1}$. Put $J=\pi^*h^n/\Omega$, so $J\asymp|z|^{2(r-1)}$ on nested charts and $F\circ\pi=f/J$ off $E$. For $1<s<p$, change of variables and H\"older's inequality give
\begin{align}\label{eq:pushforward-holder}
 \int_YF^s h^n
 &=\int_X f^sJ^{1-s}\Omega\\
 &\leq\|f\|_{L^p(\Omega)}^s
 \left(\int_XJ^{-(s-1)p/(p-s)}\Omega\right)^{(p-s)/p}.
\end{align}
The normal complex integral is finite precisely if $(r-1)(s-1)p/(p-s)<1$, which is \eqref{eq:pushforward-exponent}. \Cref{thm:kn-continuous,thm:hermitian-holder-elementary} on $(Y,h)$ supply $q$ and $\kappa$ with $(h+\ddc q)^n=\kappa Fh^n$. Use \eqref{eq:blowup-representation} off $E$ and \cref{thm:arbitrary-extended-ma} across it. H\"older continuity gives $q(\pi(y,z,w))=q(y,0)+O(|z|^\alpha)$ uniformly on smaller charts.

For $f$ bounded above and below by positive constants near a point exceptional divisor, $F$ is comparable to $|y|^{-2(n-1)}$, giving the strict threshold. More generally, $f\asymp|s_E|^{-2a}$ with $0\leq a<1/p$ belongs to $L^p$ upstairs, while its pushforward has threshold $s<n/(n-1+a)$. Letting $a\uparrow1/p$ proves the stated limitation; it makes no finite-$p$ endpoint assertion.

When $h$ is K\"ahler, the positive form $\pi^*h-\delta\Theta_E$ makes $[\theta]$ big. Its degree on an exceptional-fiber line is $-c$, so it is not nef. The minimal current $c[E]+\pi^*h$ has non-pluripolar top mass $\int_Yh^n$, and the minimal mass theorem identifies this with $\operatorname{vol}([\theta])$ as recalled in \cref{sec:history-closed-loci}. Full-mass uniqueness and mass integration fix the scalar and normalized current as recalled in \cref{sec:history-regularity}. A possibly discontinuous exact-type competitor still has bounded $q$ by \cref{prop:blowup-representation}; on every relatively compact set off $E$, deep truncations equal that competitor, so locality in \cref{thm:arbitrary-extended-ma} gives the bounded target equation off $Z$. Both target measures give $Z$ zero mass, so closed uniqueness applies globally. If only the power conditions hold, \cref{thm:mass-invariance-characterization} fixes the scalar of the constructed solution. Quantitative H\"older bounds depend on $\kappa F$, not on an unnormalized $F$ alone.
\end{proof}

For the prescribed pullback measure $\pi^*(h^n)$, choose $q=0$ and $\kappa=1$. The current is $c[E]+\pi^*h$; its residual form has exactly the exceptional vertical directions as kernel on $E$, and its density has order $|z|^{2(r-1)}$ times a smooth positive factor. The regular metric is isometric to $(Y\setminus Z,h)$ and its completion restores $Z$, collapsing each exceptional fiber. The distance to $E$ is comparable to $|z|$ on bounded adapted charts. Neither the density order nor this distance exponent depends on $c$.

\begin{example}\label{ex:surface-blowup}
If $n=2$ and $Z$ is a point on a compact K\"ahler surface, $[\theta]=\pi^*[h]+c[E]$ has Zariski positive part $\pi^*[h]$ and negative part $cE$: $E^2=-1$ and $\pi^*[h]\cdot E=0$. Its volume is $\int_Yh^2$, although it is non-nef. The scalar-one solution for $\pi^*(h^2)$ has exact logarithmic coefficient $c$, a quadratic density zero upstairs, and a regular metric whose completion collapses $E$ to the target point. The stronger barrier from \cref{prop:blowup-representation} has coefficient $c+\delta$.
\end{example}

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:iterated-blowups}\index{Blowup!iterated equations}
Let $\pi:Y_m\to Y_0$ be a finite nonempty sequence of blowups of connected smooth centers of codimensions $r_j\geq2$ over a compact Hermitian $(Y_0,h_0)$. Let $E_j$ be the exceptional divisor at stage $j$, let $\pi_{>j}$ denote the subsequent map, choose $a_j>0$, and set
\[
 \Delta=\sum_j a_j\pi_{>j}^*E_j,
 \qquad \ddc L_\Delta=[\Delta]-\Theta_\Delta,
 \qquad \theta=\pi^*h_0+\Theta_\Delta.
\]
Every $\theta$-positive current is $\pi^*(h_0+\ddc q)+[\Delta]$ and every potential is $L_\Delta+q\circ\pi+C$, with $q\in\PSH(Y_0,h_0)$. Minimal ordinary type is exactly $L_\Delta+O(1)$, equivalent to bounded $q$. For potentials of this type, every prime exceptional coefficient is the one prescribed by the effective total transform $\Delta$. There is a strict barrier with divisor $\Delta+B$ for an effective exceptional $B$ positive on every exceptional component. For nonzero nonnegative upstairs $L^p$ data, $p>1$, a minimal solution has H\"older $q$, with pushed density in every $L^s$ satisfying
\begin{equation}\label{eq:iterated-exponent}
 1<s<\frac{Rp}{(R-1)p+1},\qquad R=\prod_jr_j.
\end{equation}
For K\"ahler $h_0$ the mass, scalar, and closed full-mass uniqueness are those of the target; the power conditions on $h_0$ fix the scalar for the constructed nonclosed solution. For the pullback measure $\pi^*(h_0^n)$, the residual metric completes to $Y_0$, identifying fibers, and the density-zero divisor is fixed by the squared holomorphic Jacobian independently of the $a_j$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Apply \cref{prop:blowup-representation} successively, allowing an intermediate smooth background to be indefinite. The difference between each current and the pullback of its pushforward is closed of order zero, and exceptional-fiber degree fixes its coefficient. Pullback of earlier divisor currents produces the total transforms, proving the representation without an SNC requirement. The initial $q=0$ is admissible; upper bounds on compact targets prove minimality and the bounded-$q$ criterion. At SNC charts this is the usual sum of logarithms.

At each stage the positive vertical curvature construction in \cref{prop:blowup-representation} gives $h_j=\pi_j^*h_{j-1}-\delta_j\Theta_{E_j}>0$ for a sufficiently small $\delta_j>0$. Expanding yields $h_m=\pi^*h_0-\Theta_B$ for $B=\sum_j\delta_j\pi_{>j}^*E_j$, effective with a positive coefficient on every exceptional component. Hence $h_m+[\Delta+B]$ is the strict barrier current. The Jacobian calculation and H\"older estimate apply stagewise. If $s=rp/((r-1)p+1)$, then $1-1/s=(1-1/p)/r$; iteration over the stages gives \eqref{eq:iterated-exponent}, with strictness retained. The target H\"older equation, mass and uniqueness are obtained as in \cref{thm:minimal-blowup-data}. For the pullback measure take $q=0$ and use the target path comparison away from the exceptional set. The Jacobian fixes the density, while $[\Delta]$ fixes the divisor current; these are independent pieces of the equation.
\end{proof}
