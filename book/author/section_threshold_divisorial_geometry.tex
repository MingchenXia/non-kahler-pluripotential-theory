\Needspace{12\baselineskip}
\begin{example}\label{ex:gauduchon-current-smoothing-obstruction}
The weak closure of smooth strictly positive normalized Gauduchon forms can be smaller than $\mathscr G_\omega$, even on a K\"ahler surface. Let $X$ be the blowup of $\mathbb P^2$ at one point and $C$ its exceptional curve. Fix a K\"ahler form $\omega$ and a smooth closed representative $\beta_C$ of $\{C\}$. Then
\[
 T_C=\frac{[C]}{\int_C\omega}\in\mathscr G_\omega,
 \qquad
 \int_X\beta_C\wedge T_C=\frac{-1}{\int_C\omega}<0.
\]
Every smooth strictly positive $T\in\mathscr G_\omega$ instead satisfies
$\int_X\beta_C\wedge T=\int_C T>0$.
\end{example}
\begin{proof}
The integration current $[C]$ is positive and closed, so its normalization belongs to $\mathscr G_\omega$. Its pairing with the smooth form $\beta_C$ is the self-intersection $C^2=-1$, computed in \cref{ex:gaud-metric-blowup}. For smooth $T$, the Poincar\'e--Lelong formula and $\ddc T=0$ give the second identity. The pairing with the fixed smooth $\beta_C$ is continuous under current convergence. It is therefore nonnegative on the weak closure of these smooth tests, which excludes $T_C$.
\end{proof}

Let $\mathscr C_\omega$ denote the weak closure of smooth normalized Gauduchon tests from \cref{sec:gauduchon-linear-duality}, and let $m_\omega$ be the minimum over all normalized currents from \cref{sec:smooth-cone-current-duality}. In particular $T_C\notin\mathscr C_\omega$. Since $[C]$ is a positive
representative of $\beta_C$, the two thresholds satisfy
\[
 m_\omega(\beta_C)\leq-\frac1{\int_C\omega}<0
 \leq\kappa_\omega(\beta_C).
\]
Thus extending the minimum in \eqref{eq:gauduchon-linear-minimum} to all
normalized Gauduchon currents changes its value in this example.

Return to the original $(X,\omega,f)$. The next two corollaries use uniform integrability along one smooth maximizing sequence. The root-tail condition $p\in\mathcal D_\omega^0$ is sufficient, since it gives $b>0$ and uniform integrability for the entire test family.

\Needspace{12\baselineskip}
\begin{corollary}\label{cor:semipositive-divisor-exclusion}
Assume $b>0$ and that $D_p$ has a smooth maximizing sequence $(T_j)$ with uniformly integrable densities $p k_{T_j}$. Let $E$ be an effective real divisor whose class has a smooth semipositive representative $\beta_E$. If
\[
 \int_{\{\beta_E=0\}}f\,\mathrm{d}V=0,
\]
no subsolution at the threshold $b_\omega(f)$ satisfies $\omega+\ddc u\geq[E]$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
If such a subsolution existed, \cref{prop:threshold-divisorial-constraint} would supply a maximizing current $T\in\mathscr G_\omega$ with $\int_X\beta_E\wedge T=0$. In the matrix-density notation of \cref{sec:weak-gauduchon-duality}, write $\sigma_T=\omega\wedge T$ for the trace measure, $P_T$ for the matrix density of $T$ and $B$ for the matrix of $\beta_E$. Positivity makes $\operatorname{tr}(BP_T)=0$ for $\sigma_T$-almost every point. Wherever $B\ne0$, this forces $P_T$ to be singular: a positive definite $P_T$ would have strictly positive trace against the nonzero positive semidefinite $B$. Thus $\Gamma_0(T)$ is supported on $\{\beta_E=0\}$. It equals $k_T\,\mathrm{d}V$ by \cref{thm:gaud-determinant-subcritical}. The hypothesis gives $p k_T=0$ almost everywhere, contradicting $D_p(T)=\rho_\omega(p)>0$.
\end{proof}
