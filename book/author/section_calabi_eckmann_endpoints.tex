\subsection{A Calabi--Eckmann family without LCK metrics}
\label{sec:calabi-eckmann-endpoints}
\index{Calabi--Eckmann manifold!determinant endpoint}

A Hermitian metric $\Omega$ is \emph{locally conformally K\"ahler}, or LCK, if $\mathrm d\Omega=\vartheta\wedge\Omega$ for a closed real one-form $\vartheta$, its Lee form; such metrics are studied in \cref{chap:lck}. The quotients of the preceding subsection resemble Hopf manifolds. A second family shows that the curvature criterion also applies on manifolds that carry no LCK metric at all.

Let $\tau=A+\mathrm iB_0$, where $A<0$ and $B_0>0$, and let $\C$ act holomorphically on $(\C^2\setminus\{0\})^2$ by
\[
 t\cdot(z,w)=(\mathrm e^t z,\mathrm e^{\tau t}w).
\]
Write $X_\tau$ for the quotient and put $r_1=\log|z|^2$, $r_2=\log|w|^2$. The map $(z,w)\mapsto([z],[w])$ descends to a projection $\pi\colon X_\tau\to\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1$; let $\omega_1,\omega_2$ be the pullbacks to the base of the Fubini--Study forms of the two factors, normalized to have integral one.

\begin{proposition}\label{prop:calabi-eckmann-endpoint}
The quotient $X_\tau$ is a compact complex threefold diffeomorphic to $S^3\times S^3$, and $\pi$ is a principal elliptic fibration over $\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1$. The form $\eta=\tau\partial r_1-\partial r_2$ descends to $X_\tau$, and for
\[
 g=\pi^*(\omega_1+\omega_2)+s\mathrm i\eta\wedge\bar\eta,
 \qquad s>0,
\]
one has
\begin{equation}\label{eq:calabi-eckmann-lower-mass}
 v_-(g)=48\pi^2sB_0.
\end{equation}
Every Hermitian form $h\geq c g$, with $c>0$, satisfies
\[
 \sup_{T\in\mathscr G_h}\|k_T\|_{L^{3/2}(\mathrm dV)}
 \leq c^{-1}(48\pi^2sB_0)^{-1/3}.
\]
There is no balanced, pluriclosed or LCK metric on $X_\tau$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Every orbit contains a unique point with $|z|=|w|=1$: the required parameter is determined by
\[
 \operatorname{Re}t=-r_1/2,\qquad
 \operatorname{Im}t=(r_2-Ar_1)/(2B_0).
\]
These smooth formulas identify $(\C^2\setminus\{0\})^2$ with $\C\times(S^3\times S^3)$, so the action is free and proper, and $X_\tau$ is diffeomorphic to $S^3\times S^3$. To obtain holomorphic charts, choose a nonzero coordinate $z_k$ of $z$ and a branch of its logarithm, and use the action to normalize $z_k=1$; the functions $z_l/z_k$, $l\ne k$, and $w_jz_k^{-\tau}$ are then local holomorphic coordinates on the quotient. Changing the branch of the logarithm accounts for the periods along the fibers. Thus $X_\tau$ is a compact complex threefold.

The invariant form $\eta$ vanishes on the generator $z\partial_z+\tau w\partial_w$ of the action, so it descends. On an elliptic fiber it equals $\mathrm d\chi$, where $\chi=\tau\log v_1-\log v_2$ in terms of fiber coordinates $(v_1z,v_2w)$; its period lattice is generated by $2\pi\mathrm i\tau$ and $-2\pi\mathrm i$. Hence $A_E=8\pi^2B_0$. Direct differentiation gives
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 F&=2\pi\mathrm i(\tau\omega_1-\omega_2),&
 \alpha&=-2\pi B_0\omega_1,\\
 \beta&=2\pi(A\omega_1-\omega_2),&
 Q&=-8\pi^2A\,\omega_1\wedge\omega_2>0.
 \end{aligned}
\]
Since $\int_B(\omega_1+\omega_2)^2=2$, \cref{thm:elliptic-curvature-endpoint} gives the lower mass, the endpoint bound and the absence of pluriclosed metrics.

Moreover $\mathrm d\operatorname{Re}\eta=-2\pi B_0\pi^*\omega_1$. This nonzero semipositive exact form excludes every balanced metric by the Stokes argument at the end of the proof of \cref{prop:elliptic-line-endpoint-example}, and hence also every K\"ahler metric. The quotient is simply connected, so the Lee form of a hypothetical LCK metric would be exact, equal to $\mathrm df$; multiplying the metric by $\mathrm e^{-f}$ would then give a K\"ahler metric, a contradiction. In particular $X_\tau$ carries no Vaisman metric, that is, no LCK metric with parallel Lee form.
\end{proof}

The curvature criterion controls bounded potentials and the full set of normalized Gauduchon currents in these threefolds. Curvature pairs of changing sign, primitive curvature pairs and higher-dimensional analogues are not covered by this argument.
