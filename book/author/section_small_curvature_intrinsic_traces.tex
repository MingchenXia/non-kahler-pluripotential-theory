\subsection{Logarithmic divisors with a small-curvature residual}
\label{subsec:small-curvature-intrinsic-traces}

An ambient residual can be invisible to every multiplier ideal even
when its restriction to $Y$ is identically $-\infty$. The restricted
ideals then recover the imposed logarithmic divisor directly.

\begin{proposition}\label{prop:small-curvature-intrinsic-traces}\index{Trace!small-curvature residual}\index{Multiplier ideal!normal crossing divisor}
Let $q\in\QPSH(X)$ have representatives of arbitrarily small negative
curvature in its ordinary type, as in \eqref{eq:small-curvature-type},
and let $\Delta=\sum_ja_jD_j$ be a finite effective real divisor with
logarithmic potential $\sigma_\Delta$, as defined before
\cref{thm:small-curvature-strict-type}. For every $t>0$,
\[
 \mathcal I\bigl(t(\sigma_\Delta+q)\bigr)
                  =\mathcal I(t\sigma_\Delta).
\]
If $Y$ is contained in none of the components $D_j$, then for every
divisor $E$ over $Y$, on a modification $\pi\colon Z\to Y$,
\[
 \tau_{Y,\sigma_\Delta+q}(E)
     =\sum_ja_j\operatorname{ord}_E\pi^*(D_j|_Y).
\]
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The first equality follows from
\cref{lem:small-curvature-zero-lelong,lem:zero-lelong-ideal-neutrality}.
Let $s_j$ be local equations of the $D_j$, so that
$\sigma_\Delta=\sum_ja_j\log|s_j|^2+O(1)$ locally. If
$f=g\prod_js_j^{\lceil ta_j\rceil}$, then
$|f|^2\mathrm e^{-t\sigma_\Delta}$ is locally bounded, so
$f\in\mathcal I(t\sigma_\Delta)$. Conversely, near a general point of
$D_j$, where $\sigma_\Delta=a_j\log|s_j|^2+O(1)$ with $s_j$ a
coordinate, \cref{lem:smooth-center-multiplier-ideal} in codimension one
shows that every germ of $\mathcal I(t\sigma_\Delta)$ vanishes to order
at least $\lfloor ta_j\rfloor$ along $D_j$. Such a germ is therefore
divisible by $\prod_js_j^{\lfloor ta_j\rfloor}$, and
\[
 \mathcal O_X\Bigl(-\sum_j\lceil ta_j\rceil D_j\Bigr)
 \subseteq\mathcal I(t\sigma_\Delta)
 \subseteq\mathcal O_X\Bigl(-\sum_j\lfloor ta_j\rfloor D_j\Bigr).
\]

Since $Y$ is contained in no $D_j$, each restricted equation $s_j|_Y$
is a nonzero holomorphic germ on $Y$. Its pullback to $Z$ is nonzero
and has a finite order $N_{j,E}=\operatorname{ord}_E\pi^*(D_j|_Y)$ along
$E$. Restricting the inclusions to $Y$ and pulling back to $Z$ give
\[
 \sum_j\lfloor ta_j\rfloor N_{j,E}
 \leq\operatorname{ord}_E\mathfrak a_t(\sigma_\Delta+q;Y)
 \leq\sum_j\lceil ta_j\rceil N_{j,E}.
\]
Moreover $\nu(\sigma_\Delta+q,Y)=0$, since at points of $Y$ outside the
finitely many divisors both summands have zero Lelong number. Thus these
orders are those of \cref{thm:intrinsic-trace-profile}, and dividing by
$t$ and letting $t\to\infty$ proves the trace formula.
\end{proof}
