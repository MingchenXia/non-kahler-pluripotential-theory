\begin{corollary}\label{cor:divisorial-current-ordinary-type}
Let $Z$ be a connected compact complex manifold, let $D_j$ be distinct prime divisors with integration currents $[D_j]$, and let $c_j>0$. Suppose $S=\sum_jc_j[D_j]$ is a closed positive current of finite mass. Use the ordinary and ideal envelopes of \cref{def:singularity-envelope,def:hermitian-i-envelope} and write $\nu(a,D_j)$ for generic Lelong numbers. For quasi-plurisubharmonic functions, $a\preceq b$ means $a\leq b+C$ for some constant $C$, $a\simeq b$ means both inequalities, and $a\preceq_{\mathcal I}b$ means inclusion of the multiplier ideals at every positive weight. It determines a canonical ordinary quasi-plurisubharmonic type with representatives $v$ satisfying
\[
 \ddc v=S-\beta
\]
for a smooth closed real form $\beta$. Any two such representatives differ smoothly. For every $a\in\QPSH(Z)$, this type is characterized by
\[
 a\preceq v\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 a\preceq_{\mathcal I}v\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \nu(a,D_j)\geq c_j\ \hbox{for all }j.
\]
For every Hermitian background $\Omega$ admitting the chosen representative,
\[
 P_\Omega[v]=P_\Omega[v]_{\mathcal I}\simeq v.
\]
In the trace setting $Y\subset X$, $u\in\PSH(X,\theta)$ and $\nu(u,Y)=0$, the Gauduchon bound in \cref{thm:trace-divisorial-current-budget} gives this canonical ordinary type for $S_Z(u;Y)$ on each fixed compact model $Z$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
\Cref{lem:bc-one-one-current-description} writes $S=\beta+\ddc v$ with $\beta$ smooth and closed and $v$ quasi-plurisubharmonic. If another pair is used, the difference of the potentials has smooth $\ddc$. Taking a smooth Hermitian trace and applying local elliptic regularity shows that the difference is smooth. Thus the ordinary type depends only on $S$.

The three-way characterization is \cref{lem:divisorial-potential-domination}. The ideal envelope preserves all multiplier ideals, so its generic Lelong number on each $D_j$ is still $c_j$. The same lemma gives $P_\Omega[v]_{\mathcal I}\preceq v$. Also $P_\Omega[v]_{\mathcal I}\geq v-\sup v$, so these functions are ordinarily equivalent. Consequently $P_\Omega[v]_{\mathcal I}$ is admissible in the ordinary envelope of $v$, giving $P_\Omega[v]_{\mathcal I}\leq P_\Omega[v]$. The reverse inequality is \cref{prop:p-below-i}. This proves the envelope identity and ordinary equivalence. The final assertion uses the positive closed current of finite mass already constructed from the Gauduchon estimate.
\end{proof}

This realization describes the divisorial part on one specified model. It does not identify the higher-codimensional singularities needed to recover every $\tau_{Y,u}(E)$ on further modifications. In the curve-fibration setting, \cref{thm:curve-fibration-exact-trace} gives that additional identification. For general trace data it remains a separate problem. The canonical ordinary type here is selected by the smooth residual term; other potentials with the same multiplier ideals need not belong to it.
