\Cref{lem:closed-current-global-potential} attaches a canonical ordinary singularity type to a countable divisorial current of finite mass, and \cref{lem:divisorial-potential-domination} describes, through generic Lelong numbers along the divisors, the potentials dominated by it.
\begin{corollary}\label{cor:divisorial-current-ordinary-type}\index{Divisorial current!ordinary type}
Let $Z$ be a connected compact complex manifold, let $(D_j)$ be a finite or countable family of distinct prime divisors on $Z$, and let $c_j>0$. Suppose that the current $S=\sum_jc_j[D_j]$ has finite mass. Then there are quasi-plurisubharmonic functions $v$ on $Z$ satisfying
\[
 \ddc v=S-\beta
\]
for a smooth closed real $(1,1)$-form $\beta$, and any two such functions differ by a smooth function. In particular, they determine a canonical ordinary singularity type, which depends only on $S$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
As a convergent sum of closed positive currents, $S$ is closed and positive. \Cref{lem:closed-current-global-potential}, applied to $T=S$, gives the decomposition, with $v$ quasi-plurisubharmonic since $S\geq0$, and shows that any two such potentials differ by a smooth function. By \cref{prop:singularity-elementary}, they have the same ordinary singularity type.
\end{proof}
