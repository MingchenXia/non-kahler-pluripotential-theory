\section{Local continuity in a semipositive big background}
\label{sec:local-big-continuity}

We return to a semipositive big background $\theta$. Where $\theta$ degenerates, regularization loses some positivity, and this curvature error can be repaired by adding a small multiple of a strict barrier. Bounded stability then shows that a bounded solution is continuous wherever the barrier is locally bounded.

Fix $\rho\in\PSH(X,\theta)$ with $\theta+\ddc\rho\geq\kappa\omega$ for some $\kappa>0$. For bounded $u\in\PSH(X,\theta)$ we use the averages $R_tu$, the transforms $\Phi_{c,\delta}$ and the curvature modulus $\epsilon(\delta)$, with the constants $A,K$, from \cref{subsec:general-curvature-modulus,lem:general-kiselman-modulus}.

\Needspace{12\baselineskip}
\begin{theorem}\label{thm:local-big-bounded-continuity}
Let $p>1$ and let $u\in\PSH(X,\theta)\cap L^\infty(X)$ satisfy
\[
 \mathrm{MA}_\theta(u)=f\,\mathrm{d}V,\qquad 0\leq f\in L^p(\mathrm{d}V).
\]
Then $u$ is continuous on every open set $U\subseteq X$ on which $\rho$ is locally bounded.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
After adding constants we may assume $-C_0\leq u\leq0$ and $\rho\leq-C_0-1$. Choose
\[
 0<\eta<\frac{p-1}{np+p-1},\qquad \alpha=2\eta<1,
\]
and set, for small $\delta>0$,
\[
 c=\delta^\alpha+\epsilon(\delta),\qquad
 e=Ac+\epsilon(\delta),\qquad s=e/\kappa,\qquad
 v_\delta=\frac{\Phi_{c,\delta}+s\rho}{1+s}.
\]
Since $s\kappa=e$, the curvature bounds for $\Phi_{c,\delta}$ and $\rho$ give $v_\delta\in\PSH(X,\theta)$. Moreover
\[
 \rho\leq-C_0-1\leq\Phi_{c,\delta}
                  \leq R_\delta u\leq0
\]
for small $\delta$: the lower bound follows from
$\Phi_{c,\delta}\geq u-K(\delta^2+\delta)$, exactly as in the proof of \cref{thm:hermitian-holder-elementary}. Hence $v_\delta\leq\Phi_{c,\delta}\leq R_\delta u\leq0$.

\Cref{thm:bounded-one-sided-stability} allows an unbounded comparison potential, so it applies to $v_\delta$. Together with the $L^1$ smoothing estimate, it gives
\[
 \sup_X(v_\delta-u)_+
 \leq C\left(\int_X(v_\delta-u)_+\,\mathrm{d}V\right)^\eta
 \leq C\delta^\alpha.
\]
Fix $M$ and consider the points where $\rho\geq-M$. Since $s\leq Cc$ and $c\to0$, the identity
\[
 \Phi_{c,\delta}-u=(1+s)(v_\delta-u)+s(u-\rho)
\]
gives $\Phi_{c,\delta}-u\leq C_Mc$ there.

At a radius $t$ where the infimum defining $\Phi_{c,\delta}$ is attained, the same lower estimate yields
\[
 -c\log(t/\delta)
       \leq\Phi_{c,\delta}-u+K(\delta^2+\delta)
       \leq C_Mc.
\]
Thus $t\geq a_M\delta$ with $a_M>0$ independent of the point and of $\delta$. Since $R_tu+Kt^2$ is increasing in $t$, we obtain
\[
 R_{a_M\delta}u+K(a_M\delta)^2
       \leq R_tu+Kt^2
       \leq\Phi_{c,\delta}+K\delta^2+K\delta.
\]
\Needspace{5\baselineskip}
This upper bound and the lower bound $R_su\geq u-Ks^2$ imply
\[
 \sup_{\{\rho\geq-M\}}
       |R_{a_M\delta}u-u|
       \leq C_M\bigl(\delta^\alpha+\epsilon(\delta)\bigr)
                       \longrightarrow0.
\]
On every compact subset of $U$, the function $\rho$ is bounded below. The smooth functions $R_{a_M\delta}u$ therefore converge uniformly to $u$ on each such compact set, which proves that $u$ is continuous on $U$.
\end{proof}

The proof also gives a modulus of continuity on each compact subset of $U$, obtained by combining the uniform smoothing error with the gradient bound $C/t$. A H\"older exponent would require a power bound for the modulus $\epsilon(\delta)$. For potentials that are locally bounded off the poles of a strict analytic barrier, \cref{lem:smooth-background-regularization-rate} refines the classical expansion and gives a curvature error linear in $\delta$. Combined with stability above a strict barrier, this yields local H\"older continuity even for solutions with minimal singularities (\cref{thm:big-minimal-holder}). Neither argument gives continuity across the pole set of the barrier.
