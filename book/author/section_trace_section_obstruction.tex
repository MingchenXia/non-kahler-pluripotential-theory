\section{Restricted sections and trace data}

Let $Y\subset X$ be a connected compact complex submanifold of dimension $r\geq1$, and suppose that the generic Lelong number $\nu(u,Y)=\inf_{y\in Y}\nu(u,y)$ vanishes. Recall from \cref{sec:restricted-ideals} the image ideals $\mathfrak a_m=\mathfrak a_m(u;Y)=\mathcal I(mu)\mathcal O_Y$ and the divisorial trace data $\tau_{Y,u}(E)$ of a prime divisor $E$ on a smooth compact modification of $Y$, defined in \eqref{eq:trace-profile} as the limit of $\operatorname{ord}_E\mathfrak a_m/m$. Put
\[
 V_m=\operatorname{im}\bigl(\mathcal H_m(u)\longrightarrow H^0(Y,L^m|_Y)\bigr).
\]
The local coefficients of a restricted section lie in $\mathfrak a_m$, so $V_m\subseteq H^0(Y,L^m|_Y\otimes\mathfrak a_m)$; the right-hand space can contain sections that do not extend to $X$.

On a modification of $Y$, the pullback of a nonzero restricted section vanishes along each prime divisor at least to the order imposed by the image ideal. Subtracting finitely many of these orders still leaves an effective divisor, which the Poincar\'e--Lelong formula turns into a positive current. If such sections exist in arbitrarily large degrees, normalized compactness produces a positive current in the class obtained by subtracting the trace data from the curvature class, and pairing it with a Gauduchon metric gives a degree inequality. Below, $c_1$ denotes the first Chern class in real Bott--Chern cohomology (\cref{sec:bc-closed-currents}); it is represented by the curvature form of any smooth Hermitian metric.

\Needspace{18\baselineskip}
\begin{theorem}\label{thm:gauduchon-trace-section-obstruction}\index{Trace!obstruction from sections}
Suppose that $V_m\neq0$ for arbitrarily large integers $m$. Let $\pi:Z\to Y$ be any smooth compact modification, let $E_1,\ldots,E_q$ be distinct prime divisors on $Z$, and let $\gamma$ be a Gauduchon metric on $Z$. Then
\[
 \deg_\gamma(\pi^*(L|_Y))
 \geq\sum_{i=1}^q\tau_{Y,u}(E_i)\int_{E_i}\gamma^{r-1}.
\]
Moreover, the real closed $(1,1)$ class
\[
 c_1(\pi^*(L|_Y))-
       \sum_{i=1}^q\tau_{Y,u}(E_i)c_1(\mathcal O_Z(E_i))
\]
is pseudoeffective: it contains a closed positive current.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose an unbounded set of degrees $m$ and nonzero sections $s_m\in V_m$, and put $b_i(m)=\operatorname{ord}_{E_i}\mathfrak a_m$. The pullback $\pi^*s_m$ is nonzero, and its local coefficients belong to the pulled-back ideal $\mathfrak a_m\mathcal O_Z$, so it vanishes to order at least $b_i(m)$ along $E_i$. Thus
\[
 D_m:=\operatorname{div}(\pi^*s_m)-\sum_{i=1}^q b_i(m)E_i
 \geq0.
\]
Choose smooth Hermitian metrics on the line bundles $\mathcal O_Z(E_i)$, with curvature forms $\beta_i$, and set
\[
 \Theta_m=\pi^*(\beta|_Y)-\sum_{i=1}^q\frac{b_i(m)}m\beta_i,
 \qquad
 \Theta=\pi^*(\beta|_Y)-\sum_{i=1}^q\tau_{Y,u}(E_i)\beta_i.
\]
Dividing $\pi^*s_m$ by the corresponding powers of the canonical sections of the bundles $\mathcal O_Z(E_i)$ gives a nonzero holomorphic section of $\pi^*(L^m|_Y)\otimes\mathcal O_Z(-\sum_i b_i(m)E_i)$ with divisor $D_m$. Equip this line bundle with the metric induced by $h$ and the chosen metrics. One $m$th of the logarithm of the squared norm of this section is a quasi-plurisubharmonic function $w_m$ on $Z$, and the Poincar\'e--Lelong formula gives
\[
 \Theta_m+\ddc w_m=\frac1m[D_m]\geq0.
\]
By \cref{thm:intrinsic-trace-profile}, $b_i(m)/m\to\tau_{Y,u}(E_i)$ as $m\to\infty$ through all integers, hence also along the chosen degrees, so $\Theta_m\to\Theta$ smoothly. Consequently a single Hermitian form $\Lambda$ on $Z$ dominates all the forms $\Theta_m$, and every $w_m$ belongs to $\PSH(Z,\Lambda)$. Normalize $\sup_Zw_m=0$. By \cref{thm:normalized-compactness}, a subsequence converges in $L^1$, and its limit $w$ satisfies
\[
 \Theta+\ddc w\geq0.
\]
Since $\Theta$ is closed and represents the class in the statement, this positive current proves the asserted pseudoeffectivity.

Finally, pair this current with $\gamma^{r-1}$, where $\gamma^{r-1}=1$ if $r=1$. Integration by parts removes $\ddc w$ because $\gamma$ is Gauduchon, and the Poincar\'e--Lelong formula for the canonical section of $\mathcal O_Z(E_i)$ gives $\int_Z\beta_i\wedge\gamma^{r-1}=\int_{E_i}\gamma^{r-1}$. The nonnegativity of the pairing is exactly the stated degree inequality.
\end{proof}

Equivalently, if one of these inequalities fails for some choice of $Z$, $E_1,\ldots,E_q$ and $\gamma$, then $V_m=0$ for all sufficiently large $m$.

The converse of the theorem fails in general. Let $E=\C/(\mathbb Z+\mathrm{i}\mathbb Z)$ be the square elliptic curve, choose an irrational real number $a$, and let $L$ be the flat line bundle on $E$ with multipliers $\mathrm{e}^{2\pi\mathrm{i} a}$ and $1$ along the two generators: its sections lift to holomorphic functions $f$ on $\C$ with $f(z+1)=\mathrm{e}^{2\pi\mathrm{i} a}f(z)$ and $f(z+\mathrm{i})=f(z)$. The constant metric on $L$ has curvature zero. Take $u=0$ and $Y=X=E$. Then every trace number is zero, and the class in the theorem is zero, hence pseudoeffective. Nevertheless no positive tensor power of $L$ has a nonzero holomorphic section. Indeed, a nonzero section of $L^m$ would have a zero divisor of degree zero and would therefore be nowhere zero. The logarithm of its norm would be harmonic on $E$, hence constant, so its lift $f$ would be a holomorphic function of constant modulus on $\C$, and thus constant. Since $ma\notin\mathbb Z$, the relation $f(z+1)=\mathrm{e}^{2\pi\mathrm{i} ma}f(z)$ then forces $f=0$, a contradiction.
