The regularized envelope does not change if the obstacle is imposed only outside a pluripolar subset of $K$.

\begin{corollary}\label{cor:affine-polar-obstacle-invariance}
The envelope $F_\delta$ is unchanged if $u|_K\leq\psi$ is imposed only quasi-everywhere, or only on $K\setminus E$ for any pluripolar $E\subseteq K$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
By \cref{lem:affine-projective-growth}, the map $u\mapsto u-\delta\chi/2$ is a bijection from the competitors for each of these weakened constraints onto the $\theta_\delta$-plurisubharmonic functions satisfying the same constraint with the obstacle $\psi-\delta\chi/2$. Apply \cref{prop:compact-obstacle-polar-invariance} on $\mathbb P^n$ to the K\"ahler, hence big, background $\theta_\delta$ and this bounded obstacle on $K$, and add $\delta\chi/2$ as in the proof of \cref{prop:affine-projective-envelope}.
\end{proof}

These equalities concern regularized envelopes. The raw envelope $V_\delta$ is still defined with the obstacle imposed at every point of $K$.

We also use functions of logarithmic growth with poles on a given bounded pluripolar set.

\begin{lemma}\label{lem:affine-logarithmic-pole-barrier}
For every bounded pluripolar set $E\subset\mathbb C^n$, there is $b\in\mathcal L_1$ with $b=-\infty$ on $E$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The set $E$ is pluripolar as a subset of $\mathbb P^n$: it is locally pluripolar near every affine point, and a neighborhood of the hyperplane at infinity does not meet it. By Josefson's theorem on compact manifolds, \cite[Theorem~1.1.5]{SGPT}, there is a quasi-plurisubharmonic function on $\mathbb P^n$, not identically $-\infty$, that equals $-\infty$ on $E$. A small positive multiple $p$ of it belongs to $\PSH(\mathbb P^n,\theta_1)$, and $b=p+\chi/2$ belongs to $\mathcal L_1$ by \cref{lem:affine-projective-growth}.
\end{proof}
