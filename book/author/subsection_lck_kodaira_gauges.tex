\subsection{A flat conformal gauge that is not Gauduchon}
\label{subsec:lck-kodaira-gauges}

The two preferred conformal representatives need not coincide. The following example keeps the complex surface and the conformal class fixed; the obstruction is to imposing both conditions in that class.

\begin{example}\label{ex:lck-kodaira-incompatible-gauges}\index{Chern--Ricci curvature!flat conformal gauge}\index{Vaisman metric!Kodaira surface}\index{Gauduchon metric!incompatible conformal gauges}
There is a compact complex Kodaira surface with holomorphically trivial canonical bundle and a strictly LCK Chern--Ricci-flat metric $\omega$ that is not Gauduchon. No metric conformal to $\omega$ is both Gauduchon and Chern--Ricci flat, although the same conformal class contains a Vaisman metric.
\end{example}
\begin{proof}
We construct the quotient and the two conformal metrics explicitly. On $G=\R^4$ use the multiplication
\[
 (x,y,z,t)(x',y',z',t')
   =(x+x',y+y',z+z'+xy',t+t').
\]
The integer subgroup $\Gamma=\mathbb Z^4$ acts on the left by
\[
 (x,y,z,t)\longmapsto(x+a,y+b,z+c+ay,t+d).
\]
Reducing $x,y,t$ and then $z$ to unit intervals gives a compact fundamental set, so $X=\Gamma\backslash G$ is connected and compact. The forms
\[
 e^1=\mathrm dx,\quad e^2=\mathrm dy,\quad
 e^3=\mathrm dz-x\,\mathrm dy,\quad e^4=\mathrm dt
\]
descend to $X$, with $\mathrm de^3=-e^1\wedge e^2$ and the other derivatives zero. Declare $\phi^1=e^1+\mathrm i e^2$ and $\phi^2=e^3+\mathrm i e^4$ to be of type $(1,0)$. Since
\[
 \mathrm d\phi^1=0,\qquad
 \mathrm d\phi^2=-\tfrac{\mathrm i}{2}\phi^1\wedge\overline{\phi^1},
\]
there is no $(0,2)$ component and the complex structure is integrable. The form $\Sigma=\phi^1\wedge\phi^2$ is closed and nowhere vanishing, hence is a holomorphic trivialization of the canonical bundle. In particular $c_1^{\mathrm{BC}}(X)=0$. Projection to $x+\mathrm i y$ exhibits this quotient as the Kodaira elliptic bundle over $\mathbb C/(\mathbb Z+\mathrm i\mathbb Z)$.

Choose a smooth unit-periodic function $f(x)$ with $C=1-f''>0$, and put
\[
 \lambda_1=-e^4+f'e^1,\qquad
 \eta_1=J\lambda_1=e^3+f'e^2,\qquad
 \omega_1=\lambda_1\wedge\eta_1+C e^1\wedge e^2.
\]
This is positive and Hermitian, with Riemannian metric
\[
 g_1=\lambda_1\otimes\lambda_1+\eta_1\otimes\eta_1
              +C(e^1\otimes e^1+e^2\otimes e^2).
\]
The identities $\mathrm d\lambda_1=0$ and $\mathrm d\eta_1=-C e^1\wedge e^2$ give $\mathrm d\omega_1=\lambda_1\wedge\omega_1$. The Lee period along the $t$-circle is $-1$, so this metric is strictly LCK. Its Lee dual is $-\partial_t$, which is Killing because all coefficients are independent of $t$. Closedness of the Lee form and the Killing equation make $\nabla^{g_1}\lambda_1$ both symmetric and skew-symmetric; thus $\omega_1$ is Vaisman.

Write $e^{1234}=e^1\wedge e^2\wedge e^3\wedge e^4$. The volume is $\omega_1^2/2=C e^{1234}$, whereas $\Sigma\wedge\overline\Sigma$ has constant density in this coframe. For $h=C^{-1/2}$, the conformal metric $\omega=h\omega_1$ has volume $e^{1234}$ and therefore is Chern--Ricci flat. Its Lee form is
\[
 \lambda=-e^4+\mathrm d(f+\log h).
\]
Let $E_i$ be dual to $e^i$. Solving $g(A,\cdot)=\lambda$ gives
\[
 A=h'E_1+(f'h'-h^{-1})E_4.
\]
The metric volume is $\mathrm dx\,\mathrm dy\,\mathrm dz\,\mathrm dt$, and the fields $E_i$ have zero divergence for this volume. Hence $\operatorname{div}A=h''$ and $q=-h''$.

For the choice
\[
 f(x)=\frac{\cos(2\pi x)}{16\pi^2},\qquad
 C(x)=1+\tfrac14\cos(2\pi x)\geq\tfrac34,
\]
one has
\[
 q(0)=-\frac{4\sqrt5\pi^2}{25},\qquad
 q(1/2)=\frac{4\sqrt3\pi^2}{9}.
\]
Thus $\omega$ is not Gauduchon, and \cref{thm:lck-chern-ricci-rigidity} also excludes the Vaisman condition for this flat metric.

Finally, if $\mathrm e^v\omega$ is also Chern--Ricci flat, then the conformal Ricci formula gives $\ddc v=0$. The real pluriharmonic function $v$ is constant on the connected compact complex manifold by the maximum principle. A constant multiple of $\omega$ still has nonzero divergence of its Lee field, so no flat metric in this conformal class is Gauduchon. The metric $\omega_1$ already constructed is a Vaisman representative of the class.
\end{proof}
