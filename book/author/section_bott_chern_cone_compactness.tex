\begin{proposition}\label{prop:psef-cone-compact-base}
The pseudoeffective cone in $H^{1,1}_{\mathrm{BC}}(X,\mathbb R)$ is closed and convex and contains no nonzero line. For a fixed Gauduchon metric $\gamma$, the trace functional $\ell_\gamma$ of \cref{sec:bc-closed-currents} is strictly positive on its nonzero elements, and the slice of pseudoeffective classes with $\ell_\gamma=1$ is compact. This slice may be empty. Pullback and pushforward by a compact modification are continuous linear maps on Bott--Chern cohomology and preserve pseudoeffectivity.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The finite-dimensional space of smooth representatives in \cref{lem:constant-trace-bc-representative} identifies Bott--Chern cohomology with a finite-dimensional vector space and gives it its usual topology; the trace functional is continuous for this topology. Its positivity and the absence of lines in the cone were proved in \cref{sec:bc-closed-currents}, and convexity follows by adding positive currents.

Let pseudoeffective classes $\alpha_j$ converge to $\alpha$, and choose positive closed representatives $T_j$. Their masses $\ell_\gamma(\alpha_j)$ are bounded, so by weak compactness a subsequence converges to a positive closed current $T$. Let $B_\gamma$ be the continuous smooth representative map of \cref{lem:constant-trace-bc-representative}. Along the subsequence, $B_\gamma(T_j)$ converges both to $B_\gamma(T)$ and to the representative of $\alpha$, so $T$ represents $\alpha$. Thus the cone is closed. On its mass-one slice, \eqref{eq:constant-trace-representative-bound} bounds $B_\gamma(T)$ in the finite-dimensional representative space. The slice is therefore closed and bounded there, hence compact.

The class maps of a modification are linear, and they are well-defined because classes can be represented equally by smooth forms and by currents. As linear maps between finite-dimensional spaces, they are continuous. Pullback preserves positive closed currents, since it can be computed on local plurisubharmonic potentials as in \cref{prop:closed-current-modification-continuity}, and proper pushforward preserves positivity and closedness. Applying these maps to positive representatives proves the assertions about the cones.
\end{proof}

This compactness is linear. The functional $\ell_\gamma$ pairs a closed current with a fixed smooth form; it is not a top-degree volume of the class, and it gives no bound for the Monge--Amp\`ere masses of potentials.
