\subsection{Twisting and distance bounds}
\label{sec:twisted-flow-distance-dependence}

The maximal time and the initial Monge--Amp\`ere density do not alone give a uniform diameter estimate across different twisted flows. The twist and the time interval enter the geometry even for flat K\"ahler metrics.

\Needspace{14\baselineskip}
\begin{example}\label{ex:twisted-flow-distance-dependence}\index{Chern--Ricci flow!dependence on the twist}
Let $E=\C/(\mathbb Z+\mathrm i\mathbb Z)$, let $X=E^n$ with $n\geq2$, and let $\eta_j=(\mathrm i/2)\mathrm dz_j\wedge\mathrm d\bar z_j$ be the descended flat forms. Put $\omega_0=\sum_{j=1}^n\eta_j$. All the flows below start at $\omega_0$, with potential zero and initial density one relative to $\omega_0^n$. In particular that density has the representation $\mathrm e^{\psi^+-\psi^-}$ with $\psi^+=\psi^-=0$ and fixed quasi-plurisubharmonicity and $L^p$ bounds.

For each $M>0$, the twist $\zeta_M=M\eta_1-\eta_2$ gives the explicit solution
\[
 \omega_M(t)=(1+Mt)\eta_1+(1-t)\eta_2+
                         \sum_{j=3}^n\eta_j,
 \qquad 0\leq t<1,
\]
of $\partial_t\omega=-\operatorname{Ric}(\omega)+\zeta_M$. Its maximal smooth existence time is exactly one, while
\[
 \operatorname{diam}(X,\omega_M(1/2))
 \geq \sqrt{1+M/2}\,\operatorname{diam}(E,\eta_1).
\]
For the single fixed twist $\zeta=\omega_0$, the solution is $(1+t)\omega_0$ for all $t\geq0$, and its diameter grows as $\sqrt{1+t}\,\operatorname{diam}(X,\omega_0)$.
\end{example}
\begin{proof}
Each displayed metric has spatially constant coefficients, so its Chern--Ricci form vanishes. Differentiation proves the flow equations and positivity gives the stated existence intervals. In the first family, integrate any proposed positive representative at a later time against the closed positive test form $\eta_1\wedge\eta_3\wedge\cdots\wedge\eta_n$. The potential term integrates to zero, and the pairing is a positive fixed multiple of $1-t$. It cannot be strictly positive when $t\geq1$. Every smooth solution has the affine background modulo a $\ddc$-potential by integrating its equation, so no smooth positive continuation past one is possible. The empty product convention covers $n=2$.

Projection to the first elliptic factor decreases lengths after its metric is scaled by $1+Mt$. Taking points differing only in that factor proves the lower diameter bound, and the product path realizes their scaled factor distance. For the second flow every path length is multiplied by $\sqrt{1+t}$, proving the exact diameter formula.
\end{proof}

On a fixed finite interval, a common Dini modulus for the potentials and a uniform bound for the affine backgrounds do give distance and diameter control by \cref{cor:dini-family-distance-limits}. The example explains why those bounds must be specified when varying the twist or extending the time interval. It does not concern a terminal metric limit of an untwisted flow.
