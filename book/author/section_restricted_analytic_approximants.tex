\subsection{Restriction of analytic approximants}

An arbitrary quasi-plurisubharmonic function with zero generic Lelong number along a submanifold may still restrict identically to $-\infty$. Analytic regularization removes this obstruction: the approximating ideals have genuine restrictions, and a fixed lower curvature bound controls their divisorial orders on every fixed modification.

\begin{corollary}\label{cor:restricted-analytic-divisorial-bound}
Fix a smooth real $(1,1)$-form $\theta$, a Hermitian form $\omega$, and $u\in\PSH(X,\theta)$. Let $u_m$ with curvature errors $\epsilon_m$ be the approximants of \cref{cor:nonclosed-analytic-approximation}. Let $Y\subset X$ be a connected compact complex submanifold and suppose
\[
 \nu(u,Y):=\inf_{y\in Y}\nu(u,y)=0.
\]
Each $u_m|_Y$ is genuine. If $\pi:Z\to Y$ is a smooth compact modification and $E\subset Z$ is a prime divisor, then
\[
 \sup_m\nu\bigl((u_m|_Y)\circ\pi,E\bigr)<\infty.
\]
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
For each $m$, choose $y\in Y$ with $\nu(u,y)<1/m$. The local Skoda integrability criterion gives $\mathcal I(mu)_y=\mathcal O_{X,y}$. Thus the image ideal $\mathcal I(mu)\mathcal O_Y$ is not identically zero. Analytic restriction in \cref{prop:analytic-type-calculus} makes $u_m|_Y$ genuine. Its pullback to $Z$ is also genuine.

\Needspace{6\baselineskip}
The restricted and pulled-back positivity inequality gives
\[
 \pi^*(\theta|_Y)+\ddc\bigl((u_m|_Y)\circ\pi\bigr)
 \geq-\epsilon_m\pi^*(\omega|_Y).
\]
A single smooth Hermitian form on $Z$, multiplied by a large enough constant, dominates these lower bounds for all indices. \Cref{lem:gauduchon-uniform-lelong} therefore applies on $Z$ and bounds the generic Lelong number along $E$.
\end{proof}
