\subsection{Trace inequalities at the subsolution threshold}
\label{subsec:threshold-trace-inequalities}

A smooth Gauduchon test pairs with the full current $\omega+\ddc u$. For a threshold subsolution, \cref{lem:unbounded-gauduchon-trace-density} bounds that trace below by the determinant-weighted density. The nonnegative difference has a mass depending only on the test. Along a maximizing sequence this mass tends to zero, uniformly over all threshold subsolutions. Write $\|\cdot\|_{\mathrm{TV}}$ for total variation of a finite signed measure.

\Needspace{14\baselineskip}
\begin{proposition}\label{prop:threshold-trace-saturation}\index{Gauduchon duality!trace saturation}
Let $0\leq f\in L^1$ be nonzero and $b=b_\omega(f)>0$, and write $p=f^{1/n}$. Suppose $u\in\PSH(X,\omega)$ satisfies $\mathrm{MA}^{\mathrm{ext}}_\omega(u)\geq bf\,\mathrm{d}V$. For every smooth strictly positive $T\in\mathscr G_\omega$, the measure
\[
 R_{u,T}=(\omega+\ddc u)\wedge T-b^{1/n}p k_T\,\mathrm{d}V
\]
is positive and has mass
\[
 R_{u,T}(X)=1-b^{1/n}D_p(T).
\]
In particular, if $(T_j)$ is a smooth maximizing sequence for $D_p$, then $\|R_{u,T_j}\|_{\mathrm{TV}}\to0$, uniformly over all such threshold subsolutions $u$.

If also $p\in\mathcal D_\omega^0$ and $T_j\to T$ as currents, then $T$ maximizes $D_p$ and
\[
 (\omega+\ddc u)\wedge T_j
       \rightharpoonup b^{1/n}p k_T\,\mathrm{d}V.
\]
For a fixed current limit $T$, this limiting probability is independent of the threshold subsolution $u$ along the same sequence.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
A smooth strictly positive $T$ is $\gamma^{n-1}$ for a Gauduchon metric $\gamma$. The normalization is $\int_X\omega\wedge T=1$. \Cref{lem:unbounded-gauduchon-trace-density} proves positivity of $R_{u,T}$; distributional Stokes gives $((\omega+\ddc u)\wedge T)(X)=1$. This proves the mass formula. Since $\rho_\omega(p)=b^{-1/n}$, a maximizing sequence makes this nonnegative mass tend to zero. It equals the total variation norm and is independent of $u$.

Under the tail hypothesis, \cref{thm:integrable-current-attainment} gives uniform integrability of $p k_{T_j}$. Thus \cref{prop:selected-gauduchon-attainment} makes $T$ a maximizer and gives
$p k_{T_j}\,\mathrm{d}V\rightharpoonup p k_T\,\mathrm{d}V$. Adding the error measures, which tend to zero in total variation, proves the limit.
\end{proof}

The limiting formula is stated along smooth maximizing sequences. It does not define the product $(\omega+\ddc u)\wedge T$ for an arbitrary singular current $T$, or assert independence of the chosen maximizing current.

Smooth strict positivity of a maximizing test has a stronger consequence: in dimension at least two, it forces an exact smooth equation. The local regularity is supplied by \cref{lem:smooth-conformal-current}.
