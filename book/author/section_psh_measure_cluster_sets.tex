\subsection{Full cluster sets}
\label{subsec:zero-local-cluster-sets}\label{subsec:bounded-background-cluster-sets}

\Cref{thm:local-prescribed-excess} prescribes one limit of the Monge--Amp\`ere measures, but a single sequence of potentials can have many subsequential limits. On a bounded domain in dimension at least two, the sets of all subsequential limits for potentials that converge from below in local capacity to a locally bounded potential $b$ are exactly the vaguely closed sets of measures dominating $(\ddc b)^n$ (\cref{thm:bounded-background-cluster-sets}); for $b=0$, every vaguely closed set occurs.
