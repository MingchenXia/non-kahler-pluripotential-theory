\section{Divisorial components at the threshold}\label{sec:threshold-divisorial-components}

A divisorial component of a Hermitian current consumes part of its Gauduchon trace but contributes no density to its non-pluripolar measure. It can therefore lower the largest coefficient in a Monge--Amp\`ere subsolution inequality. At the maximal coefficient, every weak limit of a maximizing sequence of Gauduchon tests annihilates the class of the divisor, and uniform integrability along the sequence makes the limit a maximizer.

In this section $X$ is again an arbitrary compact complex manifold, now of dimension $n\geq2$, with the Hermitian form $\omega$. Fix a smooth strictly positive probability volume form $\mathrm{d}V$ and a nonzero function $0\leq f\in L^1(\mathrm{d}V)$, and write $p=f^{1/n}$ for its root density. Let $b=b_\omega(f)$ be its subsolution threshold: by \cref{thm:individual-density-subsolution}, it is the largest $a\geq0$ for which some $u\in\PSH(X,\omega)$ satisfies $\mathrm{MA}^{\mathrm{ext}}_\omega(u)\geq af\,\mathrm{d}V$, where $\mathrm{MA}^{\mathrm{ext}}_\omega$ is the extended non-pluripolar Monge--Amp\`ere measure of \cref{thm:arbitrary-extended-ma}. Such a $u$ with $a=b$ is a threshold subsolution in the sense of \cref{sec:individual-density-subsolutions}. We also use the following notation from \cref{sec:gauduchon-linear-duality,sec:integrable-gauduchon-currents}. The set $\mathscr G_\omega$ consists of the positive $(n-1,n-1)$-currents $T$ with $\ddc T=0$ and $\int_X\omega\wedge T=1$, and $\mathscr C_\omega\subset\mathscr G_\omega$ is the weak closure of its smooth strictly positive members, the normalized powers $\gamma^{n-1}$ of Gauduchon metrics $\gamma$. For $T\in\mathscr G_\omega$, the determinant measure of $T$ has the form $\Gamma_0(T)=k_T\,\mathrm{d}V$ by \cref{thm:gaud-determinant-subcritical}, and $D_p(T)=\int_Xp k_T\,\mathrm{d}V$. The root norm $\rho_\omega(p)=b^{-1/n}$ is the supremum of $D_p$ over $\mathscr G_\omega$, and $\mathcal D_\omega^0$ is the closure of the bounded functions in the space of roots with finite root norm. Finally, $\kappa_\omega(\beta)$ is the linear positivity threshold of a smooth real $(1,1)$-form $\beta$ from \cref{cor:gauduchon-threshold}, the infimum over Gauduchon metrics $\gamma$ of $\int_X\beta\wedge\gamma^{n-1}/\int_X\omega\wedge\gamma^{n-1}$.

\begin{proposition}\label{prop:threshold-divisorial-constraint}\index{Gauduchon test!divisorial constraints}
Let $c>0$ be real and let $E$ be an effective real divisor on $X$. Suppose $u\in\PSH(X,\omega)$ satisfies
\[
 \mathrm{MA}^{\mathrm{ext}}_\omega(u)\geq c f\,\mathrm{d}V,
 \qquad \omega+\ddc u\geq[E].
\]
Let $\beta_E$ be a smooth closed representative of the Bott--Chern class of $[E]$, and put $\kappa=\kappa_\omega(\beta_E)$. Then $b>0$, $0\leq\kappa<1$ and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:divisorial-coefficient-loss}
 c\leq b(1-\kappa)^n.
\end{equation}
\Needspace{8\baselineskip}
Suppose that $c=b$, and let $(T_j)$ be a \emph{smooth maximizing sequence} for $D_p$, that is, a sequence of smooth strictly positive forms $T_j\in\mathscr G_\omega$ with $D_p(T_j)\to\rho_\omega(p)$. Then
\[
 \int_X\beta_E\wedge T_j=\int_X[E]\wedge T_j\longrightarrow0.
\]
Every weak cluster current $T$ therefore satisfies $\int_X\beta_E\wedge T=0$, and the class of $E$ is not big. If the densities $p k_{T_j}$ of the chosen sequence are uniformly integrable, every such cluster current maximizes $D_p$. In particular, this holds for every smooth maximizing sequence when $p\in\mathcal D_\omega^0$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
By \cref{thm:individual-density-subsolution}, $b\geq c>0$. Let $T\in\mathscr G_\omega$ be smooth and strictly positive. By \cref{lem:unbounded-gauduchon-trace-density}, the measure
\[
 R=(\omega+\ddc u)\wedge T-c^{1/n}p k_T\,\mathrm{d}V
\]
is positive, and its mass is $1-c^{1/n}D_p(T)$. The absolutely continuous term gives no mass to the support of $E$, whereas $[E]\wedge T$ is concentrated there. Restricting $R$ to the support of $E$ and using $\omega+\ddc u\geq[E]$ therefore gives
\[
 \int_X[E]\wedge T\leq R(X)=1-c^{1/n}D_p(T).
\]
Let $\ell_E$ be a global logarithmic potential, so that $[E]=\beta_E+\ddc\ell_E$ by the Poincar\'e--Lelong formula. Since $\ell_E$ is locally integrable and $T$ is smooth with $\ddc T=0$, Stokes' theorem for currents identifies the left side with $\int_X\beta_E\wedge T$. This integral is nonnegative for every such $T$, so $\kappa\geq0$; since $D_p(T)>0$, we also get $\kappa<1$. Taking the supremum of $D_p(T)$, which is $\rho_\omega(p)=b^{-1/n}$, proves \eqref{eq:divisorial-coefficient-loss}.

Now let $c=b$, and let $(T_j)$ be a smooth maximizing sequence. Then the right side of the preceding bound tends to zero. Since pairing with the fixed smooth form $\beta_E$ is continuous under weak convergence of currents, every cluster current satisfies $\int_X\beta_E\wedge T=0$. Moreover, \eqref{eq:divisorial-coefficient-loss} with $c=b$ forces $\kappa=0$, so the class of $E$ is not big by \cref{cor:gauduchon-threshold}. If the densities along the sequence are uniformly integrable, \cref{prop:selected-gauduchon-attainment} shows that every cluster current maximizes $D_p$. When $p\in\mathcal D_\omega^0$, \cref{thm:integrable-current-attainment} gives this uniform integrability for every sequence.
\end{proof}

Geometrically, at the threshold $c=b$ with $E\ne0$, the equation $\int_X\beta_E\wedge T=0$ defines a nonempty proper exposed face of $\mathscr C_\omega$. Indeed, $\mathscr C_\omega$ is compact and convex, and the pairing with $\beta_E$ is continuous and nonnegative on it and strictly positive on every smooth strictly positive member. The proposition places every cluster current of a smooth maximizing sequence on this face. The statement concerns $\mathscr C_\omega$, which can be strictly smaller than $\mathscr G_\omega$, as the next example shows. Moreover, for a singular $T$ the smooth pairing $\int_X\beta_E\wedge T$ does not define a product $[E]\wedge T$.
