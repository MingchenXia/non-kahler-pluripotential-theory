\section{H\"older potentials and metric distance}
\label{sec:psh-holder-geometry}

A H\"older modulus controls the trace mass of a positive Hessian on small balls. Averaging path lengths against that trace then gives an upper bound for the distance of the regular metric. This converts regularity of the potential into a metric estimate without requiring a pointwise Hessian bound.

Let $U\subset\C^n$ be a coordinate domain, $\omega_{\mathrm E}=\ddc|z|^2$ its Euclidean form, and $\mathrm{d}\lambda$ Euclidean volume. Let $0<\alpha\leq1$, $v\in C^{0,\alpha}(U)$, $\theta$ be a smooth real $(1,1)$-form on $U$, and $T=\theta+\ddc v\geq0$. Write $\mu=T\wedge\omega_{\mathrm E}^{n-1}$.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:holder-current-distance}\index{Holder potential@H\"older potential!trace mass}\index{Holder potential@H\"older potential!metric distance}
On nested relatively compact charts, for all sufficiently small Euclidean balls,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:trace-ball}
 \mu(B(x,r))\leq C r^{2n-2+\alpha}.
\end{equation}
Every coefficient measure of $T$ has total variation dominated by a multiple of $\mu$. Hence coefficients vanish on sets of zero Hausdorff $(2n-2+\alpha)$-measure, and the local quasi-plurisubharmonic Lelong numbers vanish. This estimate does not imply absolute continuity or a pointwise Hessian bound.

Suppose in addition that $T$ is a smooth strictly positive Hermitian metric $\eta$ on $U\setminus A$ for a proper analytic subset $A$. Let $d_\eta^U$ be the infimum of $\eta$-lengths of piecewise smooth paths in $U\setminus A$ joining the given points. On sufficiently small nested charts $V\Subset U$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:holder-distance}
 d_\eta^U(x,y)\leq C|x-y|^{\alpha/2},\qquad x,y\in V\setminus A.
\end{equation}
Each point of $A\cap V$ has a finite completion limit. If these local hypotheses hold in charts covering $X$, with $A\subset X$ proper analytic and $\eta$ a smooth Hermitian metric on $X\setminus A$, the completion of $X\setminus A$ for its intrinsic $\eta$-distance is a compact continuous metric quotient of $X$. The metric is incomplete when $A\ne\varnothing$. Both exponents in \eqref{eq:trace-ball} and \eqref{eq:holder-distance} are sharp.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Choose a nonnegative cutoff $\chi$ equal to one on $B(x,r)$ and supported on $B(x,2r)$ with $|\mathrm{d}^2\chi|\leq Cr^{-2}$. Positivity and integration by parts, subtracting the constant $v(x)$, give
\[
 \mu(B(x,r))\leq C_1r^{2n}+C_2r^{2n-2}
  \operatorname{osc}_{B(x,2r)}v
 \leq Cr^{2n-2+\alpha}.
\]
The constant term integrates to zero against $\ddc\chi\wedge\omega_{\mathrm E}^{n-1}$. The Radon--Nikodym matrix of the positive coefficient measures relative to their trace is positive semidefinite almost everywhere, so $|a_{ij}|\leq(a_{ii}+a_{jj})/2$. A ball cover proves the Hausdorff-null-set assertion. Dividing \eqref{eq:trace-ball} by $r^{2n-2}$ gives zero Lelong numbers after a smooth local plurisubharmonic shift.

For the distance, let $m=2n$ and let $\tau$ be the Euclidean trace of $\eta$. Then $\tau\,\mathrm{d}\lambda\leq C\mu$ on the regular locus and $|\xi|_\eta\leq C\sqrt\tau|\xi|_{\mathrm E}$. Polar integration bounds the average length of segments from $x$ to points of $B(x,R)$ by a constant times $\int_{B(x,R)}\sqrt\tau(\zeta)|\zeta-x|^{1-m}\,\mathrm{d}\lambda(\zeta)$. On a dyadic annulus of radius $s$, Cauchy--Schwarz and \eqref{eq:trace-ball} bound this integral by $Cs^{1-m}s^{m/2}s^{(m-2+\alpha)/2}=Cs^{\alpha/2}$. Summing annuli gives $CR^{\alpha/2}$.

For $x,y$ in an inner chart, average broken segments $[x,z]\cup[z,y]$ over a ball of radius $|x-y|/2$ about their midpoint. The preceding bound supplies a path of length at most $C|x-y|^{\alpha/2}$. Almost every such endpoint avoids $A$ along both segments: proper analytic sets have locally countably many smooth strata of real codimension at least two, and the cones from a regular fixed endpoint over these strata have zero volume. Nested charts keep the chosen path inside $U\setminus A$.

The estimate sends all regular sequences approaching one point of $A$ to a common completion point and extends continuously across $A$. Compactness makes the image the entire completion, but different points of $A$ may be identified. A sequence tending to $A$ cannot converge to a regular point in the metric, since a small closed neighborhood of that regular point has a uniform positive lower metric bound. Thus deletion is incomplete when $A$ is nonempty. The local model $|z|^\alpha+|w|^2$, in coordinates $(z,w)\in\C\times\C^{n-1}$, has trace mass of exact ball order $r^{2n-2+\alpha}$ and normal distance $|z|^{\alpha/2}$, proving both sharpness claims. For a logarithmic solution, this theorem is applied to its positive residual current, with the divisor term recorded separately.
\end{proof}
