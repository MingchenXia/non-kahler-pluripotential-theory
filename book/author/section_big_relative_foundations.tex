\section{Convergence under a fixed measure}
\label{sec:big-relative-foundations}
\label{sec:big-relative-continuity}

As in the semipositive case, a fixed dominating measure gives a common lower envelope after extraction, and then convergence in capacity. The proof follows that of \cref{lem:dominated-relative-rooftop-extraction,thm:relative-fixed-measure-continuity}: the mass interval \eqref{eq:big-relative-mass-interval} replaces the lower bound $\kappa$ used there, and weighted lower semicontinuity holds in the present background by \cref{lem:weighted-extended-lsc}.

\Needspace{20\baselineskip}
\begin{theorem}\label{thm:big-relative-fixed-continuity}
Suppose $u_j\in\mathcal E_\varphi$, $u_j\to u\in\PSH(X,\theta)$ in $L^1(X)$, and $\mu_{u_j}\leq\nu$ for one finite non-pluripolar measure $\nu$. Then there is a subsequence with
\[
 h=P_\theta\left(\inf_j u_j\right)\in\mathcal E_\varphi.
\]
Moreover, for the original sequence,
\[
 u\in\mathcal E_\varphi,\qquad
 u_j\longrightarrow u\text{ in capacity},\qquad
 \mu_{u_j}\rightharpoonup\mu_u\leq\nu.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
We follow the proofs of \cref{lem:dominated-relative-rooftop-extraction,thm:relative-fixed-measure-continuity} and check their ingredients in the present background. The envelope identity and Hartogs' lemma give $u_j\leq\varphi+C$. Finite rooftops and stretching envelopes of members of $\mathcal E_\varphi$ stay in $\mathcal E_\varphi$ by \cref{prop:relative-finite-rooftops,lem:relative-stretch-closure}, and their masses are at least $\ell$ by \eqref{eq:big-relative-mass-interval}. These facts replace the corresponding ones for semipositive backgrounds.

After a common translation, assume $u_j,u\leq\varphi\leq0$. Using \cref{lem:fixed-npp-integrals}, extract once and for all a subsequence along which $u_j\to u$ almost everywhere for smooth volume and
\[
 I_j:=\int_X|\mathrm{e}^{u_j}-\mathrm{e}^u|\,\mu_{u_j}\leq2^{-j}.
\]
For $p_{j,N}=P_\theta(u_j,\ldots,u_N)$, partitioning the contact set according to the first obstacle touched gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:big-finite-roof-domination}
 \mu_{p_{j,N}}\leq\nu,\qquad
 \int_X|\mathrm{e}^{p_{j,N}}-\mathrm{e}^u|\,\mu_{p_{j,N}}\leq2^{1-j}.
\end{equation}
In the proof of the extraction lemma, replace $\kappa$ by $\ell$. For each fixed $B\geq1$, the stretched rooftops $r_{j,N}=Q_B(p_{j,N},\varphi)$ satisfy
\[
 \ell\leq\mu_{r_{j,N}}(X),\qquad
 \mu_{r_{j,N}}\leq B^n\nu.
\]
The exponential test \eqref{eq:relative-rooftop-noncollapse-test} therefore bounds $\sup_Xr_{j,N}$ from below, independently of $N$, once $j$ is sufficiently large. The construction combining the tail with the finitely many earlier terms, with $B=2b$, uses only convex combinations and finite rooftops that are not identically $-\infty$. It provides every defining competitor for
\[
 h=P_\theta(\inf_j u_j)\in\mathcal E_\varphi.
\]
Since $h\leq u\leq\varphi$, upward stability gives $u\in\mathcal E_\varphi$.

For the continuity statements, put $p_j=P_\theta(\inf_{k\geq j}u_k)$ and $p=(\lim_jp_j)^*$. The monotone limits $p_{j,N}\downarrow p_j$ and $p_j\uparrow p$ have the common lower bound $h\not\equiv-\infty$, so they hold in capacity by \cref{lem:monotone-psh-capacity}. All the limits lie in $\mathcal E_\varphi$, and $p\leq u$. Applying \cref{lem:weighted-extended-lsc} twice to \eqref{eq:big-finite-roof-domination} gives
\[
 \int_X|\mathrm{e}^p-\mathrm{e}^u|\,\mu_p=0.
\]
Hence $\mu_p(\{p<u\})=0$, and zero domination (\cref{thm:big-relative-domination} with coefficient zero) gives $p=u$. The upper envelopes $(\sup_{k\geq j}u_k)^*$ decrease to $u$ by Hartogs' lemma, and squeezing the $u_j$ between them and $p_j$ proves convergence in capacity.

Finally, the fixed finite dominating measure gives
\[
 \mu_{u_j}(\{u_j\leq-T\})\leq\nu(\{h\leq-T\})\longrightarrow0
\]
uniformly in $j$, and \cref{thm:general-background-damping} proves weak convergence to $\mu_u\leq\nu$. Starting with an arbitrary subsequence, we obtain a further subsequence with both convergences and the same limit. Hence the conclusions hold for the original sequence.
\end{proof}
