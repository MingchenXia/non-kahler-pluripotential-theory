\chapter{Gauduchon degrees and positivity of vector bundles}
\label{chap:gauduchon-bundle-positivity}

Throughout this chapter, $\omega$ is a fixed Hermitian form on $X$, and $n\geq2$ unless a section specifies another setting. The inputs from \cref{chap:gauduchon} are Gauduchon's theorem, the trace identity, the Gauduchon roots of strictly positive $\ddc$-closed $(n-1,n-1)$-forms found before the proof of \cref{thm:lamari}, and the compatibility condition of \cref{prop:adjoint-gauduchon}.

For a holomorphic line bundle, the conserved Gauduchon pairing of a curvature form is the degree of the bundle, and a positive degree excludes a metric with nonpositive curvature. Fiber averaging extends this obstruction to plurisubharmonic norms near a direct sum of line bundles of positive degree. Polar duality and homogeneous regularization then turn it into an obstruction to semipositive Finsler curvature. The stable ample bundles constructed on Hirzebruch surfaces show that ampleness alone does not remove the obstruction; their projective complements give a geometric way to measure it.

The compatibility condition for the scalar trace equation also improves curvature when the mean curvature is nonnegative: a conformal change spreads directional positivity from a nonempty open set to the whole manifold. Ricci-flat K\"ahler surfaces illustrate the distinction between this directional condition and positivity on every tangent and fiber pair. Holomorphic sectional curvature evaluates the same tangent vector in both places. An integral identity against a K\"ahler metric with nonnegative Ricci curvature excludes quasi-negative holomorphic sectional curvature for every Hermitian metric, closed or not, and on a complex torus it forces a metric with nonpositive holomorphic sectional curvature to be translation invariant. On a projective manifold, positivity on all tangent and fiber pairs admits another duality criterion, expressed by positive measures over the dual bundle; on a branched double cover of the projective line, its untwisted test fails for a line bundle with a positively curved metric. Finally, for a uniformly negative bundle over a K\"ahler base with a plurisubharmonic exhaustion, the squared norm gives logarithmic weights along the zero section and a complete K\"ahler metric on the punctured total space.
