\subsection{Integer orders in fixed projective cones}
\label{sec:nef-projective-integer-cones}
\index{Nef degeneration!nonclosed projective cones}

In the examples of \cref{sec:nef-surface-orders} the order is not an integer because a coefficient of the background vanishes at a point. We now consider backgrounds that are combinations, with strictly positive coefficient functions, of finitely many fixed closed semipositive generators on products of projective spaces and their linear blowups. Such backgrounds have integral orders even when they are not closed. The tool is a comparison with a closed semipositive form $B$. If $\theta$ lies between two positive multiples of $B$, then the Gauduchon quotients of $\theta+\epsilon h$ and of $B+\epsilon\Omega$, for a K\"ahler form $\Omega$, are comparable, and the intersection polynomial of $B+\epsilon\Omega$ has order $n-R$, where $R$ is the largest rank of $B$.

\Needspace{14\baselineskip}
\begin{lemma}\label{lem:nef-logarithmic-rank-comparison}\label{prop:nef-maximum-rank-comparison}
Let $X$ be a compact K\"ahler manifold of dimension $n$, let $B$ be a smooth closed semipositive $(1,1)$-form, and put $R=\max_X\operatorname{rank}B$. Let $\theta$ be a smooth real $(1,1)$-form with
\[
 cB\leq\theta\leq CB
\]
for constants $c,C>0$. Then $\theta$ is nef, and for every Hermitian form $h$ and every smooth positive probability form $\mu$ there are constants $L,U>0$ such that
\[
 L\epsilon^{n-R}\leq\mathcal V_\mu(\theta+\epsilon h)
                       \leq U\epsilon^{n-R},\qquad 0<\epsilon\leq1.
\]
In particular, $\theta$ has common order $n-R$. The same holds, with the same exponent, for $\pi^*\theta$, for every modification $\pi\colon Y\to X$ from a compact K\"ahler manifold $Y$ and every Hermitian form and smooth positive probability form on $Y$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Semipositivity of $\theta$ gives nefness. Fix a K\"ahler form $\Omega$ on $X$ and constants $c_h,C_h>0$ with $c_h\Omega\leq h\leq C_h\Omega$, and put $k=\min(c,c_h)$ and $K=\max(C,C_h)$. For $\epsilon>0$ the forms $\theta+\epsilon h-k(B+\epsilon\Omega)$ and $K(B+\epsilon\Omega)-(\theta+\epsilon h)$ are semipositive, so the monotonicity and homogeneity in \cref{prop:gaud-volume-elementary} give
\begin{equation}\label{eq:nef-logarithmic-quotient-comparison}
 k\lambda_\mu(B+\epsilon\Omega)
 \leq\lambda_\mu(\theta+\epsilon h)
 \leq K\lambda_\mu(B+\epsilon\Omega).
\end{equation}
Since $B$ is semipositive of rank at most $R$, the forms $B^j\wedge\Omega^{n-j}$ are semipositive and vanish for $j>R$. The closed nef formula following \cref{thm:gaud-volume-nef-limit} therefore gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:nef-projective-pointwise-volume}
 \mathcal V_\mu(B+\epsilon\Omega)
 =\sum_{j=0}^{R}\binom nj\epsilon^{n-j}
                         \int_X B^j\wedge\Omega^{n-j},
\end{equation}
with nonnegative coefficients. The form $B^R\wedge\Omega^{n-R}$ is positive at a point where $B$ has rank $R$, so the coefficient of $\epsilon^{n-R}$ is positive. For $0<\epsilon\leq1$ every power $\epsilon^{n-j}$ that occurs is at most $\epsilon^{n-R}$, so the polynomial lies between its leading term and the sum of its coefficients times $\epsilon^{n-R}$. Raising \eqref{eq:nef-logarithmic-quotient-comparison} to the $n$th power gives the two-sided bounds. Fixed positive factors do not affect the order, since their logarithms are divided by $\log\epsilon$.

For a modification $\pi\colon Y\to X$, the inequalities $c\pi^*B\leq\pi^*\theta\leq C\pi^*B$ hold on $Y$, and $\pi^*B$ is smooth, closed and semipositive of rank at most $R$. The set where $B$ has rank $R$ is open and nonempty, so it meets the dense open set over which $\pi$ is biholomorphic; hence $\pi^*B$ has maximal rank $R$. Since $\dim Y=n$, the first part applies on $Y$.
\end{proof}

\subsubsection{Linear blowups and the fixed generators}
The manifolds are products of factors of two kinds. A \emph{plain} factor is $Z_i=\mathbb P^{m_i}$ with $m_i\geq1$; let $H_i$ be its Fubini--Study form normalized to have integral one on lines. A \emph{blown-up} factor is
\[
 Z_i=\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathbb P^{\ell_i}}\mathbb P^{m_i},
 \qquad m_i\geq2,\quad 0\leq\ell_i\leq m_i-2,
 \qquad r_i=m_i-\ell_i-1,
\]
the blowup of $\mathbb P^{m_i}$ along a linear subspace of dimension $\ell_i$. We describe one such factor and suppress the index for the moment. In homogeneous coordinates $[z_0:\cdots:z_\ell:w_0:\cdots:w_r]$ on $\mathbb P^m$, with the center $\{w=0\}$, the blowup has the incidence model
\[
 Z=\{(x,[t])\in\mathbb P^m\times\mathbb P^r:
                         w_at_b=w_bt_a\text{ for all }a,b\}.
\]
Let $p\colon Z\to\mathbb P^m$ and $q\colon Z\to\mathbb P^r$ be the projections. In the chart $t_j=1$, the equations $w_a=t_aw_j$ are the usual charts of the blowup along $\{w_0=\cdots=w_r=0\}$. Writing $w=\lambda t$ shows that $q$ is locally a $\mathbb P^{\ell+1}$-bundle, with fiber coordinates $[z:\lambda]$. Thus $Z$ is smooth, $q$ is a submersion, and the incidence inclusion is an embedding. With $\omega_m$ and $\omega_r$ the normalized Fubini--Study forms on $\mathbb P^m$ and $\mathbb P^r$, set
\[
 H=p^*\omega_m,\qquad Q=q^*\omega_r,\qquad \kappa=H+Q.
\]
The form $\kappa$ is K\"ahler, as the restriction of a product K\"ahler form, whereas $Q$ is semipositive of constant rank $r$.

Let $E=\{w=0\}$ be the exceptional divisor. The coordinate section $w_j$ of $p^*\mathcal O(1)$ vanishes exactly on $E+q^{-1}\{t_j=0\}$, so $\mathcal O(E)=p^*\mathcal O(1)\otimes q^*\mathcal O(-1)$. Give this line bundle the product metric, whose curvature is $H-Q$. For its canonical section $s_E$, the function $\psi_E=\log|s_E|^2$ is locally integrable, and the Poincar\'e--Lelong formula, as in \cref{sec:divisorial-gaud-volume}, gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:nef-projective-exceptional-potential}
 \ddc\psi_E=[E]-H+Q.
\end{equation}

\Needspace{9\baselineskip}
On $X=\prod_i Z_i$, put $n=\sum_i m_i$, and pull back all forms from the factors. Fix smooth strictly positive functions $f_i$ on $X$, and, for each blown-up factor, a smooth strictly positive function $g_i$ on $X$; these functions may depend on all coordinates of the product. The \emph{fixed cone} consists of the backgrounds
\begin{equation}\label{eq:nef-fixed-projective-cone}
 \theta=\sum_i\bigl((a_i+b_if_i)H_i+(c_i+d_ig_i)Q_i\bigr),
 \qquad a_i,b_i,c_i,d_i\geq0,
\end{equation}
where the terms involving $Q_i$, and the parameters $c_i,d_i$, are omitted for plain factors. All coefficient functions and generators are fixed, and only the parameters vary. Every member of the cone is smooth and semipositive, hence nef. We say that $H_i$ \emph{occurs} in $\theta$ if $a_i+b_i>0$, and that $Q_i$ occurs if $c_i+d_i>0$. Define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:nef-projective-effective-rank}
 R_i(\theta)=
 \begin{cases}
 m_i,&\text{if }H_i\text{ occurs},\\
 r_i,&\text{if }H_i\text{ does not occur and }Q_i\text{ occurs},\\
 0,&\text{if neither occurs},
 \end{cases}
 \qquad R(\theta)=\sum_i R_i(\theta).
\end{equation}
The middle case can only occur on blown-up factors.

\Needspace{9\baselineskip}
\begin{theorem}\label{thm:nef-projective-integer-orders}
Every background $\theta$ in the fixed cone \eqref{eq:nef-fixed-projective-cone} has common order $n-R(\theta)$. More precisely, for every smooth positive probability form $\mu$ and Hermitian form $h$ there are constants $L,U>0$, depending on $\theta,\mu,h$, such that
\begin{equation}\label{eq:nef-projective-power-bounds}
 L\epsilon^{n-R(\theta)}
 \leq\mathcal V_\mu(\theta+\epsilon h)
 \leq U\epsilon^{n-R(\theta)},\qquad 0<\epsilon\leq1.
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Let $B_\theta$ be the sum of the generators $H_i$ and $Q_i$ that occur in $\theta$; it is closed and semipositive. The coefficient function of an occurring generator takes values in a compact subinterval of $(0,\infty)$, because $f_i$ and $g_i$ are strictly positive, so $cB_\theta\leq\theta\leq CB_\theta$ for some constants $c,C>0$ (any constants if $\theta=0$). Ranks of forms pulled back from different factors add pointwise. On a plain factor $H_i$ has rank $m_i$. On a blown-up factor, $H_i$ and $H_i+Q_i$ have rank $m_i$ off $E_i$, where $p_i$ is biholomorphic, while $Q_i$ has constant rank $r_i$. Hence the maximal rank of $B_\theta$, attained off all exceptional divisors, is $R(\theta)$, and \cref{lem:nef-logarithmic-rank-comparison} applies.
\end{proof}

Members of the fixed cone need not be closed: by construction
\[
 \mathrm d\theta=\sum_i b_i\,\mathrm df_i\wedge H_i
                  +\sum_{i\text{ blown up}}d_i\,\mathrm dg_i\wedge Q_i.
\]
If only one of these terms has a nonzero coefficient and the corresponding form, say $\mathrm df_i\wedge H_i$, is nonzero, then $\theta$ is not closed. On a blown-up factor, for instance, if $g$ is a small positive perturbation of a constant that varies along a fiber of $q$, then $\mathrm dg\wedge Q\ne0$, since $r\geq1$. On $\mathbb P^{m_1}\times\mathbb P^{m_2}$ with $m_1,m_2\geq1$, a positive function $f$ that varies on the second factor gives the nonclosed background $fH_1$ of order $m_2$. Adding $\ddc$ of a smooth function does not change the exterior derivative.

\subsubsection{Equality of orders under modifications}
By \cref{prop:nef-modification-volume}, pulling back by a modification can only increase the orders. For the fixed cone, the orders are preserved.

\Needspace{11\baselineskip}
\begin{proposition}\label{prop:nef-projective-modification-equality}
Let $\theta$ be in the fixed cone, and let $\pi\colon Y\to X$ be a modification from a compact K\"ahler manifold $Y$, for instance the product in which some plain factors are replaced by linear blowups as above. Then $\pi^*\theta$ has common order $n-R(\theta)$, and satisfies two-sided power bounds with this exponent for every smooth positive probability form and every Hermitian perturbation on $Y$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
By the proof of \cref{thm:nef-projective-integer-orders}, $cB_\theta\leq\theta\leq CB_\theta$, where $B_\theta$ is closed and semipositive of maximal rank $R(\theta)$. The last assertion of \cref{lem:nef-logarithmic-rank-comparison} applies.
\end{proof}

\subsubsection{Subtracting effective divisors}
We now subtract effective divisors from members of the fixed cone. Let $\mathcal O(H_i)$ and $\mathcal O(Q_i)$ be the pullbacks of $\mathcal O(1)$ under the projections defining $H_i$ and $Q_i$, with their Fubini--Study metrics, whose curvature forms are $H_i$ and $Q_i$. Consider line bundles that are tensor products of integer powers of these bundles, negative powers included, and finite nonnegative real combinations of zero divisors of their nonzero holomorphic sections $s_\nu$:
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 D=\sum_\nu t_\nu\operatorname{div}(s_\nu),\quad t_\nu\geq0,\\
 \eta_D=\sum_i(\delta_iH_i+\rho_iQ_i),\qquad
 \Phi_D=\sum_\nu t_\nu\log|s_\nu|^2.
 \end{gathered}
\]
Here $\eta_D$ is the corresponding combination of the curvature forms of the fixed metrics, so that $[D]=\eta_D+\ddc\Phi_D$. The terms involving $Q_i$ are omitted for plain factors.

\Needspace{8\baselineskip}
\begin{lemma}\label{lem:nef-projective-effective-divisor-range}
The coefficients $(\delta_i,\rho_i)$ that occur for divisors $D$ of this form are exactly those satisfying
\[
 \delta_i\geq0\text{ on plain factors};\qquad
 \delta_i\geq0,\quad\delta_i+\rho_i\geq0
                         \text{ on blown-up factors}.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
It suffices to treat one nonzero holomorphic section $s$, since nonnegative real combinations preserve the inequalities. Choose a point at which $s$ is nonzero and which lies outside all exceptional divisors, and fix the coordinates of the other factors. A projective line through that point inside a fiber of $q_i$ has $H_i$-degree one and $Q_i$-degree zero. The restriction of $s$ to it is a nonzero section, so the exponent of $\mathcal O(H_i)$ is nonnegative. On a plain factor, a line through the point gives the same inequality.

On a blown-up factor, write the chosen point as $([z:w],[w])$ with $w\ne0$, and choose a linear map $A$ with $Aw=z$. The section of $q_i$ given by $[t]\mapsto([At:t],[t])$ passes through the point, and both $H_i$ and $Q_i$ have degree one on each projective line in its image. Such a line through the point exists because $r_i\geq1$, and $s$ restricts nontrivially to it. Its degree shows that the sum of the two exponents is nonnegative.

Conversely, let $F_i$ be the pullback under $q_i$ of a hyperplane. An admissible pair $(\delta_i,\rho_i)$ is realized by $\delta_iE_i+(\delta_i+\rho_i)F_i$, since $E_i$ has curvature $H_i-Q_i$, and coefficients on plain factors are realized by hyperplanes. These are allowed nonnegative combinations and give every coefficient tuple in the stated range.
\end{proof}

\Needspace{13\baselineskip}
\begin{theorem}\label{thm:nef-projective-residual-equality}
Let $\theta$ be in the fixed cone, with parameters $a_i,b_i,c_i,d_i$, and let $D$ be a divisor of the above form. Assume
\[
 a_i\geq\delta_i\text{ for all }i,\qquad
 c_i\geq\rho_i\text{ for every blown-up factor}.
\]
Then the residual $\beta=\theta-\eta_D$ lies in the fixed cone, and the common orders $a(\beta)$ and $a(\theta)$ satisfy
\[
 a(\beta)-a(\theta)=\sum_i\bigl(R_i(\theta)-R_i(\beta)\bigr)\geq0.
\]
The two orders are equal if and only if, for every factor,
\begin{align*}
 a_i-\delta_i+b_i&>0&&\text{whenever }a_i+b_i>0,\\
 c_i-\rho_i+d_i&>0&&\text{whenever }a_i=b_i=0\text{ and }c_i+d_i>0;
\end{align*}
the second condition concerns only blown-up factors.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The residual parameters $(a_i-\delta_i,b_i,c_i-\rho_i,d_i)$ are nonnegative, so $\beta$ lies in the fixed cone; in particular it is smooth, semipositive and nef. Apply \cref{thm:nef-projective-integer-orders} to $\theta$ and to $\beta$. If $H_i$ occurs in $\theta$ but not in $\beta$, the rank $R_i$ drops from $m_i$ to $r_i<m_i$ or to zero. If $H_i$ does not occur in $\theta$, then $a_i=b_i=0$, and the range of \cref{lem:nef-projective-effective-divisor-range} together with $a_i\geq\delta_i$ forces $\delta_i=0$ and hence $\rho_i\geq0$; thus $Q_i$ cannot appear in $\beta$ if it was absent from $\theta$. Therefore no rank $R_i$ increases, each difference $R_i(\theta)-R_i(\beta)$ is nonnegative, and the sum vanishes exactly when the generator that determines $R_i(\theta)$ still occurs in $\beta$ for every $i$. This is the stated pair of conditions.
\end{proof}

The parameter inequalities guarantee that the residual stays in the fixed cone; they do not describe all divisors whose residual is nef.

\Needspace{10\baselineskip}
\begin{example}\label{ex:nef-nonclosed-projective-order-jumps}
On $Z=\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathbb P^\ell}\mathbb P^m$, choose a smooth function $g>0$ with $\mathrm dg\wedge Q\ne0$. For $t>0$, the nonclosed positive background $\theta_t=tH+gQ$ has order zero. Removing $sE$, with its fixed curvature form $s(H-Q)$ and $0\leq s\leq t$, gives
\[
 \beta_s=(t-s)H+(g+s)Q,\qquad
 \theta_t+\ddc(s\psi_E)=s[E]+\beta_s.
\]
Every residual $\beta_s$ is nef and not closed. Its order equals that of $\theta_t$ exactly for $s<t$, and at $s=t$ the order jumps by $\ell+1$. These examples realize each integer jump $1,\ldots,m-1$.
\end{example}
\begin{proof}
The lower bound $\theta_t\geq\min(t,\min g)\kappa>0$ shows that $\theta_t$ is positive. For $s<t$ the form $H$ occurs in $\beta_s$ and the rank is $m$; at $s=t$ only $Q$ occurs, with rank $r=m-\ell-1$. The order formula gives zero and $m-r=\ell+1$, respectively. The exterior derivative of every residual is $\mathrm dg\wedge Q\ne0$. Finally, let $\ell$ range from zero to $m-2$.
\end{proof}

At the endpoint $s=t$, the current $t[E]+\beta_t$ in the class of $\theta_t$ has a divisorial part, although the minimal multiplicities of $\theta_t$ vanish because $\theta_t$ is nef, as explained before \cref{cor:nef-boundary-minimal-removal}.
