\subsection{Pluriclosed metrics under deformation}
\label{sec:pluriclosed-deformations}

Pluriclosed metrics persist under small deformations of the complex structure when the Aeppli number $h_A^{1,1}$ stays constant. Let $M$ be a compact smooth manifold of real dimension $2n$, $n\geq1$, and let $J_t$, $t\in U\subset\mathbb R^\ell$, be a smooth family of integrable complex structures, where $U$ is an open neighborhood of $0$. Write $\Omega_t^{p,q}$ for the smooth complex forms of type $(p,q)$ for $J_t$. A pluriclosed form belongs to the kernel of $\partial_t\bar\partial_t$, and the corresponding Aeppli group is
\[
 H_A^{1,1}(M,J_t)
 =\frac{\ker(\partial_t\bar\partial_t\colon\Omega_t^{1,1}\to\Omega_t^{2,2})}
 {\partial_t\Omega_t^{0,1}+\bar\partial_t\Omega_t^{1,0}},
 \qquad h_A^{1,1}(M,J_t)=\dim_{\mathbb C}H_A^{1,1}(M,J_t).
\]
\index{Aeppli cohomology}
Constancy of this dimension gives a smooth family of projections onto the forms satisfying the closedness constraint, and positivity then persists near a given strictly positive form.

\Needspace{8\baselineskip}
\begin{lemma}\label{lem:aeppli-smooth-projection}
The number $h_A^{1,1}(M,J_t)$ is finite. If it is constant near $0$, then, after shrinking $U$, the orthogonal projections
\[
 \mathcal P_t\colon\Omega_t^{1,1}\longrightarrow\ker(\partial_t\bar\partial_t)
\]
for any smooth family of auxiliary Hermitian metrics vary smoothly with $t$ on smooth forms and preserve reality.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
\emph{Step 1: an elliptic operator.} Fix $t$ and suppress it from the notation; the auxiliary metric defines the adjoints. Put $C=\partial\bar\partial$ and $E=\bar\partial\partial^*$. The nonnegative self-adjoint operator
\begin{equation}\label{eq:aeppli-elliptic-operator}
 \mathcal L=C^*C+CC^*+E^*E+EE^*
                  +\partial\partial^*+\bar\partial\bar\partial^*
\end{equation}
is elliptic of order four. Indeed, at a nonzero real covector $\xi$, the principal symbols of $\partial$ and $\bar\partial$ are, up to the common factor $\mathrm i$, exterior multiplication by the $(1,0)$ and $(0,1)$ parts of $\xi$. These parts are orthogonal and have the same norm $r>0$. Split the exterior algebra according to which of these two directions are present. The four terms of order four act on the four resulting summands respectively, and the sum of their symbols is $r^4\operatorname{Id}$. The terms of lower order do not affect ellipticity.

The quadratic form of $\mathcal L$ is a sum of squared norms, which gives the inclusion $\subseteq$ in
\[
 \ker\mathcal L=\ker C\cap\ker\partial^*\cap\ker\bar\partial^*.
\]
For the reverse inclusion, note that $C^*=\bar\partial^*\partial^*$, $E=\bar\partial\partial^*$ and $E^*=\partial\bar\partial^*$ all vanish under these three conditions. Elliptic theory on the compact manifold gives a finite-dimensional space of smooth forms $\ker\mathcal L$, the orthogonal projection $\mathcal H$ onto it and the Green operator $\mathcal G$, with $\mathcal L\mathcal G=\operatorname{Id}-\mathcal H$.

\emph{Step 2: the projection.} Set $R=\mathcal L-C^*C$. Each term of $R$ has image in $\operatorname{im}\partial+\operatorname{im}\bar\partial$. Thus every smooth $(1,1)$-form $\alpha$ has the decomposition
\begin{equation}\label{eq:aeppli-projection-decomposition}
 \alpha=\mathcal H\alpha+R\mathcal G\alpha+C^*C\mathcal G\alpha.
\end{equation}
The first two terms lie in $\ker C$, since $C\partial=C\bar\partial=0$, and the last is orthogonal to $\ker C$. Consequently
\begin{equation}\label{eq:aeppli-closed-projection}
 \mathcal P=\operatorname{Id}-C^*C\mathcal G
\end{equation}
is the orthogonal projection onto $\ker C$. If $C\alpha=0$, the last term in \eqref{eq:aeppli-projection-decomposition} vanishes, so the Aeppli class of $\alpha$ is represented by $\mathcal H\alpha$. A harmonic form in $\operatorname{im}\partial+\operatorname{im}\bar\partial$ is zero, by orthogonality. This identifies $H_A^{1,1}$ with $\ker\mathcal L$ and proves finiteness.
\index{Aeppli Laplacian}

\emph{Step 3: dependence on $t$.} Now identify the varying type bundles and their $L^2$ spaces smoothly with those at $t=0$, using a unitary identification for the auxiliary metrics. The coefficients of $\mathcal L_t$ then vary smoothly. Choose a circle $|z|=\varepsilon$ enclosing no nonzero eigenvalue of $\mathcal L_0$. The resolvents of $\mathcal L_0$ on this circle are bounded from $H^s$ to $H^{s+4}$. The Neumann series for their perturbations gives smooth resolvents on the circle for small $t$, and the contour projections have constant rank. Constancy of $h_A^{1,1}$ makes that rank equal to $\dim\ker\mathcal L_t$. Hence the contour projections are exactly $\mathcal H_t$, and $\mathcal L_t$ has no additional small eigenvalues.

\Needspace{5\baselineskip}
The operators
\[
 \mathcal G_t=(\mathcal L_t+\mathcal H_t)^{-1}-\mathcal H_t
\]
vary smoothly on every Sobolev scale, by the same inverse argument and elliptic regularity. Formula \eqref{eq:aeppli-closed-projection} therefore gives the asserted smooth family $\mathcal P_t$. Complex conjugation sends $C_t$ to $-C_t$, exchanges $E_t$ with $E_t^*$, and exchanges the last two terms of \eqref{eq:aeppli-elliptic-operator}. Thus $\mathcal L_t$, $\mathcal H_t$, $\mathcal G_t$ and $\mathcal P_t$ commute with conjugation.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:pluriclosed-deformation-persistence}
Suppose that $(M,J_0)$ carries a pluriclosed Hermitian form $\omega_0$ and that $h_A^{1,1}(M,J_t)$ is constant near $0$. Then for small $t$ there is a smooth family of pluriclosed Hermitian forms $\omega_t$ on $(M,J_t)$ with the prescribed value $\omega_0$ at $t=0$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Let $\pi_t^{1,1}$ denote the projection onto forms of type $(1,1)$ for $J_t$, and use the operators of \cref{lem:aeppli-smooth-projection}. Define
\[
 \omega_t=\mathcal P_t\bigl(\pi_t^{1,1}\omega_0\bigr).
\]
These are smooth real $(1,1)$-forms satisfying $\partial_t\bar\partial_t\omega_t=0$. At $t=0$ the projection fixes $\omega_0$, since $\omega_0$ already satisfies this equation. The tensors $\omega_t(\cdot,J_t\cdot)$ converge uniformly to the positive definite tensor $\omega_0(\cdot,J_0\cdot)$, which has a uniform positive lower bound by compactness. Hence they remain positive definite after shrinking $U$.
\end{proof}
\index{Pluriclosed metric!deformation}

The construction projects onto $\partial_t\bar\partial_t$-closed forms and obtains positivity by continuity near $\omega_0$; closedness and conditions on the curvature would impose further equations.
