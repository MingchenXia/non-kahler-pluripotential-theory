\extrachap{Conventions}

Unless otherwise stated, $X$ is a connected compact complex manifold of dimension $n\geq1$. A Hermitian form is a smooth real strictly positive $(1,1)$-form. We reserve $\omega$ for such a form and allow $\theta$ to be an arbitrary smooth real $(1,1)$-form. Closedness of $\theta$ or $\omega$ will always be stated.

We use the normalizations of \cite{SGPT}:
\[
 \dc=\frac{\mathrm i}{4\pi}(\bar\partial-\partial),
 \qquad \ddc=\frac{\mathrm i}{2\pi}\partial\bar\partial.
\]
On a disk in $\mathbb C$ with coordinate $z$, $\ddc\log|z|^2$ is the unit Dirac measure at the origin. For a quasi-plurisubharmonic weight $u$, multiplier ideals use $\exp(-u)$. Monge--Amp\`ere masses are computed without a factor $1/n!$.
