\subsection{Spherical Monge--Amp\`ere measures}
\label{subsec:psh-spherical-measures}

A bounded radial maximum gives a measure concentrated on a real hypersurface. This supplies singular measures that still give zero mass to pluripolar sets.

\begin{lemma}\label{lem:spherical-bounded-product}\index{Monge--Ampere product@Monge--Amp\`ere product!spherical measure}
For $r>0$, let $\sigma_r$ be the normalized Euclidean surface measure on $\{|z|=r\}\subset\mathbb C^n$. Then
\[
 \bigl(\ddc\log\max\{|z|^2,r^2\}\bigr)^n=\sigma_r.
\]
In particular, $\sigma_r$ does not charge pluripolar sets.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write $t=\log|z|^2$ and approximate $\max\{t,\log r^2\}$ uniformly by smooth convex increasing functions $F_\epsilon(t)$ whose derivatives equal zero below $\log r^2-\epsilon$ and one above $\log r^2+\epsilon$. Their compositions with $t$ extend smoothly across zero. Since $\ddc t$ has rank $n-1$ off zero, the chain rule gives
\[
 (\ddc F_\epsilon(t))^n
 =n(F_\epsilon')^{n-1}F_\epsilon''\,\mathrm dt\wedge\dc t
                                      \wedge(\ddc t)^{n-1}.
\]
The integral of $\dc t\wedge(\ddc t)^{n-1}$ over each sphere is one. Indeed, $\dc t$ integrates to one on the Hopf circle, and the induced form on $\mathbb P^{n-1}$ is $\ddc\log(1+|w|^2)$; its top power has density $(n-1)!\pi^{-(n-1)}(1+|w|^2)^{-n}$ on the affine chart, whose integral is one. Thus each displayed radial measure has total mass $\int ( (F_\epsilon')^n)'\,\mathrm dt=1$. Its support shrinks to the sphere, and its rotation invariance identifies the limit as $\sigma_r$. Uniform bounded-potential convergence identifies that limit with the product in the statement. Non-pluripolarity follows from \cref{thm:bt-input}.
\end{proof}
