\subsection{Negative-line-bundle quotients}
\label{sec:elliptic-line-endpoint-examples}
\index{Elliptic fibration!negative-line-bundle quotient}

Take $B=\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1$, with normalized pulled Fubini--Study forms $\omega_1,\omega_2$, and put
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 \Omega=a\omega_1+b\omega_2,\quad a,b\in\mathbb N_{>0},\qquad
 L=\mathcal O(-a,-b),\\
 X=L^\times/\langle v\mapsto\rho v\rangle,\quad0<\rho<1.
 \end{gathered}
\]
Let $r=\log\|v\|^2$ for the tensor-product tautological norm and set $\eta=\partial r$.

\begin{proposition}\label{prop:elliptic-line-endpoint-example}
The quotient $X$ is a compact complex threefold with a principal elliptic fibration over $B$. For $g=\pi^*\Omega+s\mathrm i\eta\wedge\bar\eta$, $s>0$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:elliptic-line-lower-mass}
 v_-(g)=24\pi s ab\log(1/\rho).
\end{equation}
Every Hermitian background satisfies the full-current endpoint bound of \cref{thm:elliptic-curvature-endpoint}. The manifold has $H^2(X,\R)\ne0$ and admits no balanced or pluriclosed metric; in particular it is not a scalar Hopf threefold.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
In product coordinates the squared frame norm is $(1+|z_1|^2)^a(1+|z_2|^2)^b$. Therefore
\[
 \ddc r=\pi^*\Omega,\qquad \mathrm d\eta=2\pi\mathrm i\pi^*\Omega.
\]
Scaling changes $r$ by a constant, so $\eta$ descends. The nonzero radial period makes the action free and proper; a compact unit-circle bundle times a fundamental radial interval gives compactness. Each fiber is $\C^*/\rho^{\mathbb Z}$. Its logarithmic coordinate has periods $\log\rho$ and $2\pi\mathrm i$, giving
\[
 A_E=4\pi\log(1/\rho),\qquad Q=4\pi^2\Omega^2>0,
 \qquad\int_B\Omega^2=2ab.
\]
The curvature theorem gives \eqref{eq:elliptic-line-lower-mass} and excludes pluriclosed metrics.

To check $H^2\ne0$, put $A=\eta+\bar\eta$ and $B_1=\mathrm i(\eta-\bar\eta)$. Then $\mathrm dA=0$ and $\mathrm dB_1=-4\pi\pi^*\Omega$. If $\pi^*(\omega_1-\omega_2)=\mathrm d\zeta$, average $\zeta$ over the compact elliptic translation group. Its invariant decomposition is
\[
 \zeta=\pi^*\xi+(\pi^*f)A+(\pi^*j)B_1.
\]
Evaluating one vertical and one horizontal direction gives $\mathrm df=\mathrm dj=0$. Thus
\[
 \omega_1-\omega_2=\mathrm d\xi-4\pi j\Omega.
\]
The two ruling integrals force $1=-4\pi ja$ and $-1=-4\pi jb$, a contradiction. A scalar Hopf threefold is diffeomorphic to $S^5\times S^1$ and has zero second real cohomology.

Finally $\pi^*\Omega$ is a nonzero semipositive exact form. Its pairing with the closed positive square of a balanced metric would be zero by Stokes and strictly positive by positivity. Hence no balanced metric exists.
\end{proof}
