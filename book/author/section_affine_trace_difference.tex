On a higher model, the trace current can exceed the pullback of the trace current from a lower model. The effective exceptional difference of \cref{thm:trace-exceptional-difference} therefore translates the residual class, and the translation preserves the prescribed exterior derivative.

\begin{corollary}\label{cor:affine-trace-exceptional-difference}\index{Affine class!exceptional translation}
Let $g\colon Z'\to Z$ be a smooth compact modification over $Y$, and let $E_g$ be the effective exceptional divisor of \cref{thm:trace-exceptional-difference}. The trace classes of \cref{thm:nonclosed-trace-bclass} satisfy
\[
 \mathbb B_{Z'}(u;Y,\theta)
       =g^*\mathbb B_Z(u;Y,\theta)-\{[E_g]\}_{\mathrm{BC}},
\]
where the Bott--Chern class on the right acts by translation on the affine space $\mathscr A_{Z'}(\theta)$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Let $\pi\colon Z\to Y$ be the structure map and put $\pi'=\pi\circ g$. Abbreviate $S_Z=S_Z(u;Y)$, and use \cref{lem:closed-current-global-potential} to write $S_Z=B_Z+\ddc v_Z$ with $B_Z$ smooth and closed and $v_Z$ quasi-plurisubharmonic. The affine class $\mathbb B_Z$ has the smooth representative $\pi^*\theta-B_Z$. Its pullback is therefore the class of $\pi'^*\theta-g^*S_Z$, since the two representatives differ by $\ddc(v_Z\circ g)$. Substituting $S_{Z'}=g^*S_Z+[E_g]$ gives the asserted identity. Both representatives have exterior derivative $\pi'^*\mathrm{d}\theta$, because the subtracted currents are closed, so the subtraction takes place within $\mathscr A_{Z'}(\theta)$.
\end{proof}

The cocycle identity \eqref{eq:trace-exceptional-cocycle} makes these translations compatible with composition of modifications, and by \cref{thm:trace-exceptional-difference} their analytic-ideal approximants converge in total variation of the trace measures. On the other hand, \cref{thm:trace-envelope-modification} identifies the trace envelopes on the models with pullbacks of the original trace envelope. The exceptional translation accounts for the additional divisorial subtraction on the higher model; it is consistent with this exact pullback of the envelope, and it can be nonzero even when the trace data are realized exactly, as in \cref{ex:exceptional-trace-with-exact-potential}.
