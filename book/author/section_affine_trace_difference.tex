On a higher model, the trace current can exceed the pullback of the trace current on the original model. The effective exceptional difference from \cref{thm:trace-exceptional-difference} therefore translates the residual class, while preserving the prescribed exterior derivative.

\begin{corollary}\label{cor:affine-trace-exceptional-difference}\index{Affine class!exceptional translation}
For the trace classes in \cref{thm:nonclosed-trace-bclass} and a compact modification $g:Z'\to Z$ over $Y$, let $E_g$ be the effective exceptional divisor in \cref{thm:trace-exceptional-difference}. Then
\[
 \mathbb B_{Z'}(u;Y,\theta)
       =g^*\mathbb B_Z(u;Y,\theta)-\{[E_g]\}_{\mathrm{BC}}.
\]
Here the Bott--Chern class on the right acts by translation on the affine space $\mathscr A_{Z'}(\theta)$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Let $\pi:Z\to Y$ be the structure map. Abbreviate $S_Z=S_Z(u;Y)$ and use \cref{lem:closed-current-global-potential} to write $S_Z=B_Z+\ddc v_Z$ with $B_Z$ smooth and closed and $v_Z$ quasi-plurisubharmonic. The affine class $\mathbb B_Z$ has smooth representative $\pi^*\theta-B_Z$. Its pullback therefore equals the class of $\pi'^*\theta-g^*S_Z$, where $\pi'=\pi\circ g$; the two representatives differ by $\ddc(v_Z\circ g)$. Substituting $S_{Z'}=g^*S_Z+[E_g]$ gives the displayed identity. The exterior derivative of both representatives is $\pi'^*\mathrm{d}\theta$, since the subtracted currents are closed. Thus the subtraction takes place within the stated affine space.
\end{proof}

The cocycle identity \eqref{eq:trace-exceptional-cocycle} makes these translations compatible with composition, and \cref{thm:trace-exceptional-difference} gives total variation convergence of their analytic-ideal approximants in trace mass. Meanwhile, \cref{thm:trace-envelope-modification} identifies the envelopes on the models as pullbacks of the original trace envelope. The exceptional translation records the additional divisorial subtraction; it is compatible with exact pullback of this envelope and can be nonzero even for exactly realized trace data, as in \cref{ex:exceptional-trace-with-exact-potential}.
