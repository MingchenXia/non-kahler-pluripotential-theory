\section{Exceptional divisors between trace models}
\label{sec:trace-exceptional-divisors}

Return to the ambient trace data fixed at the beginning of the chapter, and write $S_Z=S_Z(u;Y)$. Pullback of the full current is now available. Under a modification, it is bounded above by the trace current on the higher model, with equality of coefficients along strict transforms. The difference is therefore a finite effective exceptional divisor. Its logarithmic potential describes the change in the ordinary types of the divisorial parts.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:trace-exceptional-difference}\index{Exceptional divisor!trace correction and cocycle}
For a smooth compact modification $g:Z'\to Z$, there is a unique finite effective real divisor $E_g$ supported on its exceptional locus such that
\begin{equation}\label{eq:trace-exceptional-difference}
 S_{Z'}=g^*S_Z+[E_g].
\end{equation}
For $h:Z''\to Z'$, the divisors satisfy
\begin{equation}\label{eq:trace-exceptional-cocycle}
 E_{g\circ h}=E_h+h^*E_g.
\end{equation}
Their coefficients are additive and positively homogeneous in the ambient trace potential $u$.

The analytic-ideal approximants on the two models give finite effective exceptional divisors
\[
 [E_{m,g}]=D_{m,Z'}-g^*D_{m,Z}.
\]
\Needspace{5\baselineskip}
For any Gauduchon metric $\gamma'$ on $Z'$, the measures
\[
 [E_{m,g}]\wedge(\gamma')^{r-1}
     \longrightarrow [E_g]\wedge(\gamma')^{r-1},\qquad r=\dim Y,
\]
converge in total variation.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Enumerate the positive divisorial components of $S_Z$. Every finite partial sum has pullback at most $S_{Z'}$ by \cref{thm:trace-divisorial-model-compatibility}. \Cref{cor:countable-divisorial-pullback} identifies the increasing limit of those pullbacks with $g^*S_Z$. Thus $S_{Z'}-g^*S_Z$ is a positive closed divisorial current. The two currents have equal coefficients along every strict transform of a divisor on $Z$, because these orders describe the same valuation. Every other prime divisor of $Z'$ is exceptional for $g$. The exceptional analytic set has only finitely many irreducible components on compact $Z'$. Its divisorial components therefore support a finite effective real divisor $E_g$. Coefficients of integration currents identify it uniquely.

Pullback of positive closed $(1,1)$-currents is functorial, by composition of local psh potentials. Using \eqref{eq:trace-exceptional-difference} for $h$ and $g$ gives
\[
 S_{Z''}=h^*S_{Z'}+[E_h]
        =(g\circ h)^*S_Z+[h^*E_g]+[E_h].
\]
Uniqueness gives \eqref{eq:trace-exceptional-cocycle}. Additivity and positive homogeneity follow from the same properties of every trace coefficient and linearity of pullback.

\Needspace{10\baselineskip}
For a fixed $m$, apply \eqref{eq:trace-ideal-model-inequality} to the finite divisorial support of the image ideal on $Z$. After division by $m$, it gives $D_{m,Z'}-g^*D_{m,Z}\geq0$. The coefficients on strict transforms are zero, so this difference is the integration current of an effective exceptional real divisor $E_{m,g}$.

Choose also a Gauduchon metric $\gamma$ on $Z$. By \cref{thm:trace-divisorial-current-budget}, the currents $S_Z-D_{m,Z}$ and $S_{Z'}-D_{m,Z'}$ are positive and their trace masses tend to zero. Subtraction of the two identities for the exceptional currents gives
\[
 [E_{m,g}]-[E_g]
   =-(S_{Z'}-D_{m,Z'})+g^*(S_Z-D_{m,Z}).
\]
Both terms on the right, before the minus sign, are positive. The total variation of the signed trace measure is consequently at most
\[
 \int_{Z'}(S_{Z'}-D_{m,Z'})\wedge(\gamma')^{r-1}
 +C_g\int_Z(S_Z-D_{m,Z})\wedge\gamma^{r-1},
\]
by \eqref{eq:modification-linear-mass-bound}. Both terms tend to zero, proving total variation convergence.
\end{proof}

Fix a modification $g:Z'\to Z$ as in the theorem and its divisor $E_g$. The coefficient at an exceptional prime $F\subset Z'$ is explicitly
\begin{equation}\label{eq:trace-exceptional-coefficient}
 \tau_{Y,u}(F)
  -\sum_{D\subset Z}\tau_{Y,u}(D)\operatorname{ord}_F(g^*D).
\end{equation}
Here $D$ ranges over prime divisors on $Z$ with positive trace coefficient. The sum is countable and convergent. Indeed it is the coefficient of $g^*S_Z$ in the positive divisorial series of \cref{cor:countable-divisorial-pullback}; it is bounded above by $\tau_{Y,u}(F)$. The same current identity compares the ordinary types supplied by \cref{cor:divisorial-current-ordinary-type}.

\Needspace{14\baselineskip}
\begin{corollary}\label{cor:trace-model-ordinary-comparison}\index{Divisorial current!ordinary types under pullback}
Choose decompositions $S_Z=B_Z+\ddc v_Z$ and $S_{Z'}=B_{Z'}+\ddc v_{Z'}$ with smooth closed forms $B_Z,B_{Z'}$, as in \cref{cor:divisorial-current-ordinary-type}. If $\rho_{E_g}$ is a global logarithmic potential of the finite real divisor $E_g$, then
\[
 v_{Z'}-(v_Z\circ g)-\rho_{E_g}
\]
is represented by a smooth function. In particular
\[
 v_{Z'}\preceq v_Z\circ g,
\]
and the two ordinary types are equal if and only if $E_g=0$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Choose smooth Hermitian metrics on the line bundles of the prime components of $E_g$. Let $\alpha_{E_g}$ be the weighted sum of the smooth closed curvature forms of these metrics. The weighted sum of the logarithms of the squared canonical-section norms gives $\rho_{E_g}$ with
\[
 \ddc\rho_{E_g}=[E_g]-\alpha_{E_g}.
\]
The function $\rho_{E_g}$ is quasi-plurisubharmonic and bounded above on compact $Z'$.

\Needspace{10\baselineskip}
The chosen decompositions and \eqref{eq:trace-exceptional-difference} give
\[
 \ddc\bigl(v_{Z'}-v_Z\circ g-\rho_{E_g}\bigr)
       =g^*B_Z+\alpha_{E_g}-B_{Z'},
\]
which is smooth. By \cref{lem:closed-current-global-potential}, the $L^1$ difference is represented by a smooth function. The corresponding equality of quasi-plurisubharmonic functions extends from almost everywhere to everywhere by \cref{lem:qpsh-almost-everywhere-order}. The bounded-above logarithmic potential gives the ordinary domination.

If $E_g=0$, the smooth difference gives ordinary equivalence. Conversely ordinary equivalence would give equal generic Lelong numbers on every prime divisor of $Z'$. The smooth residual terms show that their difference on a prime component of $E_g$ is precisely its positive coefficient, a contradiction. Hence ordinary equivalence forces $E_g=0$.
\end{proof}

When $\theta$ is closed, the positive residual currents $R_Z$ constructed in the proof of \cref{prop:trace-bott-chern-bclass} and their Bott--Chern classes satisfy
\[
 \begin{split}
 R_{Z'}&=g^*R_Z-[E_g],\\
 \mathbb A_{Z'}(u;Y,\theta)
       &=g^*\mathbb A_Z(u;Y,\theta)-\{[E_g]\}_{\mathrm{BC}}.
 \end{split}
\]
Indeed the smooth background and the common trace potential pull back, while \eqref{eq:trace-exceptional-difference} gives the change in the subtracted divisorial current. The exceptional term removes the additional prescribed divisorial vanishing on the higher model. It is compatible with exact pullback of the trace envelope, which retains the inequalities on all models. The following example also shows that this term can be nonzero when the trace data has an exact potential.

\Needspace{9\baselineskip}
\begin{example}\label{ex:exceptional-trace-with-exact-potential}
Let $Y=\mathbb P^2$ with homogeneous coordinates $[Z_0:Z_1:Z_2]$ and choose $a\in\mathbb Q_{>0}$. Put
\[
 u=a\log\frac{|Z_1|^2+|Z_2|^2}
                   {|Z_0|^2+|Z_1|^2+|Z_2|^2}.
\]
Take the ambient manifold to be $Y$ itself. If $\sigma$ is the Fubini--Study form, then $u\in\PSH(Y,a\sigma)$. It has analytic singularities only at $p=[1:0:0]$, and has zero generic Lelong number on $Y$. Its trace is exactly the ordinary singularity data of $u$, by \cref{prop:trace-naive-comparison}. There are no divisorial poles on $Y$, so $S_Y=0$ and its canonical ordinary type is smooth. If $\pi:\widetilde Y\to Y$ is the blowup at $p$ with exceptional curve $F$, then
\[
 S_{\widetilde Y}=a[F],\qquad E_\pi=aF.
\]
Indeed on either standard blowup chart the numerator has one exceptional factor of squared modulus and a smooth positive remaining factor. The pole order is $a$ along $F$ and zero along every other prime divisor. Thus a nonzero exceptional difference does not obstruct the existence of an exact trace potential. It obstructs identifying that potential with the canonical type of the divisorial part on the original model.

\Needspace{8\baselineskip}
The same distinction occurs on a manifold failing BMP. Take a compact Hermitian manifold $(W,\omega_W)$ of dimension $k$ failing BMP, such as the Hopf threefold in \cref{thm:hopf-unbounded}, and pull this potential back to $Y\times W$. Its pole set is $\{p\}\times W$, of codimension two, and the modification $\widetilde Y\times W\to Y\times W$ has exceptional coefficient $a$ along $F\times W$. Analytic restriction again gives an exact trace. Let $\operatorname{pr}_Y$ and $\operatorname{pr}_W$ be the two product projections.

\Needspace{9\baselineskip}
Choose smooth $\omega_W$-plurisubharmonic functions $H_j$ whose masses tend to infinity. For the Hermitian form $\Omega=\operatorname{pr}_Y^*\sigma+\operatorname{pr}_W^*\omega_W$, their pullbacks have masses
\[
 \binom{k+2}{2}\left(\int_Y\sigma^2\right)
          \left(\int_W(\omega_W+\ddc H_j)^k\right)\longrightarrow+\infty,
\]
by the smooth binomial expansion and the dimensions of the two factors. Hence the product also fails BMP. The pulled-back potential belongs to $\PSH(Y\times W,C\Omega)$ for every $C\geq\max\{a,1\}$, since its curvature is bounded below by $-a\operatorname{pr}_Y^*\sigma$. Scaling the smooth witnesses by $C$ shows that $C\Omega$ also has unbounded mass.
\end{example}
