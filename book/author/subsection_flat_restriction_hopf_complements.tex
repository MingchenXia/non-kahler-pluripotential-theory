\subsection{Flat restrictions and Hopf complements}
\label{subsec:flat-restriction-hopf-complements}

Let $F\to B$ be a holomorphic bundle of positive rank on a connected compact complex manifold $B$ of positive dimension, and put
\[
 U_F=\mathbb P(\mathcal O_B\oplus F)\setminus\mathbb P(\mathcal O_B).
\]
A flat restriction obstructs strict plurisubharmonicity near the removed zero section. This supplies a lower convexity bound for the Hopf example below.

\Needspace{11\baselineskip}
\begin{lemma}\label{lem:unitary-flat-complement-obstruction}
\index{Unitary flat bundle!complement obstruction}\index{Projective bundle!flat restriction}
Let $B$ be a connected compact complex manifold of positive dimension, and let $F\to B$ be a holomorphic vector bundle of positive rank which admits a Hermitian metric with flat Chern connection. Then $U_F$ is not $1$-convex.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Fix a Hermitian metric on $F$ with flat Chern connection. Suppose that a smooth exhaustion $\psi$ is strictly plurisubharmonic outside a compact set $K\subset U_F$. A sufficiently small punctured metric disc bundle about the removed zero section is disjoint from $K$. Indeed, otherwise a sequence in $K$ with fibre norm tending to zero would accumulate on that section in the compact ambient projective bundle.

Fix a radius $t>0$ inside this disc bundle. On a simply connected coordinate neighbourhood choose a parallel holomorphic unitary frame for the flat Chern connection. Such frames have constant unitary transition matrices. Let $\mathrm d\sigma$ be the rotation-invariant probability measure on the unit sphere in $\mathbb C^{\operatorname{rank}F}$, and put
\[
 A(x)=\int_{|v|=1}\psi([1:tv])\,\mathrm d\sigma(v).
\]
The transitions preserve the sphere and its measure, so $A$ is a global smooth real function on $B$. For each fixed $v$, the map $x\mapsto[1:tv]$ in a parallel frame is holomorphic. Differentiating the compact sphere integral gives a positive definite complex Hessian for $A$, since each horizontal Hessian of $\psi$ is positive definite. This contradicts the nonpositive complex Hessian at a maximum of $A$ on $B$.
\end{proof}

\Needspace{8\baselineskip}
Fix $n\geq2$ and real numbers $0<q,a<1$. On the scalar Hopf manifold
\[
 X_q=(\mathbb C^n\setminus\{0\})/\langle z\mapsto qz\rangle
\]
let $E_a$ be the holomorphic line bundle defined by $(z,v)\sim(qz,av)$. Put
\[
 b=-\frac{\log a}{\log q}<0,
 \qquad G(z,v)=|z|^{2b}|v|^2.
\]
The identity $q^{2b}a^2=1$ makes $G$ the squared norm of a Hermitian metric on $E_a$.

\Needspace{10\baselineskip}
\begin{example}\label{ex:hopf-exact-complement-index}
\index{Hopf manifold!least complement convexity index}\index{Gauduchon degree!de Rham trivial line bundle}\index{$q$-convexity!Hopf complement}
The complement $U_{E_a}$ is $2$-convex but not $1$-convex, so its least smooth convexity index is two. The de Rham class $c_1(E_a)$ vanishes, whereas $\deg_\gamma E_a>0$ for every Gauduchon form $\gamma$ on $X_q$.
\end{example}
\begin{proof}
Write $\alpha=\ddc\log|z|^2$. This form descends because the contraction changes its potential by a constant. For $u\in T_z^{1,0}(\mathbb C^n\setminus\{0\})$, its coefficient form, apart from the positive factor $1/(2\pi)$, is
\[
 \frac{|u|^2}{|z|^2}-\frac{|\langle u,z\rangle|^2}{|z|^4},
\]
where $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle$ is the Euclidean Hermitian inner product. Cauchy--Schwarz shows that it is positive semidefinite with kernel $\mathbb Cz$, hence of rank $n-1$. The normalized line curvature of $G$ is $-b\alpha$. Thus every nonzero fibre vector supplies $n-1$ positive curvature directions. Apply \cref{thm:bundle-curvature-complement-convexity} with $r=1$ and $s=n-1$ to obtain $2$-convexity.

The map $\pi:X_q\to\mathbb P^{n-1}$, $[z]\mapsto[z]$, has compact embedded elliptic fibres $B\simeq\mathbb C^*/q^{\mathbb Z}$. On a lifted fibre $z=\zeta z_0$, the logarithm of the metric coefficient is $b\log|\zeta|^2+b\log|z_0|^2$, which is pluriharmonic. Thus $E_a|_B$ has flat Chern connection. The complement $U_{E_a|_B}$ is a closed complex submanifold of $U_{E_a}$. A $1$-convex exhaustion of the latter would restrict to a proper exhaustion of the former, strictly plurisubharmonic outside the compact intersection of its exceptional set with the submanifold. This contradicts \cref{lem:unitary-flat-complement-obstruction}.

The map $z\mapsto(z/|z|,\log|z|)$ identifies the covering space with $S^{2n-1}\times\mathbb R$, and the contraction translates the second coordinate by $\log q$. Hence $X_q$ is diffeomorphic to $S^{2n-1}\times S^1$ and $H^2_{\mathrm{dR}}(X_q,\mathbb R)=0$. Nevertheless $-b\alpha$ is a nonzero positive semidefinite form at every point, so $(-b\alpha)\wedge\gamma^{n-1}$ is strictly positive. Its integral is $\deg_\gamma E_a>0$.
\end{proof}
