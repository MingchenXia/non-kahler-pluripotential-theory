\chapter{Gauduchon traces and the topology of potentials}
\label{chap:compactness}

The trace of a positive current against a fixed Gauduchon metric is a finite measure that determines its potential up to a constant. The normalized compactness proved in \cref{thm:normalized-compactness} turns this correspondence into a homeomorphism. Elliptic estimates give quantitative comparisons with the $L^1$ distance. The resulting topology still allows the nonlinear Monge--Amp\`ere mass to change discontinuously, as the examples at the end of the chapter show.
