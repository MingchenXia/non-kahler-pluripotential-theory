\chapter{Gauduchon traces and the topology of potentials}
\label{chap:compactness}

Recovering $u$ from the Gauduchon trace of $\theta+\ddc u$ is a scalar elliptic problem with uniqueness up to a constant. Normalized compactness identifies weak convergence of the traces with $L^1$ convergence of the potentials. Testing the elliptic inverse and smoothing the measure tests give quantitative bounds in both directions, with sharp exponents.
