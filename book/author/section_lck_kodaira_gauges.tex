\subsection{Chern--Ricci-flat and Gauduchon gauges on a Kodaira surface}
\label{subsec:lck-kodaira-gauges}
\index{Kodaira surface!flat and Gauduchon gauges}
\index{Vaisman metric!on a Kodaira surface}

The Chern--Ricci-flat and the Gauduchon representatives of a conformal class can differ even when the class contains a Vaisman metric. On a primary Kodaira surface, the standard Vaisman metric can be changed, keeping the de Rham class of the Lee form, into Vaisman metrics whose volume forms are nonconstant multiples of the volume form defined by a holomorphic two-form. The Chern--Ricci-flat representatives of their conformal classes are then not Gauduchon.

Let $G=\mathbb R^4$, with coordinates $(x,y,z,t)$ and the group law
\[
 (x,y,z,t)\cdot(x',y',z',t')=(x+x',\,y+y',\,z+z'+xy',\,t+t').
\]
The integer points form a subgroup $\Gamma=\mathbb Z^4$, which acts on $G$ by left translations
\[
 (x,y,z,t)\longmapsto(x+a,\,y+b,\,z+c+ay,\,t+d),\qquad (a,b,c,d)\in\mathbb Z^4.
\]
This action is free and properly discontinuous. Every orbit meets the unit cube: translate first $x$, $y$ and $t$ into $[0,1]$ by an element with $c=0$, and then $z$ by an element $(0,0,c,0)$. Hence $X=\Gamma\backslash G$ is a compact manifold. The functions
\[
 w_1=x+\mathrm iy,\qquad w_2=z-xy+\mathrm i\bigl(t-\tfrac12y^2\bigr)
\]
identify $G$ with $\mathbb C^2$, and in these coordinates the left translation by $(a,b,c,d)$ is the affine map
\[
 (w_1,w_2)\longmapsto\bigl(w_1+a+\mathrm ib,\;w_2-bw_1+c-ab+\mathrm i(d-\tfrac12b^2)\bigr).
\]
Thus $X$ is a compact complex surface; projecting to $w_1$ exhibits it as an elliptic fiber bundle over the elliptic curve $\mathbb C/(\mathbb Z+\mathrm i\mathbb Z)$, and it is a primary Kodaira surface. The affine maps have Jacobian determinant one, so $\mathrm dw_1\wedge\mathrm dw_2$ descends to a nowhere vanishing holomorphic two-form on $X$.

The left-invariant one-forms
\[
 e^1=\mathrm dx,\qquad e^2=\mathrm dy,\qquad e^3=\mathrm dz-x\,\mathrm dy,\qquad e^4=\mathrm dt
\]
descend to a global coframe on $X$, with $\mathrm de^3=-e^1\wedge e^2$ and $\mathrm de^1=\mathrm de^2=\mathrm de^4=0$. Since
\[
 \mathrm dw_1=e^1+\mathrm ie^2,\qquad
 \mathrm dw_2=e^3+\mathrm ie^4-y\,(e^1+\mathrm ie^2),
\]
the forms $e^1+\mathrm ie^2$ and $e^3+\mathrm ie^4$ span the $(1,0)$-forms. With the notation $J\alpha=-\alpha\circ J$ for real one-forms, this means $Je^1=e^2$ and $Je^3=e^4$. Moreover,
\[
 e^1\wedge e^2\wedge e^3\wedge e^4
 =\bigl(\tfrac{\mathrm i}{2}\bigr)^2\mathrm dw_1\wedge\mathrm d\bar w_1\wedge\mathrm dw_2\wedge\mathrm d\bar w_2.
\]
Consequently, if a Hermitian metric $\omega$ on $X$ satisfies $\omega^2=2F\,e^1\wedge e^2\wedge e^3\wedge e^4$, then $\operatorname{Ric}(\omega)=-\mathrm i\partial\bar\partial\log F$. In particular $c_1^{\mathrm{BC}}(X)=0$, and $\omega$ is Chern--Ricci flat if and only if $F$ is constant, since a pluriharmonic function on $X$ is constant. The Riemannian volume form of $\omega$ is $\omega^2/2=F\,\mathrm dx\wedge\mathrm dy\wedge\mathrm dz\wedge\mathrm dt$.

Let $f$ be a smooth function of $x$ of period one, regarded as a function on $X$, with $f''<1$, and put $C=1-f''$. Define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:lck-kodaira-family}
 \begin{gathered}
 \lambda_f=f'e^1-e^4=\mathrm df-\mathrm dt,\qquad
 J\lambda_f=e^3+f'e^2,\\
 \omega_f=\lambda_f\wedge J\lambda_f+C\,e^1\wedge e^2.
 \end{gathered}
\end{equation}
For $f=0$ this is the left-invariant form $\omega_0=e^1\wedge e^2+e^3\wedge e^4$.

\begin{proposition}\label{prop:lck-kodaira-flat-gauge}
Let $f$, $C$ and $\omega_f$ be as in \eqref{eq:lck-kodaira-family}, and put $h=C^{-1/2}$.
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)},leftmargin=*,itemsep=.2em,topsep=.3em]
\item The form $\omega_f$ is a Vaisman metric with Lee form $\lambda_f$, and $\omega_f^2=2C\,e^1\wedge e^2\wedge e^3\wedge e^4$.
\item Up to a positive constant factor, the only Chern--Ricci-flat metric conformal to $\omega_f$ is $\widehat\omega_f=h\omega_f$. It is strictly LCK, and its Lee form $\widehat\lambda_f=\lambda_f+\mathrm d\log h$ satisfies $\mathrm d^*\widehat\lambda_f=-h''$ with respect to $\widehat\omega_f$.
\item If $f$ is not constant, then $\widehat\omega_f$ is neither Gauduchon nor Vaisman, its Lee field is not holomorphic, and no metric conformal to $\omega_f$ is both Chern--Ricci flat and Gauduchon.
\end{enumerate}
In particular, $\omega_0$ is a Chern--Ricci-flat Vaisman metric on $X$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
(i) Since $\mathrm d\lambda_f=f''e^1\wedge e^1=0$, $\mathrm d(J\lambda_f)=-C\,e^1\wedge e^2$ and $\mathrm d(C\,e^1\wedge e^2)=0$,
\[
 \mathrm d\omega_f=-\lambda_f\wedge\mathrm d(J\lambda_f)
 =C\,\lambda_f\wedge e^1\wedge e^2=\lambda_f\wedge\omega_f.
\]
The one-forms $e^1,e^2,\lambda_f,J\lambda_f$ form a coframe, and $J$ maps $e^1$ to $e^2$ and $\lambda_f$ to $J\lambda_f$. Hence $\omega_f$ is the Hermitian form of the $J$-invariant Riemannian metric
\[
 g_f=\lambda_f^2+(J\lambda_f)^2+C\bigl((e^1)^2+(e^2)^2\bigr),
 \qquad g_f(U,V)=\omega_f(U,JV),
\]
and $\omega_f$ is LCK with Lee form $\lambda_f$. The integral of $\lambda_f$ over the closed curve $t\mapsto(0,0,0,t)$, $0\leq t\leq1$, is $-1$, so $\lambda_f$ is not exact. Since $\lambda_f(\partial_t)=-1$ and $J\lambda_f$, $e^1$, $e^2$ vanish on $\partial_t$, the vector field $\partial_t$ is the $g_f$-dual of $-\lambda_f$. Its flow $(x,y,z,t)\mapsto(x,y,z,t+s)$ commutes with $\Gamma$ and preserves $e^1,\dots,e^4$ and all functions of $x$, hence $g_f$; thus $\partial_t$ is a Killing field. Since $\lambda_f$ is closed, $\nabla\lambda_f$ is symmetric, whereas the Killing equation makes it skew-symmetric. Therefore $\nabla\lambda_f=0$, and $\omega_f$ is Vaisman. Finally, $\omega_f^2=2C\,e^1\wedge e^2\wedge\lambda_f\wedge J\lambda_f=2C\,e^1\wedge e^2\wedge e^3\wedge e^4$.

(ii) For a smooth real function $a$, $(\mathrm e^a\omega_f)^2=2\mathrm e^{2a}C\,e^1\wedge e^2\wedge e^3\wedge e^4$. By the discussion above, $\mathrm e^a\omega_f$ is Chern--Ricci flat if and only if $\mathrm e^{2a}C$ is constant, that is, if and only if $\mathrm e^a$ is a constant multiple of $h$. The Lee form of $\widehat\omega_f=h\omega_f$ is $\widehat\lambda_f=\lambda_f+\mathrm d\log h$, which is cohomologous to $\lambda_f$; hence $\widehat\omega_f$ is strictly LCK.

To compute the divergence, let $E_1=\partial_x$, $E_2=\partial_y+x\partial_z$, $E_3=\partial_z$, $E_4=\partial_t$ be the frame dual to $e^1,\dots,e^4$. Each $E_i$ has divergence zero with respect to $\mathrm dx\wedge\mathrm dy\wedge\mathrm dz\wedge\mathrm dt$, which is the Riemannian volume form $\widehat\omega_f^2/2$ of the metric $\widehat g_f=hg_f$. Evaluating $g_f$ on the coframe gives
\[
 g_f(E_1+f'E_4,\cdot)=C\,e^1,\qquad g_f(E_4,\cdot)=-\lambda_f.
\]
Since $\widehat\lambda_f=\lambda_f+(h'/h)e^1$ and $C^{-1}=h^2$, the $\widehat g_f$-dual of $\widehat\lambda_f$ is
\[
 h^{-1}\bigl(-E_4+hh'(E_1+f'E_4)\bigr)=h'E_1+(f'h'-h^{-1})E_4.
\]
Since $E_4$ annihilates functions of $x$, the divergence of this vector field is $E_1(h')=h''$, and hence $\mathrm d^*\widehat\lambda_f=-h''$.

(iii) Suppose that $h''=0$. Then $h'$ is constant, hence zero since $h$ is periodic. Thus $C=h^{-2}$ is constant; since $f''=1-C$ has integral zero over a period, $f''=0$. Then $f'$ is constant, hence zero since $f$ is periodic, and $f$ is constant. Consequently, if $f$ is not constant, then $\mathrm d^*\widehat\lambda_f\neq0$, and $\widehat\omega_f$ is not Gauduchon by \cref{prop:lck-gauduchon}. By \cref{thm:lck-chern-ricci-rigidity}, its Lee field is then not holomorphic and its Lee form is not parallel. Every Chern--Ricci-flat metric conformal to $\omega_f$ is a constant multiple of $\widehat\omega_f$ by~(ii), and the Gauduchon condition is invariant under multiplication by constants; hence none of these metrics is Gauduchon. For $f=0$ we have $C=1$, so $\omega_0=\widehat\omega_0$ is Chern--Ricci flat and Vaisman by~(i).
\end{proof}

For instance, $f=\cos(2\pi x)/(16\pi^2)$ gives $C=1+\frac14\cos(2\pi x)\geq\frac34$, and $\mathrm d^*\widehat\lambda_f$ equals $-4\sqrt5\pi^2/25$ where $x=0$ and $4\sqrt3\pi^2/9$ where $x=\frac12$.

The Lee forms $\lambda_f$ all lie in the class of $-\mathrm dt$, and $\int_X\omega_f^2=2$ because $f''$ has integral zero over a period. Hence $\omega_0$ is a Vaisman metric with the Lee class of $\omega_f$ and with the Chern--Ricci-flat volume form of total mass $\int_X\omega_f^2$; by the uniqueness in \cref{thm:lck-vaisman-volume}, it is the metric constructed from $\omega_f$ in the proof of \cref{cor:lck-calabi-yau-vaisman-reduction}. For nonconstant $f$, this Chern--Ricci-flat Gauduchon metric lies outside the conformal class of $\omega_f$, as it must by \cref{prop:lck-kodaira-flat-gauge}(iii).
