\begin{proposition}\label{prop:i-envelope-modification}\index{Envelope!pullback by a modification}
Let $\mu:Y\to X$ be a modification of connected compact complex manifolds. For every smooth real $(1,1)$-form $\theta$ on $X$ and every $u\in\PSH(X,\theta)$,
\[
 P_{\mu^*\theta}[u\circ\mu]_{\mathcal I}
       =P_\theta[u]_{\mathcal I}\circ\mu.
\]
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
By \cref{prop:psh-descent-modification}, pullback identifies the potentials in the two backgrounds. Surjectivity of $\mu$ identifies their nonpositivity constraints, and \cref{lem:ideal-equivalence-modification} identifies their all-weight multiplier-ideal classes. Hence pullback gives a bijection of the complete competitor families. The ideal envelope is itself a competitor and is their greatest member by \cref{thm:hermitian-i-projection}. Its pullback is therefore the greatest competitor upstairs, which proves the identity.
\end{proof}
