\begin{proposition}\label{prop:i-envelope-modification}\index{Envelope!pullback by a modification}
Let $\pi\colon Y\to X$ be a modification of connected compact complex manifolds. For every smooth real $(1,1)$-form $\theta$ on $X$ and every $u\in\PSH(X,\theta)$,
\[
 P_{\pi^*\theta}[u\circ\pi]_{\mathcal I}
       =P_\theta[u]_{\mathcal I}\circ\pi.
\]
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Pullback gives a bijection from $\PSH(X,\theta)$ onto $\PSH(Y,\pi^*\theta)$, by \cref{prop:psh-descent-modification}. Since $\pi$ is surjective, pullback preserves and reflects pointwise inequalities, in particular the constraint $v\leq0$, and \cref{lem:ideal-equivalence-modification} shows that it preserves and reflects all-weight multiplier-ideal equivalence. Hence pullback is an order-preserving bijection between the two competitor families. By \cref{thm:hermitian-i-projection}, each ideal envelope is itself a competitor, hence the greatest member of its family. The pullback of $P_\theta[u]_{\mathcal I}$ is therefore the greatest competitor upstairs, which proves the identity.
\end{proof}
