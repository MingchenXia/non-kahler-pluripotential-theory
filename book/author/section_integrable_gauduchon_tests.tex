\section{Gauduchon tests for integrable densities}
\label{sec:integrable-gauduchon-tests}

For a smooth strictly positive probability density, the normalization scalar is an infimum of Gauduchon quotients by \cref{thm:gaud-volume-dual-formula}. We extend this dual formula to the threshold of an arbitrary integrable density. A uniform bound for all Gauduchon tests in a single $L^r$ norm allows the passage from smooth to bounded densities, and monotone truncation then reaches all integrable densities, including those with zero threshold. The formula is most naturally written for the $n$th root of the density.

Recall that a Hermitian form $\gamma$ is \emph{Gauduchon} if $\ddc(\gamma^{n-1})=0$ (\cref{def:gauduchon-metric}); in dimension one every Hermitian form is Gauduchon. For a Gauduchon form $\gamma$ and a nonnegative measurable function $p$, put
\[
 L_{h,\gamma}(p)=
 \frac{\displaystyle\int_Xp(\mathrm{d}V/\gamma^n)^{1/n}\gamma^n}
      {\displaystyle\int_Xh\wedge\gamma^{n-1}}
 =\int_Xp\,a_\gamma\,\mathrm{d}V,
 \qquad
 a_\gamma=\frac{(\gamma^n/\mathrm{d}V)^{(n-1)/n}}{\int_Xh\wedge\gamma^{n-1}}.
\]
Since the coefficient $a_\gamma$ is smooth and strictly positive, each individual test $L_{h,\gamma}$ is finite on $L^n$; the supremum over $\gamma$ may be infinite. In dimension one the test is $\int_Xp\,\mathrm{d}V/\int_Xh$. For real $p\in L^n(\mathrm{d}V)$, define the \emph{root functional}\index{Root functional}
\[
 \rho_h(p)=b_h(|p|^n)^{-1/n}\quad(p\ne0),\qquad\rho_h(0)=0,
\]
with the convention $0^{-1/n}=+\infty$.

\Needspace{18\baselineskip}
\begin{theorem}\label{thm:integrable-gauduchon-formula}\index{Gauduchon duality!integrable density}
For every real $p\in L^n(\mathrm{d}V)$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:integrable-gauduchon-root}
 \rho_h(p)=\sup_{\gamma\text{ Gauduchon}}L_{h,\gamma}(|p|).
\end{equation}
Equivalently, for every nonzero nonnegative $f\in L^1(\mathrm{d}V)$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:integrable-gauduchon-threshold}
 b_h(f)^{1/n}=
 \inf_{\gamma\text{ Gauduchon}}
 \frac{\displaystyle\int_Xh\wedge\gamma^{n-1}}
      {\displaystyle\int_X(f\,\mathrm{d}V/\gamma^n)^{1/n}\gamma^n}.
\end{equation}
Both sides of \eqref{eq:integrable-gauduchon-root} may be $+\infty$, and both sides of \eqref{eq:integrable-gauduchon-threshold} may vanish.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
It suffices to prove \eqref{eq:integrable-gauduchon-root} for $p\geq0$, since both sides depend only on $|p|$; the case $p=0$ is immediate.

\emph{Step 1: smooth strictly positive $p$.} Apply \cref{thm:gaud-volume-dual-formula}, with background $h$, to the probability measure $\mu=p^n\mathrm{d}V/Z$, where $Z=\int_Xp^n\,\mathrm{d}V$. By homogeneity (\cref{lem:bounded-normalizer-subsolution}) its scalar is $c_h(\mu)=Zc_h(p^n)$, while the denominator of each Gauduchon quotient is $Z^{-1/n}\int_Xp(\mathrm{d}V/\gamma^n)^{1/n}\gamma^n$. The factors of $Z$ cancel, and the dual formula becomes
\[
 c_h(p^n)^{-1/n}=\sup_\gamma L_{h,\gamma}(p).
\]
Since $b_h(p^n)=c_h(p^n)$ by \cref{prop:individual-normalizer-lp}, this proves \eqref{eq:integrable-gauduchon-root} for such $p$.

\emph{Step 2: a uniform bound for the tests.} The lower-density estimate of \cref{thm:lp-potential-estimate}, with exponent two, gives a constant $m_h>0$ such that
\[
 c_h(p^n)\|p^n\|_{L^2}\geq m_h
\]
for smooth strictly positive $p$. By Step 1, therefore,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:gauduchon-test-uniform-bound}
 L_{h,\gamma}(p)\leq m_h^{-1/n}\|p\|_{L^{2n}}
 \quad\text{for every }\gamma.
\end{equation}
Approximating nonnegative functions in $L^{2n}$ by smooth strictly positive ones extends this bound to all nonnegative $p\in L^{2n}$. Since $a_\gamma>0$, duality gives $\|a_\gamma\|_{L^{2n/(2n-1)}}\leq m_h^{-1/n}$ for every $\gamma$. In particular, $p\mapsto\sup_\gamma L_{h,\gamma}(p)$ is Lipschitz on the cone of nonnegative functions in $L^{2n}$, with the same constant.

\emph{Step 3: bounded $p$.} Let $p\geq0$ be bounded and nonzero, and approximate it in $L^{2n}$ by smooth strictly positive functions $p_j$. By H\"older's inequality, $p_j^n\to p^n$ in $L^2$, so \cref{lem:lp-normalizer-continuity} gives
\[
 c_h(p_j^n)\longrightarrow c_h(p^n)>0.
\]
By the Lipschitz bound derived from \eqref{eq:gauduchon-test-uniform-bound},
\[
 \sup_\gamma L_{h,\gamma}(p_j)
       \longrightarrow\sup_\gamma L_{h,\gamma}(p).
\]
Passing to the limit in the identity of Step 1 for $p_j$, and using $b_h(p^n)=c_h(p^n)$ (\cref{prop:individual-normalizer-lp}), we obtain \eqref{eq:integrable-gauduchon-root} for bounded $p$.

\emph{Step 4: general $p$.} For nonnegative $p\in L^n$, put $p_k=\min(p,k)$. Then $p_k^n=\min(p^n,k^n)$ are truncations of $p^n$, so the definition of $b_h$ and Step 3 give
\[
 \rho_h(p)=\lim_{k\to\infty}c_h(p_k^n)^{-1/n}
          =\sup_k\sup_\gamma L_{h,\gamma}(p_k).
\]
For each fixed $\gamma$, monotone convergence gives
$\sup_kL_{h,\gamma}(p_k)=L_{h,\gamma}(p)$. Interchanging the two suprema proves \eqref{eq:integrable-gauduchon-root}. Finally, every denominator in \eqref{eq:integrable-gauduchon-threshold} is positive; taking reciprocals in \eqref{eq:integrable-gauduchon-root} with $p=f^{1/n}$ proves \eqref{eq:integrable-gauduchon-threshold}, including the case $b_h(f)=0$.
\end{proof}

For a nonsmooth density we do not claim that the infimum in \eqref{eq:integrable-gauduchon-threshold} is attained by a Gauduchon form; attainment by Gauduchon currents is studied in \cref{sec:integrable-gauduchon-currents}.

For $n\geq2$, the coefficient of the test has norm
\[
 \|a_\gamma\|_{L^{n/(n-1)}}
 =\frac{(\int_X\gamma^n)^{(n-1)/n}}
        {\int_Xh\wedge\gamma^{n-1}}
\]
in the exponent dual to $n$. By \eqref{eq:gaud-lower-mass-formula} in \cref{thm:gaud-volume-mass-extrema}, positive lower mass is therefore equivalent to a uniform bound for these norms, that is, for all tests on $L^n$ simultaneously. Without positive lower mass, Step 2 of the proof still bounds the coefficients uniformly in the weaker norm of $L^{2n/(2n-1)}$, on every compact Hermitian manifold. This suffices to pass from smooth to bounded densities, and monotone truncation then treats all integrable densities.

\Needspace{12\baselineskip}
\begin{proposition}\label{prop:individual-threshold-backgrounds}
Let $f$ be a nonzero nonnegative function in $L^1(\mathrm{d}V)$ and let $p\in L^n(\mathrm{d}V)$ be real. If $h_1,h_2$ are Hermitian forms on $X$ and $m h_1\leq h_2\leq M h_1$ for positive constants $m,M$, then
\[
 m^n b_{h_1}(f)\leq b_{h_2}(f)\leq M^n b_{h_1}(f),
 \qquad
 M^{-1}\rho_{h_1}(p)\leq\rho_{h_2}(p)\leq m^{-1}\rho_{h_1}(p).
\]
If $h_2-h_1=\ddc a$ for a smooth real function $a$, then $b_{h_1}(f)=b_{h_2}(f)$ and $\rho_{h_1}(p)=\rho_{h_2}(p)$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
For every Gauduchon form $\gamma$, the numerators in \eqref{eq:integrable-gauduchon-threshold} satisfy
\[
 m\int_Xh_1\wedge\gamma^{n-1}\leq\int_Xh_2\wedge\gamma^{n-1}
 \leq M\int_Xh_1\wedge\gamma^{n-1},
\]
while the denominators do not depend on the background. Taking the infimum over $\gamma$ and then $n$th powers proves the bounds for the thresholds; taking reciprocal $n$th roots gives the bounds for $\rho$, with the stated conventions at zero and infinity. If $h_2-h_1=\ddc a$, the numerators agree by Stokes' theorem, since $\ddc(\gamma^{n-1})=0$.
\end{proof}

Any two Hermitian metrics on the compact manifold $X$ are comparable. Hence the positivity of $b_h(f)$, and the set of real $p\in L^n$ with $\rho_h(p)<+\infty$, do not depend on the choice of $h$. The dependence on the reference volume is equally simple. If $\mathrm{d}V$ is replaced by $\mathrm{d}V'=\sigma\,\mathrm{d}V$ with a smooth function $\sigma>0$, the measure $f\,\mathrm{d}V$ has density $f'=f/\sigma$ with respect to $\mathrm{d}V'$, and \cref{thm:individual-density-subsolution} shows that the threshold of $f'$ relative to $\mathrm{d}V'$ equals $b_h(f)$. Correspondingly, the map $p\mapsto\sigma^{-1/n}p$ from $L^n(\mathrm{d}V)$ to $L^n(\mathrm{d}V')$ preserves both the $L^n$ norm and the root functional.
