\subsection{Stable extensions approaching the split comparison bundle}
\label{subsec:hirzebruch-stable-extensions}
\index{Hirzebruch surface!stable extensions}

Keep the Hirzebruch surface, ruling and line bundles of
\Cref{subsec:hirzebruch-ample-comparison}. Thus
\[
 L_1=\mathcal O_X(5S-f),\qquad
 L_2=\mathcal O_X(-S+(12e+5)f),\qquad E_0=L_1\oplus L_2.
\]
Use the very ample polarization
\[
 P=4H-f=4S+(12e+3)f,
 \qquad \mu_P(E)=\frac{c_1(E)\cdot P}{\operatorname{rank}E}.
\]
The split bundle is unstable for this polarization. Nonsplit extensions
with the same Chern classes provide stable comparison points. We also
construct a smooth family approaching the split point and check why its
members do not supply ample approximations.

\Needspace{5\baselineskip}
\begin{proposition}\label{prop:hirzebruch-stable-extensions}
\index{Vector bundle!slope-stable extensions}
The extension space has dimension
\[
 \dim\operatorname{Ext}^1_X(L_2,L_1)=105e+35.
\]
Every nonsplit extension
\[
 0\longrightarrow L_1\longrightarrow V\xrightarrow{q}L_2
 \longrightarrow0
\]
is a locally free, $\mu_P$-stable bundle with
$c_1(V)=4H$ and $c_2(V)=13H^2$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Extensions of line bundles are locally free: the quotient is free on a
small trivializing neighbourhood, so the sequence splits there. Their
classes form
\[
 \operatorname{Ext}^1_X(L_2,L_1)
 =H^1(X,\mathcal O_X(6S-(12e+6)f)).
\]
The ruling formula in \cref{lem:hirzebruch-ruling-sections} has zero
higher direct image and gives the seven summands
$\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^1}(-12e-6-ie)$, $0\leq i\leq6$.
Their first cohomology dimensions sum to
\[
 \sum_{i=0}^6(12e+5+ie)=105e+35>0.
\]
The Chern classes follow from the extension sequence. Direct intersection
computations give
\[
 \mu_P(L_1)=40e+11,\qquad \mu_P(V)=40e+14,
 \qquad \mu_P(L_2)=40e+17.
\]

It suffices to test line-bundle subsheaves $M\hookrightarrow V$.
Indeed, a rank-one torsion-free subsheaf on a smooth surface has a line
bundle as its double dual. Its inclusion extends across the finitely
many missing points by Hartogs extension in local bundle frames.
The extended map remains injective and has the same first Chern class.
This argument does not require its quotient to be locally free.

If the composite $M\to L_2$ is zero, $M$ maps nontrivially into $L_1$,
so $\deg_P M\leq\mu_P(L_1)<\mu_P(V)$. Otherwise its zero divisor $D$
is effective and $M=L_2(-D)$. If $D=0$, the composite is an isomorphism
and its inverse splits $q$, contrary to the hypothesis. Hence $D\neq0$,
and
\[
 \deg_P M=\mu_P(V)+3-D\cdot P.
\]
Every effective class is $aS+bf$ with $a,b\geq0$, and
\[
 S\cdot P=8e+3,\qquad f\cdot P=4.
\]
For $e\geq1$ every nonzero effective divisor has $P$-degree at least
four, proving the strict slope inequality.

For $e=0$, the only remaining class with degree at most three is $S$.
A lift of multiplication by a nonzero section
$s\in H^0(X,\mathcal O_X(S))$ from $L_2(-S)$ to $V$ would make the
pullback of the nonzero extension class vanish. That pullback is
\[
 H^1(X,\mathcal O_X(6S-6f))
 \xrightarrow{\ \cdot s\ }H^1(X,\mathcal O_X(7S-6f)).
\]
On $X=\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1$, order the factors so that
$\mathcal O_X(S)=\mathcal O(1,0)$ and $\mathcal O_X(f)=\mathcal O(0,1)$.
The K\"unneth formula identifies this map with
\[
 \bigl[H^0(\mathbb P^1,\mathcal O(6))
 \xrightarrow{\ \cdot s\ }H^0(\mathbb P^1,\mathcal O(7))\bigr]
 \otimes\operatorname{Id}_{H^1(\mathbb P^1,\mathcal O(-6))}.
\]
Multiplication by a nonzero section is injective on global sections;
tensoring with a vector space preserves injectivity. Thus the nonzero
extension class cannot pull back to zero. The possible equality in slope
is excluded, proving stability also for $e=0$.
\end{proof}

\Needspace{5\baselineskip}
\begin{proposition}\label{prop:hirzebruch-extension-prioritary}
\index{Prioritary bundle!extensions}
\index{Vector bundle!negative fibre quotient}
Every extension of $L_2$ by $L_1$, including the split extension, is
prioritary for the ruling and is not ample. Its restriction to every
ruling fibre is $\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^1}(5)\oplus
\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^1}(-1)$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Put $D=K_X+f=-2S-(e+1)f$. The four line bundles
$L_i^*\otimes L_j(D)$ have classes
\[
 D,\quad D,\quad 4S-(13e+7)f,\quad -8S+(11e+5)f.
\]
Each has a negative coefficient in the effective cone, hence no section.
Applying $\operatorname{Hom}_X(L_i,\cdot)$ to the twisted extension gives
$\operatorname{Hom}_X(L_i,V(D))=0$ for $i=1,2$. Applying
$\operatorname{Hom}_X(\cdot,V(D))$ to the original extension then gives
$\operatorname{Hom}_X(V,V(D))=0$. Serre duality identifies this with
$\operatorname{Ext}^2_X(V,V(-f))=0$, the prioritary condition.

Restriction to a ruling fibre is exact because the quotient is locally
free. Its line terms have degrees five and minus one. The restricted
extension splits since
$\operatorname{Ext}^1_{\mathbb P^1}(\mathcal O(-1),\mathcal O(5))
=H^1(\mathbb P^1,\mathcal O(6))=0$.
The negative-degree quotient on this curve excludes ampleness.
\end{proof}

\Needspace{5\baselineskip}
\begin{proposition}\label{prop:hirzebruch-explicit-stable-degeneration}
\index{Dolbeault operator!smooth convergence}
\index{Vector bundle!stable degeneration}
On the fixed smooth bundle underlying $E_0$ there is a holomorphic
one-parameter family of Dolbeault operators
$\bar\partial_t=\bar\partial_0+tA$, $t\in\mathbb C$, such that every
nonzero fibre is $\mu_P$-stable and prioritary, but not ample.
As $t\to0$, these operators converge to $\bar\partial_0$ in the
$C^m$ norm for every integer $m\geq0$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Choose a nonzero extension class and a smooth Dolbeault representative
$\eta\in\Omega^{0,1}(X,L_2^*\otimes L_1)$ with
$\bar\partial\eta=0$. On the smooth direct sum set
\[
 A=\begin{pmatrix}0&\eta\\0&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]
The covariant Dolbeault derivative of $A$ is zero and $A\wedge A=0$.
Thus $\bar\partial_t^2=0$. Adding the ordinary base Dolbeault operator
on $X\times\mathbb C$ also gives an integrable operator because $t$ is
holomorphic. This defines a holomorphic bundle family whose extension
class is $t[\eta]$. It is nonsplit for $t\neq0$, so the two preceding
propositions apply. For a fixed smooth background connection and metrics,
\[
 \|\bar\partial_t-\bar\partial_0\|_{C^m}
 =|t|\,\|A\|_{C^m}\longrightarrow0.
\]
Compactness of $X$ makes the last norm finite for every finite $m$.
\end{proof}

The elementary Hom calculation proves prioritariness for all extensions;
the explicit family also makes the lack of ampleness visible.
Obtaining stable \emph{ample} bundles near $E_0$ requires an additional
deformation argument joining the stable locus to the ample comparison
point of \cref{prop:hirzebruch-ample-comparison}.
