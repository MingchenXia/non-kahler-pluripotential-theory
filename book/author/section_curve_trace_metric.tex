\section{Singularity distance on a curve}

Every positive form on the curve $Y$ is K\"ahler, so the singularity pseudometric $d_{S,\Theta}$ of \cite[Section~6.2]{SGPT} applies there. For ordered potentials it measures the difference of their non-pluripolar masses. Thus the total-variation convergence of $D_m$ gives the distance from $u_m|_Y$ to the atomic trace, even when the ambient manifold $X$ is Hermitian.

\begin{proposition}\label{prop:curve-trace-source-distance}
Choose a smooth positive form $\eta$ of integral one on $Y$ and choose $A>D_u(Y)$ sufficiently large that every restricted approximant $u_m|_Y$ is $A\eta$-plurisubharmonic. Use a fixed nonpositive Green kernel to define $w,w_m$ as above. In the source book's normalization of $d_{S,A\eta}$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:curve-trace-source-distance}
 d_{S,A\eta}(w,u_m|_Y)
 =d_{S,A\eta}(w,w_m)
 =\frac12\bigl(D_u(Y)-D_m(Y)\bigr)
 \longrightarrow0.
\end{equation}
In particular the atomic trace has $\mathcal I$-good singularities in the source sense on the curve. If the ambient manifold is K\"ahler, it represents the $P$-equivalence class of the source book's trace.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The restricted curvature bounds of \cref{cor:nonclosed-analytic-approximation} are dominated by a single $A\eta$, after increasing $A$. Since $D_m\leq D_u$ and $G\leq0$, we have $w\leq w_m$. Also
\[
 A\eta+\ddc w=D_u+(A-D_u(Y))\eta,
 \qquad
 A\eta+\ddc w_m=D_m+(A-D_m(Y))\eta.
\]
Their extended masses are $A-D_u(Y)$ and $A-D_m(Y)$, both positive. For $a,b\in\PSH(Y,A\eta)$ with $a\leq b$, the ordered-distance formula \cite[Lemma~6.2.2]{SGPT} reads
\[
 d_{S,A\eta}(a,b)
 =\frac12\left(\int_Y\mathrm{MA}_{A\eta}(b)
                   -\int_Y\mathrm{MA}_{A\eta}(a)\right).
\]
Substituting $a=w$ and $b=w_m$ proves the second equality in \eqref{eq:curve-trace-source-distance}. Ordinary equivalence of $u_m|_Y$ and $w_m$ gives distance zero between them in the source theory, proving the first equality. Total-variation convergence of $D_m$ proves the limit.

Each $w_m$ has finitely many logarithmic poles with rational coefficients, hence analytic singularities. The positive-mass characterization recalled in \cref{chap:hermitian-igood}, now applied on the K\"ahler curve $Y$, shows that their $d_S$ limit $w$ is $\mathcal I$-good.

Finally suppose $X$ is K\"ahler. The source book supplies a quasi-equisingular approximation with analytic type
$(2^{-j},\mathcal I(2^ju))$. Its restriction to $Y$ has the ordinary type of $w_{2^j}$. The same distance calculation therefore proves that these restrictions converge to $w$ in the source metric. By \cite[Definition~8.1.1 and Proposition~8.1.2]{SGPT}, $w$ therefore represents that trace modulo $P$-equivalence.
\end{proof}
