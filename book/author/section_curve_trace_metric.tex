\section{Singularity distance on a curve}

On the curve $Y$, the K\"ahler singularity pseudometric $d_S$ of \cite[Definition~6.2.1]{SGPT} measures a mass difference for ordered potentials. Applied to the Green representatives, this difference is the missing atomic mass. It gives convergence of the restricted approximants and identifies the topology on ideal envelopes.

\begin{proposition}\label{prop:curve-trace-source-distance}
Choose a smooth positive form $\eta$ of integral one on $Y$ and choose $A>D_u(Y)$ sufficiently large that every restricted approximant $u_m|_Y$ is $A\eta$-plurisubharmonic. Use a fixed nonpositive Green kernel to define $w,w_m$ as above. Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:curve-trace-source-distance}
 d_{S,A\eta}(w,u_m|_Y)
 =d_{S,A\eta}(w,w_m)
 =\frac12\bigl(D_u(Y)-D_m(Y)\bigr)
 \longrightarrow0.
\end{equation}
In particular the atomic trace has $\mathcal I$-good singularities on the curve. If the ambient manifold is K\"ahler, it represents the $P$-equivalence class of the K\"ahler trace.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The restricted curvature bounds of \cref{cor:nonclosed-analytic-approximation} are dominated by a single $A\eta$, after increasing $A$. Since $D_m\leq D_u$ and $G\leq0$, we have $w\leq w_m$. Also
\[
 A\eta+\ddc w=D_u+(A-D_u(Y))\eta,
 \qquad
 A\eta+\ddc w_m=D_m+(A-D_m(Y))\eta.
\]
Their extended masses are $A-D_u(Y)$ and $A-D_m(Y)$, both positive. For $a,b\in\PSH(Y,A\eta)$ with $a\leq b$, the ordered-distance formula \cite[Lemma~6.2.2]{SGPT} reads
\[
 d_{S,A\eta}(a,b)
 =\frac12\left(\int_Y\mathrm{MA}_{A\eta}(b)
                   -\int_Y\mathrm{MA}_{A\eta}(a)\right).
\]
Substituting $a=w$ and $b=w_m$ proves the second equality in \eqref{eq:curve-trace-source-distance}. Ordinary equivalence of $u_m|_Y$ and $w_m$ gives distance zero between them for $d_{S,A\eta}$, proving the first equality. Total-variation convergence of $D_m$ proves the limit.

Each $w_m$ has finitely many logarithmic poles with rational coefficients, hence analytic singularities. The characterization in \cite[Theorem~7.1.1]{SGPT}, now applied on the K\"ahler curve $Y$, shows that their $d_S$ limit $w$ is $\mathcal I$-good.

Finally suppose $X$ is K\"ahler. The source book supplies a quasi-equisingular approximation with analytic type
$(2^{-j},\mathcal I(2^ju))$. Its restriction to $Y$ has the ordinary type of $w_{2^j}$. The same distance calculation therefore proves that these restrictions converge to $w$ for $d_{S,A\eta}$. By \cite[Definition~8.1.1 and Proposition~8.1.2]{SGPT}, $w$ therefore represents that trace modulo $P$-equivalence.
\end{proof}
