\section{Singularity distance on a curve}

On the curve $Y$, the singularity pseudometric $d_S$ of \cite[Definition~6.2.1]{SGPT} measures the difference of the masses of two ordered potentials. For the Green representatives $w$ and $w_m$, it is half the atomic mass missing from $D_m$. When $X$ is K\"ahler, this identifies the atomic trace with the K\"ahler trace of \cite[Section~8.1]{SGPT}, up to $P$-equivalence, that is, up to potentials with the same singularity envelope; the distance calculation itself uses only the geometry of the curve.

\begin{proposition}\label{prop:curve-trace-source-distance}
Choose a smooth positive form $\eta$ of integral one on $Y$ and choose $A>D_u(Y)$ so large that every restricted approximant $u_m|_Y$ is $A\eta$-plurisubharmonic. Define $w$ and $w_m$ as before \cref{prop:curve-trace-approximation}, with this form $\eta$ and a fixed nonpositive Green kernel. Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:curve-trace-source-distance}
 d_{S,A\eta}(w,u_m|_Y)
 =d_{S,A\eta}(w,w_m)
 =\frac12\bigl(D_u(Y)-D_m(Y)\bigr)
 \longrightarrow0.
\end{equation}
In particular, the atomic trace has $\mathcal I$-good singularities on the curve, in the sense recalled in \cref{chap:hermitian-igood}. If $X$ is K\"ahler, the atomic trace represents the $P$-equivalence class of the K\"ahler trace.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The curvature bounds of \cref{cor:nonclosed-analytic-approximation}, restricted to $Y$, are dominated by a single form $A\eta$ once $A$ is large. Since $D_m\leq D_u$ and $G\leq0$, we have $w\leq w_m$. Also
\[
 A\eta+\ddc w=D_u+(A-D_u(Y))\eta,
 \qquad
 A\eta+\ddc w_m=D_m+(A-D_m(Y))\eta.
\]
Hence their extended masses are $A-D_u(Y)$ and $A-D_m(Y)$, both positive. For $a,b\in\PSH(Y,A\eta)$ with $a\leq b$, the formula for the distance of ordered potentials \cite[Lemma~6.2.2]{SGPT} reads
\[
 d_{S,A\eta}(a,b)
 =\frac12\left(\int_Y\mathrm{MA}_{A\eta}(b)
                   -\int_Y\mathrm{MA}_{A\eta}(a)\right).
\]
Substituting $a=w$ and $b=w_m$ proves the second equality in \eqref{eq:curve-trace-source-distance}. Since $u_m|_Y$ and $w_m$ are ordinarily equivalent, their distance vanishes, which proves the first equality. The convergence of $D_m$ in total variation proves the limit.

Each $w_m$ has finitely many logarithmic poles with rational coefficients, hence analytic singularities. The characterization in \cite[Theorem~7.1.1]{SGPT}, applied on the compact K\"ahler curve $Y$, shows that their $d_S$-limit $w$ is $\mathcal I$-good.

Finally, suppose that $X$ is K\"ahler. The K\"ahler trace is defined in \cite[Definition~8.1.1 and Proposition~8.1.2]{SGPT} through a quasi-equisingular approximation of $u$ with analytic types
$(2^{-j},\mathcal I(2^ju))$, whose restriction to $Y$ has the ordinary type of $w_{2^j}$. The same distance calculation shows that these restrictions converge to $w$ for $d_{S,A\eta}$, so $w$ represents the K\"ahler trace up to $P$-equivalence.
\end{proof}
