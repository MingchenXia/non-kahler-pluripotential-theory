\subsection{Cluster sets at a bounded background}
\label{subsec:bounded-background-cluster-sets}

Let $\Omega\subset\mathbb C^n$ be a domain, $n\geq1$, and let $b$ be a locally bounded plurisubharmonic function on $\Omega$. If potentials approach $b$ from below in local capacity, every limit of their Monge--Amp\`ere measures must dominate $(\ddc b)^n$. Truncation at a fixed distance below $b$ provides a common bound, and plurifine locality keeps the measure outside the small set where the truncation changes the potential.

\begin{lemma}\label{lem:capacity-limit-measure-floor}\index{Monge--Ampere product@Monge--Amp\`ere product!capacity limit order}
Let $b,u_j$ be locally bounded plurisubharmonic functions on $\Omega$ with $u_j\leq b$ and $u_j\to b$ in local capacity. Then, for every nonnegative $f\in C_c(\Omega)$,
\[
 \liminf_{j\to\infty}\int_\Omega f(\ddc u_j)^n
      \geq\int_\Omega f(\ddc b)^n.
\]
In particular, every vague subsequential limit of $(\ddc u_j)^n$ dominates $(\ddc b)^n$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
First suppose that $f$ is supported in $B'$, where $B'\Subset B\Subset\Omega$ are concentric balls. Put $v_j=\max(u_j,b-1)$ and $E_j=\{u_j\leq b-1\}$. The functions $v_j$ lie between $b-1$ and $b$ and tend to $b$ in local capacity. Because of their common bounds, the continuity of bounded products recalled in \cref{sec:psh-local-capacity} gives $(\ddc v_j)^n\to(\ddc b)^n$ vaguely on $B$. With $R=\sup_B b-\inf_B b+1$, the capacity bound for bounded potentials gives
\[
 (\ddc v_j)^n(E_j\cap\operatorname{supp}f)
 \leq R^n\operatorname{cap}(E_j\cap\operatorname{supp}f,B)
 \longrightarrow0,
\]
where the limit holds because $E_j\subset\{|u_j-b|>1/2\}$. On the plurifine open Borel set $\{u_j>b-1\}$ we have $v_j=u_j$, so \cref{thm:bt-input} shows that the two measures agree there. By positivity,
\[
 \int f(\ddc u_j)^n
 \geq\int f(\ddc v_j)^n
       -\|f\|_\infty(\ddc v_j)^n(E_j\cap\operatorname{supp}f).
\]
Taking the lower limit proves the assertion for such $f$. For a general $f$, use a finite nonnegative partition of unity subordinate to a cover of its support by such balls. Applying the inequality along a vaguely convergent subsequence proves the final assertion.
\end{proof}

\Needspace{17\baselineskip}
\begin{theorem}\label{thm:bounded-background-cluster-sets}\index{Monge--Ampere product@Monge--Amp\`ere product!cluster sets at a bounded background}
Assume that $\Omega$ is bounded, $n\geq2$, and $b\in\PSH(\Omega)\cap L^\infty(\Omega)$. For a set $F\subset\mathcal M_+(\Omega)$, the following are equivalent:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $F$ is vaguely closed and every $\mu\in F$ satisfies $\mu\geq(\ddc b)^n$;
\item there are bounded plurisubharmonic functions $u_j\leq b$ on $\Omega$, converging to $b$ in $L^p(\Omega)$ for every $1\leq p<\infty$ and in local capacity, whose full vague cluster set is $F$.
\end{enumerate}
The empty set is allowed. If $b$ is smooth, every $u_j$ in the second condition can be chosen smooth.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If the second condition holds, \cref{lem:capacity-limit-measure-floor} gives the measure inequality, and the full cluster set is closed in the metrizable vague topology by the argument at the beginning of the proof of \cref{thm:zero-local-cluster-sets}.

Conversely, assume the first condition. If $F$ is nonempty, the concatenation of a dense sequence used in the proof of \cref{thm:zero-local-cluster-sets} gives measures $\nu_j\in F$ whose full vague cluster set is $F$. If $F$ is empty, take $\nu_j=(\ddc b)^n+j\delta_a$ for a fixed $a\in\Omega$. A nonnegative compactly supported test function equal to one at $a$ has integral at least $j$ against $\nu_j$, so this sequence has no vaguely convergent subsequence. In either case, $\nu_j-(\ddc b)^n$ is a positive Radon measure.

Let $(f_i)$ and $d_v$ be as in \cref{lem:local-vague-metric}. For each $j$, approximate the excess $\nu_j-(\ddc b)^n$ against $f_1,\ldots,f_j$ by a positive finite atomic measure, with each error less than $1/j$. If this measure has $N_j>0$ atoms, perform the construction from the proof of \cref{thm:local-prescribed-excess}, starting at $b$ and using $\tau=t=(jN_j)^{-1}$ for each atom; if it has none, take $u_j=b$. Summing the estimates of \cref{prop:local-one-atom-addition} gives
\[
 \|u_j-b\|_{L^p(\Omega)}\leq C_{\Omega,p}/j,
 \qquad
 \left|\int_\Omega f_i\bigl((\ddc u_j)^n-\nu_j\bigr)\right|
       <2/j\quad(i\leq j).
\]
For every fixed pair of concentric balls $B'\Subset B\Subset\Omega$, the same construction bounds the integral of $|u_j-b|$ over $B'$ against $(\ddc w)^n$ by $C_{B',B,\Omega}/j$, for every $w\in\PSH(B)$ with $0\leq w\leq1$. Dividing by a fixed positive threshold and taking the supremum over $w$ gives convergence in local capacity. Each $u_j$ is bounded and lies below $b$, and it is smooth when $b$ is smooth. One sequence serves all finite $p$, and no common local mass bound on the $\nu_j$ is needed.

The error in the first $j$ coordinates and the tail of the series yield
\[
 d_v\bigl((\ddc u_j)^n,\nu_j\bigr)\leq 2/j+2^{-j}\longrightarrow0.
\]
By the remark following \cref{lem:local-vague-metric}, the two sequences of measures have the same limits along corresponding subsequences. Hence their full vague cluster sets coincide.
\end{proof}

For example, on the unit ball the function $b=|z|^2-1$ has nonzero Monge--Amp\`ere measure. For every point $a$ of the ball, the theorem allows the cluster sets $\{(\ddc b)^n\}$, $\{(\ddc b)^n+\delta_a\}$ and the empty set, but it excludes $\{0\}$; this exclusion relies on the convergence in local capacity. In dimension one, $L^1$ convergence alone forces $\ddc u_j\to\ddc b$ vaguely, so the full cluster set is always $\{\ddc b\}$.
