Ideal inclusions give order and scaling directly. For addition, subadditivity gives one inequality; an analytic approximant to the sum controls sufficiently fine approximants to both summands and gives the reverse inequality.

\Needspace{14\baselineskip}
\begin{proposition}\label{prop:trace-profile-calculus}\label{thm:trace-data-additivity}
Let $v\in\QPSH(X)$ also have zero generic Lelong number along $Y$. Then, pointwise on divisors over $Y$,
\begin{enumerate}
\item $u\preceq_{\mathcal I}v$ implies $\tau_{Y,u}\geq\tau_{Y,v}$;
\item $\tau_{Y,au}=a\tau_{Y,u}$ for every $a>0$;
\item $\tau_{Y,u+v}=\tau_{Y,u}+\tau_{Y,v}$;
\item $\tau_{Y,\max(u,v)}\leq\min\{\tau_{Y,u},\tau_{Y,v}\}$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The ideal inclusion defining $\preceq_{\mathcal I}$ reverses vanishing orders after restriction. Replacing $t$ by $at$ in \eqref{eq:trace-profile} proves homogeneity. Moreover,
\[
 \mathcal I(tu)+\mathcal I(tv)\subseteq\mathcal I(t\max(u,v)).
\]
The order of a sum of ideals is the minimum of their orders; restriction and passage to the limit prove (4). Generic Lelong numbers of sums add, so $u+v$ still has zero generic Lelong number along $Y$.

Subadditivity of multiplier ideals gives $\tau_{Y,u+v}\geq\tau_{Y,u}+\tau_{Y,v}$. For the reverse inequality, fix a sufficiently large integer $k$ and an analytic approximant $S_k$ to $u+v$, of type $(1/k,\mathcal I(k(u+v)))$. We have $u+v\leq S_k$ by \cref{thm:demailly-hermitian-input}. Let $U_m,V_m$ be the corresponding approximants to $u,v$. The analytic comparison in \cref{lem:analytic-minorant-product-ideals} gives
\[
 U_m+V_m\leq(1-\delta_m)S_k+O(1),\qquad\delta_m\downarrow0.
\]
Both $\delta_m$ and the bounded error may depend on the fixed $k$. All three analytic restrictions are genuine by \cref{cor:restricted-analytic-divisorial-bound}. Restrict, pull back to a fixed modification, and take the generic Lelong number along its divisor $E$:
\[
 \frac{\operatorname{ord}_E\mathfrak a_m(u;Y)}m
 +\frac{\operatorname{ord}_E\mathfrak a_m(v;Y)}m
 \geq(1-\delta_m)
       \frac{\operatorname{ord}_E\mathfrak a_k(u+v;Y)}k.
\]
First let $m\to\infty$, with $k$ fixed, and then let $k\to\infty$. The finite limits in \cref{thm:intrinsic-trace-profile} give the reverse inequality.
\end{proof}
