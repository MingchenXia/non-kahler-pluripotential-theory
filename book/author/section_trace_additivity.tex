\section{Addition of the intrinsic trace data}

Let $X$ be a connected compact complex manifold and let $Y\subset X$ be a connected compact complex submanifold of positive dimension. Write $\QPSH$ for quasi-plurisubharmonic functions and $\nu(u,Y)=\inf_{y\in Y}\nu(u,y)$, with $\nu(u,y)$ the Lelong number. Fix $u\in\QPSH(X)$ with $\nu(u,Y)=0$. When needed choose a smooth real $(1,1)$-form $\theta$ on $X$ with $u\in\PSH(X,\theta)$. For real $t>0$, use $\mathcal I(tu)$ for the multiplier ideal of $\mathrm{e}^{-tu}$ and $\mathfrak a_t(u;Y)=\mathcal I(tu)\mathcal O_Y$ for its image ideal on $Y$. A divisor $E$ over $Y$ lies on a smooth compact modification $\pi:Z\to Y$, and $\operatorname{ord}_E$ denotes its generic vanishing order. The trace number $\tau_{Y,u}(E)$ is the limit in \eqref{eq:trace-profile}. Write $a\preceq b$ if $a\leq b+C$ for some constant $C$, $a\simeq b$ if both inequalities hold, and $a\preceq_{\mathcal I}b$ if $\mathcal I(ta)\subseteq\mathcal I(tb)$ for all $t>0$.

The addition inequality can be strengthened to equality. We apply the local approximation comparison \cref{lem:analytic-minorant-product-ideals}. No global closed background is needed.

\Needspace{8\baselineskip}
\begin{theorem}\label{thm:trace-data-additivity}
Let $u,v\in\QPSH(X)$ have zero generic Lelong number along the connected compact submanifold $Y$. Then, on every divisor over $Y$,
\[
 \tau_{Y,u+v}=\tau_{Y,u}+\tau_{Y,v}.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The inequality $\geq$ was proved in \cref{prop:trace-profile-calculus}. Fix a sufficiently large integer $k$ and let $S_k$ be the global analytic approximant to $u+v$, of type $(1/k,\mathcal I(k(u+v)))$. By \cref{thm:demailly-hermitian-input}, $u+v\leq S_k$. Apply \cref{lem:analytic-minorant-product-ideals} with $s=S_k$ to the global analytic approximants $U_m,V_m$ of $u,v$, respectively, with types $(1/m,\mathcal I(mu))$ and $(1/m,\mathcal I(mv))$. Their restrictions and that of $S_k$ are genuine because all three original potentials have zero generic Lelong number along $Y$.

Restrict the resulting ordinary inequality to $Y$, pull back to any fixed smooth compact modification, and take the generic Lelong number along its divisor $E$. This gives
\[
 \frac{\operatorname{ord}_E\mathfrak a_m(u;Y)}m
 +\frac{\operatorname{ord}_E\mathfrak a_m(v;Y)}m
 \geq(1-\delta_m)
       \frac{\operatorname{ord}_E\mathfrak a_k(u+v;Y)}k.
\]
Let $m\to\infty$. Finiteness and the limit formula in \cref{thm:intrinsic-trace-profile} yield
\[
 \tau_{Y,u}(E)+\tau_{Y,v}(E)
 \geq\frac{\operatorname{ord}_E\mathfrak a_k(u+v;Y)}k.
\]
Now let $k\to\infty$. This proves the opposite inequality and hence equality. No convergence of Monge--Amp\`ere masses or of a singularity metric enters the argument.
\end{proof}

When $Y$ is a curve, write $D_a=\sum_{y\in Y}\tau_{Y,a}(y)\delta_y$ for the atomic trace measure of a potential $a$ and $\operatorname{Tr}^{\mathrm{at}}_Y(a)$ for its Green ordinary type, as in \cref{sec:curve-trace-realization}. The theorem identifies the atomic measures themselves:
$D_{u+v}=D_u+D_v$. With one fixed nonpositive Green kernel the corresponding Green potentials add, by integration of positive measures. The atomic trace therefore satisfies
\[
 \operatorname{Tr}^{\mathrm{at}}_Y(u+v)
 \simeq\operatorname{Tr}^{\mathrm{at}}_Y(u)
       +\operatorname{Tr}^{\mathrm{at}}_Y(v).
\]
Here the statement is about ordinary singularity types; its Green representatives and their independence were established above. Positive real homogeneity follows in exactly the same way from homogeneity of the trace data. The inequality for maxima in \cref{prop:trace-profile-calculus} remains an inequality; this proof supplies no corresponding equality for maxima.
