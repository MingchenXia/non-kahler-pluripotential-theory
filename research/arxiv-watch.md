## Candidates and actual review scope

- [Bergman Kernel Asymptotics for Semipositive Line Bundles near Curvature-Degenerate Points](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24869) — Yueh-Lin Chiang. Statements checked: Definition I.1 and Theorems I.2, I.8, I.10; complete proof not audited. Chapter 27: finite-order curvature degeneracy and partial kernels. The concrete adjoint theorem uses a complete Kähler manifold; general Hermitian localization/expansion requires localized mild and model spectral gaps. Fixed-jet consequences are explored independently in the working note; degree-dependent ideal selection remains separate.

- [The $J$-Null Locus and Local Regularity of Weak Solutions of the Semistable $J$-Equation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26340) — Hao Fang , Biao Ma. Abstract checked; theorem hypotheses and complete proof pending. Chapters 28 and 33: proposed J-equation singular locus and local regularity. Investigate equation-dependent loci, without importing a Kähler J-equation result as a general Hermitian Monge–Ampère theorem.

- [Bimeromorphic invariance of $\partial\bar\partial$-property and blow-up formulae I: counterexamples and derived category approach](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23469) — Wei Liu , Sheng Rao. Abstract checked; complete proof and dependencies pending. Chapters 23, 28, 29 and the appendix: the authors announce failures of inheritance/bimeromorphic invariance of the ddbar property. Check any use of this property under restrictions or modifications. No conclusion from this preprint is a book premise; the Popovici audit requirement remains in force.

- [Parameter Dependence of Weighted Bergman Kernels Beyond Smoothness](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24258) — George Marinescu , Xu Xing. Abstract checked; complete proof pending. Chapter 27: parameter variation of weighted Bergman kernels on a fixed bounded pseudoconvex domain with C2 boundary. This fixed-domain Hilbert-space problem does not directly address jumping multiplier ideals or arbitrary compact Hermitian backgrounds.

- [Pogorelov interior estimates and a Liouville theorem for the complex Monge--Ampère equation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18265) — Hongyu Chen , Jingchen Hu , Li Sheng. Abstract checked; complete proof pending. Chapter 13: interior complex Monge–Ampère estimates. The stated estimate has an additional Hessian inequality, and its Liouville consequence assumes convexity (globally or outside a compact set). It does not remove the book interior theorem’s Holder premise for general weak solutions.

- [Weighted regularity and plurithinness in $\mathbb{C}^n$](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23530) — Nurbek Kh. Narzillaev. Abstract checked; complete proof pending. Chapters 12 and 27: weighted extremal regularity, parameter derivatives and counterexamples to preservation of weighted regularity. Check the fixed Euclidean/logarithmic-growth setting before comparing with singular compact envelopes.

- [A first-jet characterization of Griffiths positivity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26528) — Yun-Heng Du , Song-Yan Xie. Abstract checked; complete proof pending. Chapters 20 and 26: first jets and a matrix-measure dual obstruction for Griffiths positivity on smooth projective varieties. Possible analogy with scalar Gauduchon duality, not an established vector-bundle extension of this book.

- [Compact Kähler surfaces with semipositive anticanonical bundle](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26716) — Yifan Chen , Simion Filip , Song Sun , Valentino Tosatti , Junsheng Zhang. Primary title/authors/version metadata checked; detailed statement and proof review pending. Chapter 28 and non-Kähler geometry comparisons: compact Kähler surfaces with semipositive anticanonical bundle. Retain as a reading candidate; no theorem adopted.
