Metric/Levi calculation. For F=E*, a Hermitian form h determines G_h(v)=v*H(x)v. Its vertical Levi block is H, so strict Levi positivity off the zero section forces h to be a positive metric. At any point a holomorphic frame makes H=I and all first derivatives zero. In that frame the Levi value at (u,w) is |w|^2+v*H_{u,ubar}v=|w|^2-h(R_h(u,ubar)v,v), with the existing book Chern sign. Thus strict Levi positivity for every nonzero v and total-space tangent vector is equivalent to Griffiths negativity of h; the dual curvature changes sign/transposes and gives Griffiths positivity on E. This reconstructs the source's cited DDF correspondence without adopting its separate primary proof tree.

Compact test space. Fix a smooth positive metric k on F and b on the total-space tangent bundle restricted to the unit fiber sphere S_kF. The space K={(v,xi):k(v,v)=1,b(xi,xi)=1} is compact and metrizable. Fiber dilation by a nonzero constant c has G_h(cv)=|c|^2 G_h(v), and its differential sends (u,w) to(u,cw). Its Levi pullback has the same positive scaling. Therefore positivity on K tests all nonzero fiber points and tangent vectors. Local derivatives up to order two give ||D h||_C0<=C||h||_C2 for D h=Levi(G_h)|S_kF, with finitely many base charts.

First jets. Put V_m=H0(E tensor A^m), N_m=dim V_m, and use local coefficient columns p_m of evaluations against v. If the fixed A metric has weight phi, G_{m,B}=e^(-m phi)p_m*B p_m. Frame change by gamma gives p'_m=gamma^(-m)p_m and phi'=phi-log|gamma|^2, so the form is global. For xi=(u,w), put r_m=dp_m(xi)-m phi_u p_m and kappa=phi_{u,ubar}. Differentiation gives Psi_m=e^(-m phi)(r_m r_m*-m kappa p_m p_m*) and Levi G_{m,B}=tr(B Psi_m). For rho=sum|t_alpha|^2 and an orthonormal A-section basis, phi=log rho, phi_u=t*dt(u)/rho and kappa=(rho|dt(u)|^2-|t*dt(u)|^2)/rho^2. These are first-jet formulas. Under frame change r'_m=gamma^(-m)r_m and kappa'=kappa; basis congruence also preserves the zero-in-convex-hull condition.

Finite alternative. Herm(N_m) has real dimension N_m^2. Caratheodory gives at most N_m^2+1 terms for every convex-hull point and makes that hull compact as a continuous image of a compact simplex product. If zero belongs, the associated atomic probability annihilates every trace functional. If zero does not belong, the closest hull point Z0 has ||Z0||>0, and differentiating squared distance on the segment to any Z gives tr(Z0 Z)>=||Z0||^2. Thus B=Z0 is a strict separator. This directly proves the finite separation step and avoids an additional external separation premise.

Nestedness. The fixed FS identity sum e^(-phi)|t_alpha|^2=1 gives G_{m,B}=sum e^(-(m+1)phi)(p_m t_alpha)*B(p_m t_alpha). Every product section has coefficients C_alpha in the complete V_{m+1} basis, so B'=sum C_alpha* B C_alpha gives exactly the same form. Hence H_m is contained in H_{m+1}, without requiring multiplication of sections to be surjective.

The original very-ample projective map is an embedding, and the projector map is injective with injective differential on projective tangent directions. A C2 scalar function on its compact image extends to a C2 function on a surrounding real cube: local graph coordinates extend it constantly in normal coordinates, and a finite smooth ambient partition of unity patches these extensions. For F in C2([0,1]^d), the multivariate Bernstein polynomial is B_N F(y)=sum_{0<=j_l<=N}F(j/N) product_l binomial(N,j_l)y_l^j_l(1-y_l)^(N-j_l). Direct bundle density. In local frames let L=e^(-m0 phi/2)P0 be the N_0 by rank(E) normalized evaluation matrix. Global generation makes M=L*L positive invertible. For any C2 Hermitian coefficient matrix H on F, set B(x)=L M^(-1) H M^(-1)L*. Then L* B L=H. A change of F frame cancels in this expression, and a change of A frame multiplies L by a scalar phase which cancels as well. Thus B is a global C2 Herm(N_0)-valued function. Approximate its real Hermitian coordinate functions in C2 by polynomials in Y, retaining Hermitian symmetry. The resulting forms L*B_N(Y)L converge to h in C2.

A polynomial of maximum degree q in Y has terms t^I conjugate(t^J)/rho^k with |I|=|J|=k<=q. Multiply numerator/denominator by rho^(q-k) and expand the latter as sums of products. Combined with e^(-m0 phi)p0*B p0, every term is a constant coefficient pairing of product sections sigma_i t^I of E tensor A^(m0+q), divided by rho^(m0+q). No normal generation or surjectivity of a multiplication map is needed. Consequently the union of all H_m is C2-dense in all Hermitian forms on F.
