# Divisorial constraints on threshold Gauduchon tests

22 September2026. Unaudited research note; not book content. This uses the proposed trace-saturation proposition and standard divisor-current calculus. It is not covered by either root-tail or trace audit.

Let h be Hermitian, f>=0 nonzero in L1, b_h(f)>0, and u a threshold subsolution. Suppose the positive current h+dd^c u dominates a[D] for some effective divisor D and a>0. Let beta_D be a smooth Chern representative of {D}, so [D]=beta_D+dd^c ell_D with ell_D locally integrable. Let T_j be smooth strictly positive, dd^c-closed (n-1,n-1)-forms with integral h wedge T_j=1, maximizing the density-root test.

The error R_{u,T_j} is positive and its total mass tends to zero. The density-root term is absolutely continuous with respect to smooth volume, so it gives D no mass. Thus

    0 <= a integral_D T_j
       <= R_{u,T_j}(D) <= R_{u,T_j}(X) -> 0.

Distributional Stokes identifies integral_D T_j with integral_X beta_D wedge T_j. Every weak current cluster T therefore satisfies integral_X beta_D wedge T=0. This conclusion needs no root-tail condition: the pairing with beta_D is continuous on the compact current set. If f^(1/n) belongs to D_h^0, the established current-attainment mechanism additionally makes T a maximizing current. This places it on a boundary hyperplane of the Gauduchon current cone determined by the Bott-Chern divisor class.

A possible consequence is that the class of D cannot be big. Indeed, if beta_D+dd^c psi>=epsilon h for a genuine potential psi and epsilon>0, distributional Stokes against each smooth T_j gives integral beta_D wedge T_j>=epsilon, a contradiction. In particular no threshold subsolution can have a positive divisorial component along a big effective divisor. This statement should be checked carefully as a special claim about the maximal global threshold, not about solutions at an arbitrary smaller constant or in a prescribed singularity type.

More generally, for a finite collection of positive divisorial components, all their smooth Chern pairings vanish on every maximizing-sequence cluster. This does not identify an intersection product [D] wedge T, and it does not compute the null locus or non-Kähler locus. It also does not prove that any Gauduchon current with those vanishing pairings is realized by an equation.

Potential direction: compare these necessary faces of the Gauduchon cone with the residual-divisor volume and Bott-Chern locus chapters. A geometric sufficiency or realization theorem is missing. Determine whether explicit Hopf/elliptic examples realize a singular maximizing current annihilating a nonbig effective divisor. No example or theorem of realization is currently claimed.
