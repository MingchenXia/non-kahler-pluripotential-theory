## 2. Mathematical corrections made
- **proper_approximation.tex:35–43 (rigour repair, listed prominently).** Old: the trace estimate "applies with prescribed potential $v=0$ … and $h(x,v)=tH(x)$; its constants are uniform for $t\in[0,1]$". The function $h$ varied with $t$, but `hessian:prescribed-potential-trace` gives constants depending on $h$, so the claimed uniformity needed an inspection of its proof. New: use the fixed $h(x,\tau)=\tau H(x)$, the constant prescribed potential $v=t$, and constant comparison functions $\varphi=t_i$. Finitely many $t_i$ cover $[0,1]$ at tolerance $\varepsilon$, so uniformity follows from the statement itself. I also added $\|w_t\|_\infty\le\|H\|_\infty$, from the bounds just proved, to supply $M$.
- **proper_approximation.tex:41.** Old: `hessian:hessian-trace-to-gradient` "gives a uniform gradient and complex-Hessian bound". That proposition gives only the gradient bound. New: cite `hessian:prescribed-potential-complex-hessian`, which combines it with the trace estimate.
- **proper_approximation.tex:112–117 and bounded_approximation.tex:76–82 (wrong justification).** Old: "polarized Gårding positivity yields $(N+1)d_N\ge\omega\wedge T_N/\omega^n\ge d_N^{(m-1)/m}$". New wording:
  - In the continuous case, the first inequality comes from the maximum principle and the identity $\omega+(1+N^{-1})\ddc w_N=(1+N^{-1})(\omega+\ddc w_N)-N^{-1}\omega$.
  - In the bounded case, the first inequality comes from the upper-test condition plus polarized positivity.
  - In both cases, the second inequality is Maclaurin's inequality $\sigma_{m-1}\ge\sigma_m^{(m-1)/m}$, since the middle term is $\sigma_{m-1}$ of the eigenvalues of $\omega+\ddc w_N$.
- **bounded_approximation.tex:31.** The display used $c_j+\tfrac34\delta_j\le h_j$ but stated only $u+\tfrac34\delta_j\le h_j$. It now states the chain $u+\tfrac34\delta_j\le c_j+\tfrac34\delta_j\le h_j$.
