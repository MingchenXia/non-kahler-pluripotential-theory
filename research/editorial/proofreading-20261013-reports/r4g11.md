## 2. Mathematical corrections made
- sobolev:69–73 (proof of `hessian:sobolev-capacity-tools`). Old: "Positive local mollification and a partition of unity give … $f_\epsilon\geq1$ on $K$". New: mollify $f$ in charts, then glue with a fixed partition of unity, which gives $f_\epsilon=1$ on $K$. Why: if one mollifies $\chi_if$ instead, the result is only $\sum_i\chi_i*\rho_\epsilon$ on $K$, which need not be $\geq1$. The new order makes the claim exact.
- sobolev:79–80. Old: "Outer regularity and open supersets give it for every Borel $E$". New: monotonicity $\mu(E)\leq\mu(G)$ for every open $G\supset E$. Why: outer regularity is not the reason. The capacity of $E$ is defined by open supersets.
- locality:95–98. Added the definition $H_m(v)=Q_m(v,\ldots,v)$ for bounded admissible $v$ at its first bounded use, pointing to `hessian:bounded-shifted-positive` and to the continuous $H_m$ of `sec:hessian-continuous-products` (the coordinator's note). Before this, $H_m$ was defined only for continuous potentials.
- locality:186–188 (`hessian:bounded-nonvanishing`). Made explicit that $\int_XH_m(u)=0$ forces $H_m(u)=0$ by positivity. Also made explicit that the limit identity uses $\mu_m=0$, since the left side tends to $\int_X(\mu_q-\mu_m)=\int_X\mu_q$.
- locality:45–47. Old: "Choose the cutoffs so that $\chi_\delta\to\mathbf1_{\{s>0\}}$". New: this holds for every such family. No choice is needed.
