# Lee--Gauduchon invariance under modifications

## Proof check

For a proper analytic subset of a connected complex manifold, general position moves one-cycles away from the subset. Thus restriction in first real cohomology is injective. When the complex codimension is at least two, relative general position also moves bounding two-chains away; restriction is then an isomorphism. The argument uses a locally finite Whitney stratification and finite chains, with the boundary kept fixed. It does not assert degree-two invariance.

A modification with smooth target is an isomorphism off an analytic subset of the target of codimension at least two. Restriction downstairs is therefore an isomorphism. Upstairs its complement may contain divisors, so restriction is initially only injective. Surjectivity upstairs follows by extending a class from the common open set to the target and pulling it back. The restriction diagram gives the claimed degree-one isomorphism, and Poincaré duality gives the degree-(2n-1) pushforward isomorphism.

The preceding positive-current lemma supplies equivalence of pseudoeffectivity for a class and its pullback, and injectivity of Bott--Chern pullback in bidegree (1,1). Holomorphic pullback commutes with d^c, so it identifies the preimages of the pseudoeffective cones and their kernels. The already proved strict duality then identifies the Lee--Gauduchon cones. For a graph resolution p:Z->X, q:Z->Y, the degree-one map is (q^*)^(-1)p^*. Its agreement on a common isomorphism locus and injectivity of restriction make the identification independent of the resolution.
