# Standard multiplier-ideal envelope notation

At the user's request, the book now writes P_theta[u]_{I}, with calligraphic I, for the multiplier-ideal envelope. The shorthand Q_theta[u] is removed from the definition and from every subsequent occurrence, including changed backgrounds, nested projections, operators on envelope curves, trace identities and the singular-space appendix. Bare operators are written P_theta[dot]_{I}. The appendix writes P_theta[u]_{I}^{res}, retaining the resolution-based meaning and its distinction from an intrinsic ideal on a singular local ring.

There are 136 ideal-envelope operator occurrences in thirteen source files: 135 parsed replacements and removal of the alias in the definition. Five prose references to Q are expressed directly in terms of the ordinary or multiplier-ideal envelope. Two of the operator occurrences are preceded immediately by scalar coefficients; the second pass explicitly includes them. The revised definition uses only the standard notation. No additional shorthand macro is introduced. The main README and generated source-package guide use the same terminology.

The mathematical content is unchanged. Every pre-existing label is preserved. Unrelated Q symbols, including Hermitian matrices, quadratic forms, relative convex envelopes and the rational-number symbol, are preserved. The appendix's unresolved intrinsic trace and regularization questions are unchanged in substance.
