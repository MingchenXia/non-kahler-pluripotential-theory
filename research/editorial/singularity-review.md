## A shorter analytic approximation proof

For the fixed Hermitian background omega, put c_j=(1+epsilon_j)^(-1), b_j=c_j a_j and q_j=Q_{(1+epsilon_j)omega}[a_j]. The earlier theorem already proves q_j decreases to Q_omega[u]. Analytic envelope equality and exact homogeneity now give P_omega[b_j]=Q_omega[b_j]=c_j q_j. Since q_j is nonpositive and decreases while c_j increases to one, these products decrease to the same limit. They lie between the genuine integrable Q_omega[u] and zero, giving L1 convergence directly. This replaces the repeated divisorial squeeze, ideal-identification argument and normalization/restoration of additive constants.

Rational error bounds still give rational analytic coefficients. The approximants themselves converge in L1 by scalar rescaling, and their ordinary types decrease because their original types decrease, their coefficients increase and each potential is bounded above. No mass hypothesis is added or removed. All original conclusions, labels and result statements are retained.
