# De Rham classes and linear current masses

Checkpoint80 adds a corollary of the checked strongly Gauduchon criterion. All preceding manuscript text, three formal statements and existing labels in the affected source are preserved. No new theorem of Popovici is used, and no novelty is claimed.

For any fixed Hermitian h and any norm on H^2_dR, the corollary characterizes existence of a strongly Gauduchon metric by the estimate integral T h^(n-1)<=C||[T]_dR|| for all closed positive (1,1)-currents. The forward proof uses the defining closed complementary form Theta: compare h^(n-1) with its positive pure-type part, then bound the resulting fixed de Rham pairing by its dual norm. In particular h itself need not be closed or Gauduchon. The converse follows from the already checked positive-exact-current criterion.

For classes in a compact set, the estimate bounds every current's linear mass. Positive-current compactness and testing against finitely many closed Poincaré-dual forms show that the family is weakly compact with its cohomology constraint preserved. Applying this to a convergent sequence of classes proves that the de Rham image of the pseudoeffective Bott--Chern cone is closed. Addition and nonnegative scaling give convexity. If opposite classes have positive representatives, their sum is positive and exact; the bound makes the sum zero, so both representatives vanish. Thus the image cone contains no nonzero line.

The result does not make the Bott--Chern-to-de-Rham map injective, even on the pseudoeffective cone: the existing balanced example has distinct positive classes alpha and beta with the same de Rham image. Nor does closedness and absence of lines in the image cone alone characterize strongly Gauduchon geometry: in the Calabi--Eckmann example H^2_dR is zero. Neither nonlinear upper/lower mass nor uniformity under varying complex structures follows from this fixed-manifold current estimate.
