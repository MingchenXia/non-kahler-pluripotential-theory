## Scope and proof dependencies

The background is ANY smooth big real (1,1)-form. It need not be semipositive or closed. Neither BMP nor metric PVP nor a positive lower relative volume is assumed. The bounded-mass hypotheses earlier in BGL25 are explicitly dropped at the start of its Section4.

The local comparison proof uses ordinary bounded Bedford–Taylor envelope contact on a ball, plus the already proved local nonclosed ordered-contact inequality. In global strict domination, a slightly stronger multiple of the fixed analytic pole barrier confines an infimizing sequence to a compact subset of its complement. Cancellation of positive measures occurs locally where bounded-product masses are finite. No global infinite masses are subtracted.

The reciprocal construction is v=eta+(1+eta-rho-s)^(-1). Its two positive-current coefficients sum to one. This is why no positivity of theta itself is required. The bounded subsolution premise is applied only to the Hermitian form omega. Analytic removability recovers the exact smooth lower curvature bound by a freezing argument.

The Gauduchon estimate uses the globally defined LINEAR current theta_u wedge gamma^(n-1). Its possible mass on the polar set is retained. It is not a discarded degree-one non-pluripolar product. Trace positivity and Stokes then bound exponential suprema and the normalization constants.
