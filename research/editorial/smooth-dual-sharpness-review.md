The nonclosed example works on every compact complex manifold of dimension at least two. Choose a Gauduchon metric gamma and a smooth psi with ddc psi<=-gamma near a point. A cutoff a=L chi vanishes near that point and is large outside the neighborhood. Then omega=a gamma-ddc psi is Hermitian by a two-region positivity check. The smooth potential psi solves the nonzero density a^n gamma^n, which vanishes on an open set. Stokes requires only ddc gamma^(n-1)=0 and makes gamma attain the Gauduchon bound. Thus the threshold is one. If omega were closed, the preceding characterization would contradict the open zero set, so omega is nonclosed. The construction chooses the background; it does not prescribe this behavior for an arbitrary fixed metric.
