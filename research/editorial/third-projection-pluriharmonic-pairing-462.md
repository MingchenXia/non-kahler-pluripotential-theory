Fiber integration of theta_u^(p+1) theta_v^(k−1) along the third projection gives a smooth strongly positive closed (p,p)-form Phi. Its cohomology class equals the normalized base Fubini–Study pth power. Indeed, the projection classes are H−D_A, H−D_B and H−D_C. Distinct exceptional factors vanish because their divisors are disjoint. The excluded endpoint p=k−1 cannot be inferred: theta_u^k is identically zero there.

The smooth projective-space dd c lemma then gives a smooth primitive for Phi minus that Fubini–Study power.
