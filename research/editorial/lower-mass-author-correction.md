# Lower-mass source locator correction — 21 September 2026

The original draft `section_vanishing_lower_mass.tex` and its audit submission
remain unchanged. Version `section_vanishing_lower_mass_v2.tex` changes exactly
one citation locator: `[Theorem I]{Siu74}` becomes `[Main Theorem, p.53]{Siu74}`.

Root downloaded the original published scan from
https://people.math.harvard.edu/~siu/siu_reprints/siu_analyticity_lelong_set_invent1974.pdf
and visually inspected PDF pages2–3, printed pp.53–54. On p.53 the Main Theorem
states analyticity of positive Lelong superlevel sets of a positive closed
current on an open subset of complex Euclidean space. The separately numbered
Theorem1 concerns extension of positive currents. Local smooth correction makes
the Main Theorem applicable to the quasi-psh potential in the submitted proof.
The user-supplied counterexample has the same inaccurate locator as v1.

This corrects a bibliographic locator only. All mathematical statements, constants
and deductions are byte-identical. The audit of v1 remains independent; its final
verdict must be read and any further findings addressed. The corrected draft has
not yet replaced the production book.
