Thus the non-pluripolar measure is exactly the full curvature measure minus (1-tau)nu_j. Its mass is tau for every j and its weak limit is tau theta. It does not establish incompleteness of the positive-mass locus or make a claim about capacity convergence. The distinction is between continuity for two specified topologies, not well-definedness of the curve mass.
