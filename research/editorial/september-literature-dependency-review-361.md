Their integration pairing defines a projection commuting with exterior differentiation. A holonomy average identifies this projection with a positivity-preserving operator. Averaging a balanced `(n-1)`-st power and taking its unique positive root gives a parallel balanced metric; averaging a pluriclosed form gives a parallel pluriclosed metric. Its positive volume maximum lies in the interior and is balanced by the first variation. A metric simultaneously balanced and pluriclosed is Kähler. The resulting Kähler metric is `D`-parallel.
