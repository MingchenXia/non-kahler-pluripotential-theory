## Compact-obstacle regularizations

Let X be connected and compact, theta a smooth big real (1,1)-form, K a compact non-pluripolar set and psi any bounded real function on K. Let R be the raw supremum of genuine theta-plurisubharmonic functions constrained by u<=psi at every point of K, and Q the corresponding raw supremum with the inequality required quasi-everywhere on K.

The function V_theta+inf_K psi is a pointwise competitor because V_theta<=0. Both families therefore contain a genuine potential. Applying the compact-obstacle upper-bound lemma with the continuous constant sup_K psi gives a common global upper bound C for the larger family. The exact-background upper-envelope argument then makes R* and Q* genuine theta-plurisubharmonic functions.

Fix a quasi-everywhere competitor u. Define a bounded obstacle f on all of X by f=psi on K and f=max(sup_X u, sup_K psi) on X\K. Then u<=f quasi-everywhere on X. The existing big pointwise-competitor lemma gives genuine u_t in PSH(X,theta), satisfying u_t<=f everywhere and u_t->u almost everywhere. Thus every u_t is a pointwise compact-obstacle competitor, and R*>=u_t. Passage to the limit gives R*>=u almost everywhere, hence everywhere by canonical quasi-plurisubharmonic order. Taking the supremum and regularization gives R*>=Q*. The reverse inequality follows from inclusion of the families.

Both envelopes have minimal singularities: V_theta+inf_K psi<=R*=Q*<=V_theta+C. Indeed the upper-bound-normalized envelope is a competitor for V_theta. They are bounded when theta is semipositive, since V_theta=0 in that case. No boundedness conclusion is assigned to an arbitrary big background.

If E is a pluripolar subset of K, the raw family constrained only on K\E lies between the pointwise family and the quasi-everywhere family. Its regularization is therefore the same. E need not be closed, and K\E need not be compact. This argument keeps the original compact K for uniform upper bounds; it does not apply the compact-obstacle lemma to a noncompact deletion. No equality of the raw suprema is asserted.

## Logarithmic pole barriers

Let E be a pluripolar subset of C^n, with the ambient-point definition used in Chapter 1. Write E_j=E intersect the closed Euclidean ball of radius j. Each E_j is locally pluripolar on P^n: affine points have local witnesses, while a neighborhood of the hyperplane at infinity avoids that bounded set. Global Josefson gives a genuine quasi-plurisubharmonic pole function on P^n for each E_j. Rescale its curvature lower bound and normalize its supremum to zero to obtain p_j in PSH(P^n,theta_1), theta_1=omega_FS/2, with p_j<=0 and p_j=-infinity on E_j.

Normalized compactness gives uniform L1 bounds for the p_j. The partial sums p_N=sum_{j<=N}2^(-j)p_j decrease, are uniformly L1-bounded and satisfy theta_1+dd^c p_N>=0: the weights sum to at most one and theta_1 is positive. The canonical decreasing-limit theorem gives a genuine theta_1-plurisubharmonic limit p. Every point of E belongs to some E_j and is a pole of the corresponding nonpositive summand, so p=-infinity on E. The affine/projective correspondence gives b=p+chi/2 in L_1, where chi=log(1+||z||^2). Scaling gives a genuine pole barrier in L_delta for every delta>0. The local affine restriction is genuine because a proper compact quasi-plurisubharmonic function cannot be identically minus infinity on an open chart.

On a non-pluripolar compact K the barrier has a finite supremum: local upper bounds give finiteness above and non-pluripolarity excludes b=-infinity on all of K. Subtracting that supremum makes b<=0 everywhere on K. Thus for u in L_delta constrained by a bounded psi off a pluripolar E subset K, with m=inf_K psi, the functions

    u_epsilon=(u+epsilon*b+epsilon*m)/(1+epsilon)

are genuine L_delta competitors satisfying u_epsilon<=psi at every point of K. Here b is first chosen in L_delta. Off E the convex obstacle estimate uses b<=0 and m<=psi; on E the pole term enforces the constraint. Almost-everywhere convergence and canonical order prove the regularized equality directly. Alternatively the compact-obstacle equality follows from the matched projective obstacle psi-delta*chi/2. This checks the factor delta/2 and supplies the compact quasi-everywhere identity needed for the selected recent weighted-envelope source. It does not adopt that source's general product formula, weighted derivative, plurifine decomposition or chair example.
