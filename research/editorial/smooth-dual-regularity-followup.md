Starting from a in L1, this bootstraps to W^{k,1}_loc for every k, then smoothness. Elliptic regularity applied to ddc u=a gamma-omega makes u smooth. The density root therefore has a smooth nonnegative representative; strict positivity is not claimed for nonclosed omega.
