## The birational identities

For a modification pi:Y->X of smooth compact manifolds, use R_pi=K_Y tensor pi^*K_X^{-1} and its canonical Jacobian section J_pi. A codimension-at-least-two exceptional image permits Hartogs extension. Dividing an upstairs adjoint section by J off the exceptional set gives a downstairs section, and Hartogs plus the identity theorem proves the exact global identification. Local change of variables and finite covers over compact inverse images then give the multiplier-ideal transformation. The fixed auxiliary bundle is not raised to the tensor degree. An uncorrected pullback identity for multiplier-ideal sheaves would be false and is not asserted.

The all-weight ideal order is treated separately. Its reverse direction follows by adjoint pushforward. For the forward direction, fix a local modification over a coordinate neighborhood of Y and compose it with pi. This composite need not extend to a proper modification over a coordinate neighborhood of X. The proof therefore reapplies the Bergman lower bound and radial-integrability argument on relatively compact patches of the composite map. Generic one-to-one change of variables supplies the local A_F(E)/m discrepancy estimate. Pulled-back ideal inclusions, the limit at each fixed divisor and point blowups give all local Lelong inequalities upstairs. No global extension of the local modification is assumed.

The potential and envelope descent proposition retains a CLOSED background. Local ddc potentials and psh removability give the exact pullback bijection. A continuous obstacle on the whole manifold permits conversion of quasi-everywhere inequalities to everywhere inequalities. Together with ideal-order invariance and the already proved largest-member property of partial envelopes this identifies the envelopes without a formal interchange of pullback and usc regularization. The NPP pushforward identity follows on the isomorphism open sets and then across analytic sets, which both measures ignore. It is valid as an extended-measure identity before finiteness is known.

Smooth probability volumes give a smooth positive metric on R_pi whose Jacobian squared norm times the upstairs volume equals the pulled-back downstairs smooth top form. Its frame norm is a ratio of positive smooth volume densities, so it remains nonsingular where J vanishes. This produces an exact isometry for the continuous weighted norms. Orthonormal bases then yield B_Y=|J|^2 pi^*B_X and pi_*beta_Y=beta_X. The formula applies to arbitrary quasi-psh selecting weights and requires neither BMP nor a Kähler manifold. It defines no pullback of an arbitrary singular measure.
