## w7-1/n1

All conventions are those in the question, in particular dd^c=(i/(2 pi))partial barpartial. No Monge-Ampere existence theorem is used below.

LEMMA (full-infimum sandwich). Let M be a compact complex n-fold with a Kähler form Omega. Let B be a smooth real closed semipositive (1,1) form. Let theta be a smooth nef background, and assume
  (1) theta <= C B pointwise,
  (2) theta+dd^c phi >= c B as currents,
where C,c>0 and phi is a globally defined locally integrable real function. Suppose theta+epsilon h has a smooth positive potential representative for epsilon>0, as in the question. Let mu be any smooth strictly positive probability volume and h any smooth positive Hermitian form. Then there are positive constants k,K independent of 0<epsilon<=1 such that
  k lambda_mu(B+epsilon Omega) <= lambda_mu(theta+epsilon h)
       <= K lambda_mu(B+epsilon Omega).
The same conclusion holds when theta itself is semipositive, in which case the positive-representative hypothesis is automatic.

PROOF. Compactness gives c_h Omega<=h<=C_h Omega. For every smooth positive Gauduchon gamma, distributional integration by parts gives
  integral_M dd^c phi wedge gamma^(n-1)
    = integral_M phi dd^c gamma^(n-1)=0.
The pairing is well defined because gamma is smooth and phi is locally integrable. A positive current wedged with the smooth positive form gamma^(n-1) is a positive measure. Consequently
  integral (theta+epsilon h) wedge gamma^(n-1)
    >= min(c,c_h) integral (B+epsilon Omega) wedge gamma^(n-1).
The upper inequality follows pointwise from (1) and h<=C_h Omega, with max(C,C_h). Divide by the same positive D_mu(gamma) and take the infimum over ALL positive Gauduchon gamma. This proves the comparison; no subset of tests is substituted for the prescribed cone.

If B has constant rank r, then
  k_0 epsilon^(n-r) <= V_mu(theta+epsilon h)
       <= K_0 epsilon^(n-r).
Here and below the constants can depend on the fixed background, mu and h, but not on epsilon.

For completeness, this second statement follows from the variational definition, not merely from computing a determinant. Put A_epsilon=B+epsilon Omega. The r positive eigenvalues of B with respect to Omega have a common positive lower bound and a finite upper bound, by compactness and constant rank. Hence there are L,U>0 such that
  L epsilon^(n-r) mu <= A_epsilon^n <= U epsilon^(n-r) mu,
for 0<epsilon<=1. This includes r=0.

At each point the matrix arithmetic-geometric mean inequality gives, for every positive gamma,
  A_epsilon wedge gamma^(n-1)
    >= (A_epsilon^n/gamma^n)^(1/n) gamma^n.
Using the pointwise lower volume bound and integrating proves, for EVERY positive Gauduchon test,
  integral A_epsilon wedge gamma^(n-1)
    >= L^(1/n) epsilon^((n-r)/n) D_mu(gamma).
Thus lambda_mu(A_epsilon)>=L^(1/n)epsilon^((n-r)/n).

For the upper bound take gamma=A_epsilon. It is Kähler, hence Gauduchon. Write A_epsilon^n=f_epsilon mu. Since mu(M)=1,
  D_mu(A_epsilon)=integral f_epsilon^((n-1)/n) mu
    >= L^((n-1)/n)epsilon^((n-r)(n-1)/n),
while integral A_epsilon^n<=U epsilon^(n-r). This gives
  lambda_mu(A_epsilon)<=U L^(-(n-1)/n)epsilon^((n-r)/n).
Raising to the nth power and using the sandwich comparison proves the assertion. In dimension one the same computation reduces to lambda_mu(A)=integral A, since D_mu(gamma)=integral mu=1.

In particular a_minus(theta)=a_plus(theta)=n-r. Constants in two-sided bounds disappear upon division by log epsilon. The lemma uses no bounded-mass hypothesis and does not assert anything about an arbitrary nonclosed background lacking the stated sandwich.

## w7-1/n2

This gives a completely specified geometric family. The elementary geometric facts used here are reconstructed directly, so there is no unverified external existence or volume theorem.

I. THE FACTORS.
(A) A plain factor is Z=P^m, m>=1, with normalized Fubini-Study form H=omega_m, the curvature of O(1) with its standard metric. Put kappa=H; there is no Q generator.
(B) A blown factor has m>=2 and 0<=ell<=m-2. Let L=P^ell be the coordinate linear subspace of P^m with coordinates [z_0:...:z_ell:w_0:...:w_(m-ell-1)] and equations w=0. Put r=m-ell-1 (so 1<=r<m). Define
  Z={(x,[t_0:...:t_r]) in P^m x P^r : w_a t_b=w_b t_a for all a,b}.
Let pi:Z->P^m and q:Z->P^r be the projections. This is the blowup of L: on a chart t_j!=0 the equations are w_a=t_a w_j (with t_j=1), which are precisely the usual blowup charts for the ideal (w_0,...,w_r). These charts also show smoothness. For fixed [t], write w=lambda t; the remaining homogeneous coordinates [z_0:...:z_ell:lambda] give a fiber P^(ell+1). The local t-chart descriptions trivialize these fibers, so q is a holomorphic submersion, with rank r.

Set H=pi*omega_m, Q=q*omega_r, and kappa=H+Q. H and Q are closed semipositive. Q has constant rank r. The inclusion of Z into P^m x P^r is a smooth holomorphic embedding, and the sum of the two Fubini-Study forms on that product is positive; therefore kappa is Kähler.

The exceptional divisor E is {w=0}. The coordinate section w_j of pi*O(1) has divisor E+q^{-1}{t_j=0}, with the identity understood in every blowup chart. Thus
  O(E)=pi*O(1) tensor q*O(-1).
Equip this line bundle with the product metric, of curvature H-Q. For its canonical section s_E, put psi_E=log|s_E|^2. With the normalization in the question the Poincare-Lelong calculation is
  dd^c psi_E=[E]-H+Q.
One can check the normalization locally from dd^c log|z|^2=[z=0], whose coefficient is one by integration on a transverse disc; the metric term contributes minus the curvature. The logarithms are locally integrable. In particular
  H+dd^c(psi_E/2)=(H+Q+[E])/2 >= kappa/2.       (*)
This explicit current identity, not a big-class existence theorem, is the fact needed below.

II. THE FIXED BACKGROUND CONE.
Let X=product_{i=1}^s Z_i be any product of factors of the above two types. Write m_i=dim Z_i and n=sum m_i. Forms from factors are always pulled back to X. Fix ONCE AND FOR ALL smooth strictly positive real functions f_i on X and, for every blown factor, g_i on X. The functions may depend on any of the product coordinates. They do not depend on epsilon, mu, h, the divisor, or the cone parameters. Define the fixed finite-dimensional cone C_X by
  theta=sum_i ((a_i+b_i f_i)H_i+(c_i+d_i g_i)Q_i),
where a_i,b_i,c_i,d_i>=0. On plain factors the Q term and its parameters are omitted. All forms, metrics and generating functions are fixed before choosing parameters. This is a cone of smooth backgrounds, not a restriction on the Gauduchon test metrics.

Every theta in C_X is semipositive. Therefore theta+epsilon h is itself positive, without adding any potential, for every epsilon>0 and every smooth positive Hermitian h. All these backgrounds are nef in exactly the sense of the question.

Let H_i be ACTIVE if a_i+b_i>0. On a blown factor let Q_i be active if c_i+d_i>0. Positivity of the fixed functions ensures that an active coefficient has a strictly positive uniform lower bound; it never has an isolated or partial zero. Define the effective block rank
  R_i(theta)=m_i                       if H_i is active;
  R_i(theta)=r_i=m_i-ell_i-1           if H_i is inactive and Q_i active;
  R_i(theta)=0                         otherwise.
On a plain factor only the first and last alternatives occur. Set R(theta)=sum_i R_i(theta).

THE INTEGER-ORDER ASSERTION.
For every theta in this fixed cone, every smooth positive h, and every smooth strictly positive probability mu, there are constants 0<A<=B<infinity such that
  A epsilon^(n-R(theta)) <= V_mu(theta+epsilon h)
     <= B epsilon^(n-R(theta))                 (0<epsilon<=1).
Consequently both orders exist and equal the integer n-R(theta).

PROOF FROM THE FULL INFIMUM. Define a closed comparison form B_theta as follows: on an H-active block take kappa_i; on a Q-only block take Q_i; on an inactive block take 0. Sum these forms on X. It is semipositive and has constant rank R(theta). Also Omega=sum_i kappa_i is Kähler.

For the upper pointwise bound, on an H-active blown block H_i,Q_i<=kappa_i. Coefficient functions have finite upper bounds. On a Q-only block the summand is a bounded positive function times Q_i. On a plain active block H_i=kappa_i. Thus theta<=C B_theta for some fixed C>0.

For the lower current bound, let u_i=min_X(a_i+b_i f_i)>0 on each H-active blown block. Add to theta the dd^c of the global L1 function sum_i u_i psi_(E_i)/2 over precisely those blocks. The nonnegative remainder (a_i+b_i f_i-u_i)H_i and all Q terms are untouched. By (*) the result is at least sum_i (u_i/2)kappa_i on these blocks, with additional positive divisor currents. On each H-active plain block theta>=min(a_i+b_i f_i)kappa_i. On each Q-only block it is at least min(c_i+d_i g_i)Q_i. Taking the least of the finitely many positive constants gives
  theta+dd^c phi>=c B_theta.
If every block is inactive then theta=B_theta=0 and phi=0. The full-infimum sandwich and quantitative estimate proved in n1 now apply. In particular all claimed determinants have their variational relevance proved by the all-test AM-GM lower bound and an admissible closed Gauduchon upper test.

III. GENUINE NONCLOSEDNESS.
This cone is not simply a closed cone disguised by a gauge. Its exterior derivative is
  d theta=sum_i b_i df_i wedge H_i+sum_i d_i dg_i wedge Q_i.
Choose the fixed functions so at least one of these forms is nonzero; take a background with a nonzero corresponding coefficient and no cancellation. For example, on a blown factor choose g>0 with nonzero derivative along a q-fiber in a local trivializing chart and extend it by a small smooth bump perturbation of 1. Since r>=1, dg wedge Q is then nonzero. The background gQ is smooth semipositive, genuinely nonclosed, and has order ell+1. Its nonclosedness cannot be eliminated by adding dd^c u, since d dd^c u=0. On a product, fH_1 with f>0 varying on a different factor is another explicit nonclosed example, with any desired inactive-factor contribution to the order. These are projective blowup/product families, not the listed Hopf or elliptic-product isolated examples.

## w7-1/n3

COMPLETE SUFFICIENT-CONDITION AND EQUALITY THEOREM.

Use precisely the factors, fixed forms, fixed positive functions and fixed cone C_X specified in n2. This theorem is a restricted result, not a classification of arbitrary nonclosed nef backgrounds. All Gauduchon infima below use the entire prescribed smooth positive Gauduchon cone. Both mu and h are arbitrary as in the question.

(1) INTEGER ORDER. Every background theta in C_X has the quantitative two-sided estimate
  V_mu(theta+epsilon h) asymptotic up to positive fixed constants to epsilon^(n-R(theta)),
with R(theta) given blockwise in n2. Thus a_minus=a_plus=n-R(theta). This includes genuinely nonclosed backgrounds with orders 0 through n-1 in suitable dimensions and products. The full-infimum proof and the smooth-nef verification are in n1 and n2.

(2) MODIFICATION COMPARISON: AN ENTIRE SELF-CONTAINED CLASS WITH EQUALITY.
Take X to be any product of the factors of n2, and replace any collection of its plain P^{m_i} factors by Bl_{P^{ell_i}}P^{m_i}, 0<=ell_i<=m_i-2; leave the other factors unchanged. Let pi:Y->X be the resulting product modification. On Y use the fixed pullbacks of the old functions and forms; fix arbitrary positive functions for any newly available Q generators, once and for all. The pullback of the old cone embeds into the new fixed cone with zero coefficients on those new Q generators. No background or test cone is varied as epsilon changes.

For every theta in C_X,
  a_minus(pi*theta)=a_plus(pi*theta)=a_minus(theta)=a_plus(theta)=n-R(theta).
Indeed a plain active H block of dimension m_i becomes an active H block of the same effective rank m_i; an inactive block remains inactive; all other blocks remain unchanged. Apply the n2 estimates on both products. In particular the equality criterion in this product-modification class is unconditional: every allowed modification and every allowed background gives equality. This remains a substantive statement when the background is degenerate and genuinely nonclosed, and when pi*H loses pointwise rank on the exceptional divisor. The exceptional-divisor logarithmic potential, rather than a claim of constant pointwise rank of pi*H, is what proves the lower variational bound.

There is a stronger extension, if one uses the closed-nef order result expressly GIVEN in the question. For theta in C_X and ANY holomorphic modification pi:Y->X with Y compact Kähler, equality again always holds. Here is the complete reduction to that supplied premise. Let B=B_theta be the closed constant-rank comparison form from n2. Its current lower-bound potential is a sum of constant multiples of logarithms of canonical divisor sections. Such logarithms pull back to logarithms of nonzero holomorphic sections; they are locally integrable and their divisor currents remain positive. Alternatively, pull back the explicit identities in n2, including the smooth nonnegative remainder terms. Hence
  pi*theta <= C pi*B,
  pi*theta+dd^c(phi composed with pi) >= c pi*B.
The smooth background pi*theta is semipositive, so pi*theta+epsilon h_Y is positive for every h_Y>0. Choose a Kähler Omega_Y. The full-test comparison in n1, which does NOT require constant rank for its comparison part, gives
  k lambda_(mu_Y)(pi*B+epsilon Omega_Y)
    <=lambda_(mu_Y)(pi*theta+epsilon h_Y)
    <=K lambda_(mu_Y)(pi*B+epsilon Omega_Y).
The numerical dimension of the smooth closed nef pi*B is R(theta): (pi*B)^(R+1)=0 identically, while (pi*B)^R wedge Omega_Y^(n-R) has strictly positive integral because pi is an isomorphism over a nonempty open set where B has rank R. The closed-nef Kähler order fact supplied in the problem therefore gives both orders n-R on Y. This extension uses that supplied fact, not an unverified external Monge-Ampere assertion. The self-contained product-modification assertion above does not need even this premise and has quantitative two-sided estimates. No claim is made here about arbitrary non-Kähler sources outside the specified product class.

(3) EFFECTIVE-DIVISOR COMPARISON IN THE SAME FIXED CONE.
The divisor family and its curvature representatives are fixed as follows. On a plain factor use the line bundle O(H_i)=O(1) with its fixed Fubini-Study metric. On a blown factor use O(H_i)=pi_i*O(1) and O(Q_i)=q_i*O(1), with their fixed pullback metrics. Consider any tensor product on X of integer powers of these bundles, permitting negative powers. Give it the induced tensor-product metric. Let
  D=sum_nu t_nu div(s_nu),  t_nu>=0,
where s_nu is a nonzero holomorphic section of one of these tensor products. This is a fully specified class of effective real divisors: it includes arbitrary hypersurfaces in these linear systems, exceptional divisors, and their nonnegative real sums. In particular it is not limited to divisors with a prescribed support. Its smooth curvature representative is fixed by the indicated metrics and is
  eta_D=sum_i (delta_i H_i+rho_i Q_i),
with Q terms omitted on plain factors. Real coefficients are obtained by adding the corresponding integer bundle exponents with weights t_nu. Set
  Phi_D=sum_nu t_nu log|s_nu|^2.
Direct Poincare-Lelong gives
  [D]=eta_D+dd^c Phi_D>=0.
The normalizations and local integrability are as checked in n2. Thus this comparison is removal from an ACTUAL positive current.

The numerical range of these effective coefficients is explicit. On plain factors delta_i>=0. On blown factors
  delta_i>=0,       delta_i+rho_i>=0.                 (**)
For a nonzero section, restrict at a generic choice of all other product coordinates. On a blown factor a generic q_i-fiber is P^{ell_i+1}, with H_i of degree one and Q_i of degree zero on its lines. A restriction that is not identically zero proves that the H exponent is nonnegative. A generic projective line in P^{m_i} disjoint from the center has a lift on which H_i and Q_i both have degree one. Such lines sweep an open subset, so one can choose a line on which the section does not vanish identically. The sum of the H and Q exponents is consequently nonnegative. The same line argument gives delta_i>=0 on plain factors. Taking nonnegative real sums proves (**). Conversely every real pair delta>=0, delta+rho>=0 is realized numerically by delta E+(delta+rho) times a Q-hyperplane divisor, since E has curvature H-Q. On a plain factor the nonnegative range is realized by hyperplanes. Thus the range just used is neither accidental nor an extra positivity assumption silently imposed on the divisor.

Let theta have cone parameters a_i,b_i,c_i,d_i. Impose the explicit cone-preserving residual conditions
  a_i>=delta_i                    on every factor,
  c_i>=rho_i                      on every blown factor.       (***)
A negative rho_i causes no difficulty. Define
  beta=theta-eta_D.
Then beta belongs to the SAME fixed cone, with parameters
  (a_i',b_i',c_i',d_i')=(a_i-delta_i,b_i,c_i-rho_i,d_i).
This is a smooth semipositive form, hence is nef for every h, with beta+epsilon h itself positive. All smooth-nef requirements are therefore verified rather than assumed after the calculation. Moreover
  (theta-beta)+dd^c Phi_D=[D]>=0,
which is exactly the effective-divisorial-residual relation in the question.

The residual orders and their gap are
  a_minus(beta)=a_plus(beta)=n-R(beta),
  a(beta)-a(theta)=sum_i (R_i(theta)-R_i(beta)).        (****)
Here a denotes the common order already proved, not a new invariant.

PRECISE EQUALITY CRITERION.
Equality holds in both residual-order comparisons if and only if BOTH of the following conditions hold:
  • Every initially H-active block retains H:
       a_i-delta_i+b_i>0 whenever a_i+b_i>0.
  • Every initially Q-only active block retains Q:
       c_i-rho_i+d_i>0 whenever a_i=b_i=0 and c_i+d_i>0.
The second condition applies only on blown factors. On H-active blocks the Q coefficient can disappear or increase without affecting equality.

PROOF OF NECESSITY AS WELL AS SUFFICIENCY. If an H-active block loses H, its old effective rank is m_i and its new rank is either r_i<m_i or zero (or zero on a plain factor), so its rank strictly decreases. If H was initially absent, then a_i=b_i=0. Conditions (**) and (***) force delta_i=0 and rho_i>=0. Hence an initially inactive block cannot gain Q, and a Q-only block can only keep or lose its rank r_i. Consequently R_i(beta)<=R_i(theta) separately on every block. There can be no cancellation between a rank loss in one block and a rank gain in another. Formula (****) now gives exactly the stated criterion. This proves a complete equality characterization within the specified fixed background and divisor cones, with no claim that (***) characterizes nefness outside that cone.

(4) SHARP GENUINELY NONCLOSED EXAMPLES AND ACTUAL BOUNDARY REMOVAL.
On the single blown factor Z=Bl_{P^ell}P^m let r=m-ell-1 and choose a fixed smooth positive g with dg wedge Q!=0, as constructed in n2. For t>0 put
  theta_t=tH+gQ.
It is genuinely nonclosed and positive: theta_t>=min(t,min g)(H+Q)>0. Thus its order is zero. For 0<=s<=t remove the effective divisor sE with the FIXED curvature eta_(sE)=s(H-Q). The residual is
  beta_s=(t-s)H+(g+s)Q,
which is smooth semipositive, nef, and still genuinely nonclosed. The theorem gives
  a(beta_s)=0             for s<t,
  a(beta_t)=m-r=ell+1     for s=t.
Thus equality is precisely s<t, while exact removal of the H coefficient produces the integer jump ell+1. As ell ranges from 0 to m-2 this realizes all jumps 1,...,m-1, not merely a single threefold case.

The associated actual positive boundary current is explicit:
  theta_t+dd^c(s psi_E)
      =s[E]+(t-s)H+(g+s)Q.
After removing s[E] its smooth residual is beta_s. At s=t all of the effective mass t[E] is present in this chosen boundary current. This says nothing about minimal perturbative divisorial multiplicities: for these nef forms beta_s+epsilon h and theta_t+epsilon h are smooth positive, so their minimal perturbative multiplicities are zero. The two notions are visibly distinct here.

For genuinely nonclosed examples with nonzero degeneration order and modification equality, take X=P^p x P^q, p,q>=1, a fixed positive f depending nontrivially on the second factor, and theta=f H_1. Then dtheta=df wedge H_1!=0, its order is q, and every allowed product linear blowup has the same order; every modification with Kähler source has the same order by the supplied closed-nef extension. This includes nontrivial blowups of both the active and inactive directions.

SCOPE AND COMPLETENESS. This is a complete sufficient-condition answer: the geometry, background cone, divisor cone, curvature representatives and residual-nef conditions are all explicit; quantitative full-infimum bounds prove common integer orders; modification equality and a necessary-and-sufficient effective-divisorial equality criterion are proved in the stated family. The proof does not invoke a formal determinant without an all-test argument, an averaging restriction, bounded mass, or a new external analytic premise. The optional arbitrary-Kähler-modification extension uses only the closed-nef order theorem already included among the problem's known facts, after proving its variational reduction. There is no unrestricted integer-order claim and no equality classification for backgrounds, residuals or non-Kähler modifications outside these hypotheses.
