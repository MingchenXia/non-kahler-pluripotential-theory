∫(g+dd^c u)^3=V+3∫E_u S,     V=∫g^3.

The pairing on the right is a finite integral because E_u is order zero and S is smooth. Positivity of E_u and S makes it nonnegative. 3. Intrinsic determinant measure. Fix a smooth positive volume dV. In this paragraph the dimension is any n≥2 and T is any positive (n−1,n−1)-current, without a smoothness or strict-positivity assumption. The definition extends to unnormalized T. Γ_(h,dV)(T)=n(dV/h^n)^(1/n) det(C_h)^(1/n)ν.             (1)

Changing ν to another dominating measure gives the same measure, since det(fC)^(1/n)=f det(C)^(1/n) for f≥0, with zero-density sets treated as above.

Let π:X→B be the specified holomorphic principal elliptic bundle over a compact connected Kähler surface, with invariant (1,0)-form η restricting to dz on the fiber E=C/Λ. Pullbacks of base forms are suppressed temporarily in the calculation. Since dη=F=α+iβ has type (1,1),

∂η=0,   bar∂η=F,   ∂barη=bar F,   bar∂barη=0.

Closedness and pure type of F imply ∂F=bar∂F=0, and likewise for bar F. Put θ=iη∧barη. The graded product rule gives

bar∂θ=iF∧barη,
∂bar∂θ=iF∧bar F,
dd^c θ=−F∧bar F/(2π)=−(α^2+β^2)/(2π).

Here real two-forms commute, so the cross terms cancel. Thus for g=πω+sθ,

dd^c g=−sπQ/(2π),       Q=α^2+β^2.

The form g is positive definite: πω is positive on the quotient of the tangent space by the vertical complex line, and θ is positive on that line because η restricts to dz. If both terms vanish on a vector, that vector is vertical and η on it vanishes, so it is zero. Also θ^2=0, and base dimension two gives

g^2=(πω)^2+2sπω∧θ,
dd^c(g^2)=2sπω∧dd^c θ=−(s/π)π(ω∧Q)=0.

There are no omitted mixed differential terms in this equality, because the pulled-back ω is both ∂- and bar∂-closed. The nonzero volume term is

g^3=3sπ(ω^2)∧iη∧barη.

In local holomorphic bundle charts, terms of η containing horizontal differentials disappear when wedged with the base top form. Fubini's theorem and a partition of unity on B therefore give

V=∫_X g^3=3s A_E ∫_B ω^2,       A_E=∫_E i dz∧dbar z.

The total space is compact and connected: finitely many relatively compact local trivializations over the compact connected base have compact torus fibers, and a path in the base can be lifted piecewise, with any two resulting endpoints joined in the connected fiber. dθ=i(F∧barη−η∧bar F),
d(g^2)=2siπω∧(πF∧barη−η∧πbar F).                 (2)

The primitive sign can be established without a Hodge theorem. At a point choose an ω-unitary frame. The Hermitian matrix H of a real primitive (1,1)-form χ has trace zero. χ^2=−(|χ|^2/2)ω^2,
Q=−(|α|^2+|β|^2)ω^2/2                         (3)

when both curvatures are primitive. In particular nonzero primitive curvature gives Q<0 wherever the pair is nonzero, and cannot satisfy Q≥0 everywhere. The previously proved cubic identity, which algebraically holds also for signed Q, then has the opposite sign:

∫(g+dd^c u)^3=V−(3s/(4π))∫E_u∧π((|α|^2+|β|^2)ω^2)≤V.

For sufficiently small nonzero ε the smooth form g+εdd^c f remains positive, while E_(εf)=ε^2 E_f. Its cubic mass is therefore strictly below V.
