\subsection{A fixed Stein hypersurface for all Finsler metrics}
\label{subsec:fixed-stein-finsler-hypersurface}

Let $X$ be a connected smooth projective variety of dimension $n\geq1$,
and let $E\to X$ be an algebraic vector bundle of rank $r\geq2$,
viewed as a holomorphic bundle. Use the squared Finsler metrics and
Kobayashi curvature convention of \cref{subsec:polar-finsler-convexity}.
A failure of semipositive curvature gives a local hypersurface on which
a metric has a critical point with too many positive Levi directions.
Holomorphic approximation lets one hypersurface detect this failure
for every metric.

We use the classical Oka--Weil theorem in the following form:
a function holomorphic near a holomorphically convex compact subset
of a Stein manifold can be approximated uniformly there by global
holomorphic functions. We also use one-variable polynomial Runge
approximation on a union of two disjoint closed discs.

\begin{lemma}\label{lem:separated-holomorphic-convex-compacts}
Let $K,L$ be holomorphically convex compact subsets of a Stein manifold
$Y$. If a global holomorphic function maps them into disjoint closed
discs in $\mathbb C$, then $K\cup L$ is holomorphically convex.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write $h$ for the function and $\Delta_K,\Delta_L$ for the discs.
Their union is polynomially convex. Polynomials in $h$ therefore force
the holomorphic hull of $K\cup L$ to lie over their union.
Suppose that $p\notin K\cup L$ and $h(p)\in\Delta_K$.
Holomorphic convexity of $K$ gives $a\in\mathcal O(Y)$ with
$|a(p)|>\sup_K|a|$. Choose polynomials tending uniformly to one on
$\Delta_K$ and zero on $\Delta_L$. Their products with $a$ converge
to $a$ at $p$ and on $K$, and to zero on $L$.
For a sufficiently good approximation the product separates $p$
from $K\cup L$. The case $h(p)\in\Delta_L$ is identical with $K,L$
interchanged. Points outside the two discs are already separated by
polynomials in $h$. Thus no point outside the union belongs to its hull.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}\label{lem:dilated-countable-holomorphic-tests}
Let $U$ be a connected smooth affine variety and let $q\geq0$ be an integer.
Put $B=U\times\mathbb C^*\times\mathbb C^q$, with coordinates $(x,t,s)$.
For each $j\geq1$, let $C_j\subset B$ be a holomorphically convex compact
set, let $g_j$ be holomorphic near $C_j$, and let $\delta_j>0$.
Suppose $t(C_j)$ lies in the disc
$|t-t_j|\leq|t_j|/4$ for some $t_j\neq0$.
There are $\lambda_j\in\mathbb C^*$ and $f\in\mathcal O(B)$ such that,
for $d_\lambda(x,t,s)=(x,\lambda t,\lambda s)$,
\[
 \sup_{d_{\lambda_j}(C_j)}
 |f-\lambda_j g_j\circ d_{\lambda_j}^{-1}|<|\lambda_j|\delta_j
 \qquad(j\geq1).
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Choose a holomorphically convex compact exhaustion $(L_j)_{j\geq0}$
of the affine Stein manifold $B$, with $L_j\subset\operatorname{int}L_{j+1}$.
Start with $f_0=0$ and $K_0=L_0$.
At stage $j$, choose $M>0$ with $t(K_{j-1})\subset\{|t|\leq M\}$,
and choose $R>4M/3$. Dilation by $\lambda_j=R/t_j$ puts the new
$t$-projection in $|t-R|\leq R/4$, disjoint from $|t|\leq M$.
Relabel the dilated compact, function and tolerance as
$C_j,g_j,\delta_j$ for the remainder of the induction.
The preceding lemma makes $K_{j-1}\cup C_j$ holomorphically convex.

On disjoint neighbourhoods prescribe $f_{j-1}$ and $g_j$.
Oka--Weil approximation gives $f_j\in\mathcal O(B)$ with
\[
 \sup_{K_{j-1}}|f_j-f_{j-1}|<\epsilon_j,
 \qquad \sup_{C_j}|f_j-g_j|<\epsilon_j,
 \qquad
 \epsilon_j=2^{-j-2}\min(1,\delta_1,\ldots,\delta_j).
\]
Choose $K_j$ to be a member of the fixed exhaustion containing
$K_{j-1}\cup C_j\cup L_j$ in its interior. The interiors exhaust $B$,
and the summable errors make $f_j$ converge locally uniformly to
$f\in\mathcal O(B)$. For a fixed $j$, every subsequent approximation
controls its error on $C_j$, since $C_j\subset K_{m-1}$ for $m>j$.
Consequently
\[
 \sup_{C_j}|f-g_j|
 \leq\sum_{m\geq j}\epsilon_m
 \leq2^{-j-1}\delta_j<\delta_j.
\]
Restoring the original data gives the stated scaled tolerances.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:fixed-stein-finsler-hypersurface}
Suppose $E$ admits no smooth strongly pseudoconvex squared Finsler
metric with semipositive Kobayashi curvature. There is a connected
smooth Stein hypersurface $\mathcal S\subset E^\times$, locally closed
in the complex-analytic topology, such that every smooth strongly
pseudoconvex squared Finsler metric $G$ has a nondegenerate critical
point on $\mathcal S$ with at least $r$ positive Levi eigenvalues.
Its negative Levi index there is at most $n-1$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
An algebraic rational frame trivializes $E$ over a nonempty affine
open subset $U\subset X$. Smoothness and connectedness make $X$
irreducible, so $U$ is dense and connected. Write fibre coordinates as
$(w,t,s)\in\mathbb C\times\mathbb C\times\mathbb C^{r-2}$, and put
$B=U\times\mathbb C^*\times\mathbb C^{r-2}$.
We seek a graph $w=f(x,t,s)$. Such a graph avoids the zero section.

Fix a candidate metric $G$. Its horizontal Schur complement has a
positive direction somewhere, since its Kobayashi curvature is not
semipositive. This condition is open. It therefore holds at a point
$v_0=(x_0,w_0,t_0,s_0)$ with $x_0\in U$, $t_0\neq0$ and $G_w(v_0)\neq0$.
The first two conditions give a dense open set. The zero set of $G_w$
has empty interior: vanishing on an open set would give
$G_{w\bar w}=0$ there, contradicting vertical positivity.

Let $H$ be the Levi-orthogonal complement of the vertical tangent
space $V$ at $v_0$. The Euler identities in the proof of
\cref{lem:polar-finsler-osculating-section} give
\[
 K:=\ker\partial G(v_0)=H\oplus(V\cap K),
 \qquad \dim_{\mathbb C}(V\cap K)=r-1.
\]
Indeed $\partial G$ vanishes on $H$, and its value on the vertical radial
vector is $G(v_0)>0$. The summands are Levi orthogonal. The vertical
summand is positive definite, and the horizontal summand has a positive
direction. Thus the restricted Levi form on $K$ has at least $r$
positive eigenvalues.

Since $G_w(v_0)\neq0$, $K$ is the graph of a linear map over
$T_{b_0}^{1,0}B$, where $b_0=(x_0,t_0,s_0)$.
Choose a local holomorphic graph $g$ with value $w_0$ and this first jet.
Then $F=G\circ\operatorname{Graph}(g)$ has a critical point at $b_0$
with the specified Levi eigenvalues.
Keep this first jet fixed and vary the quadratic jet of $g$.
Its mixed second derivatives do not change, while in local coordinates
$z$ on $B$ the pure derivatives satisfy
\[
 F_{\alpha\beta}(b_0)=A_{\alpha\beta}+G_w(v_0)g_{\alpha\beta}(b_0),
\]
where $A$ is fixed. Prescribe the pure quadratic part of $F$ to be
$\tau\operatorname{Re}\sum_\alpha z_\alpha^2$.
Its real Hessian has eigenvalues $\pm2\tau$.
For $\tau$ larger than half the operator norm of the fixed mixed real
Hessian, the full Hessian is invertible by the Neumann-series criterion.
The critical point is then nondegenerate, with the same Levi form.

Choose compact neighbourhoods $C'\subset\operatorname{int}C$ of $b_0$
where $g$ is defined, with $C$ holomorphically convex and
$t(C)\subset\{|t-t_0|\leq|t_0|/4\}$.
A small closed Euclidean ball in a closed affine embedding of $B$
provides such a $C$; linear functions separate points outside the ball.
Fix a smooth Hermitian background metric and its compact unit sphere
bundle $\Sigma$. Homogeneity makes $C^2$ distance on $\Sigma$ control
$C^2$ distance on each fixed compact subset of $E^\times$.
Persistence of a nondegenerate critical point and openness of the
specified positive Levi directions give a $C^2$ metric neighbourhood
$\mathcal V_G$ and $\delta_G>0$ such that any metric in $\mathcal V_G$
and any holomorphic graph within $\delta_G$ uniformly on $C$ retain
that critical point nearby. Cauchy estimates on $C'$ supply the needed
$C^2$ graph control.

Restrictions of the candidate metrics form a subspace of the separable
metrizable space $C^2(\Sigma)$. This subspace is second countable and
Lindel\"of. Choose a countable subcover $(\mathcal V_j)_{j\geq1}$,
with its graph data $C_j,g_j,\delta_j$.
For $D_\lambda(x,w,t,s)=(x,\lambda w,\lambda t,\lambda s)$,
\[
 G\circ D_\lambda=|\lambda|^2G.
\]
Dilation therefore preserves critical points, real nondegeneracy and
Levi inertia for every candidate metric. A graph tolerance $\delta_j$
becomes $|\lambda|\delta_j$; pulling a perturbed graph back by dilation
restores its original tolerance. In particular the metric neighbourhood
$\mathcal V_j$ stays the same.

Apply \cref{lem:dilated-countable-holomorphic-tests} to obtain one
$f\in\mathcal O(B)$ satisfying every dilated test.
Its graph $\mathcal S$ is biholomorphic to the connected affine Stein
manifold $B$. It is smooth and closed in the open set
$\{x\in U,t\neq0\}\subset E^\times$.
Every candidate metric belongs to some $\mathcal V_j$, so it has the
required nondegenerate critical point on this same graph.
Since $\dim_{\mathbb C}\mathcal S=n+r-1$, at least $r$ positive Levi
eigenvalues leave at most $n-1$ negative eigenvalues.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}\label{cor:hirzebruch-fixed-stein-hypersurface}
For each $e\geq0$, the stable ample bundle of
\cref{cor:hirzebruch-stable-ample-finsler-obstruction} can be chosen with
a connected smooth Stein hypersurface in $E^\times$ on which every
smooth strongly pseudoconvex squared Finsler metric has a nondegenerate
critical point with at least two positive and at most one negative
Levi eigenvalue.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The bundle is a fibre of an algebraic family on the prioritary chart
used in \cref{thm:hirzebruch-stable-ample-approximation}, so is algebraic.
Apply the theorem with $n=r=2$ and the retained curvature obstruction.
\end{proof}
