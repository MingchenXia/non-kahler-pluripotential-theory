# P20: Newton selection and an actual multiplier-ideal series

The [paper](P20-convex-spectrum-partial-kernels.tex) completely proves two explicit sufficient-condition results. The first treats interior rational Newton polytopes on products of projective lines, with an integral affine offset, a smooth invariant strictly convex logarithmic obstacle and a smooth strictly positive invariant probability on the whole manifold. It identifies the entire selected space and full genuine growth envelope, derives Bernstein--Markov and a logarithmic-radial lower bound, and computes intrinsic lattice growth and the positive moment-shell limit. Shell density is expressed for arbitrary ambient Hermitian metrics, including nonclosed forms. Lower-dimensional limits differ from ambient mixed nonpluripolar measures; the full-dimensional limit equals the closed-curvature nonpluripolar Monge--Ampere measure.

The independent second result uses the actual spaces H0(O(m,m) tensor I(m psi)) on the product of two projective lines, where psi is the maximum of the squared logarithmic norms of the pencil sections T1 T2 and Z1 Z2. For every genuine competitor, including noninvariant ones, all-weight ideal domination is equivalent to ordinary upper type. The proof retains the endpoint coefficient using weighted point extension and radial convexity, then uses degree-two pencil rigidity and a maximum principle to determine the full envelope. Equality of the all-weight classes does not identify finite-degree spaces: the actual spaces have three neighboring monomial bands, dimension 3m+1 and Hilbert growth exponent one, and are not multiplicative. Ordinary-type degree-m sections would select only the diagonal band.

For the stated compact logarithmic box, quadratic obstacle and original Hilbert norm, exact truncated Gaussian integrals give the global lower bound, local uniform convergence off the two poles, global L1 convergence at rate O(log(m)/m), extended-pointwise convergence and uniform convergence after exponentiation. For each fixed m>=2 the additive error is unbounded above near either pole, so a global uniform additive error is excluded. Probability trace measures tend to the explicit diagonal-radius/angular-Haar probability; intrinsic m^-1 normalization gives mass three and ambient m^-2 normalization gives zero. No capacity-convergence or universal ideal-envelope assertion is added.
