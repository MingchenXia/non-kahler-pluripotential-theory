# P02: integer orders in nonclosed projective cones

The [paper](P02-nonclosed-projective-degeneration.tex) completely proves explicit sufficient conditions for integer degeneration orders, modification equality and equality after removing specified effective real divisors. The underlying manifolds are products of projective spaces and their blowups along linear projective subspaces. The fixed cone has nonnegative parameters multiplying fixed smooth strictly positive coefficient functions and closed pullback Fubini--Study generators. These functions may depend on every product coordinate. A nonzero fixed-generator exterior derivative gives genuinely nonclosed members. The probability and perturbation metric are arbitrary smooth positive data.

The proof compares the entire Gauduchon infimum with a closed constant-rank form using an explicit locally integrable exceptional-divisor logarithm. Matrix arithmetic-geometric mean gives the lower bound for every test; the closed comparison form itself gives the upper bound. The resulting volume has two-sided bounds by epsilon^(n-R), where each block contributes its full dimension if its H generator is active, its q-projection rank if only Q is active, and zero otherwise. Both degeneration orders are therefore the same integer n-R. All zero backgrounds and dimension-one factors are included. No restricted-test computation or Monge--Ampere mass extremum is substituted for the full variational volume.

Every specified product linear blowup preserves this order, with two-sided bounds on both sides. An extension to arbitrary modifications with compact Kahler source pulls back actual nonzero divisor sections, proves the current sandwich and computes numerical dimension R. It then uses the closed-nef order theorem explicitly given in the original P02 task. The extension asserts order equality, without deriving arbitrary-source two-sided power bounds from that premise alone. Modifications with non-Kahler source are excluded.

The fixed effective real divisor family consists of finite nonnegative real sums of zero divisors of nonzero sections of integer tensor powers of the H and Q line bundles, with fixed pullback metrics. Its exact coefficient range is delta>=0 on plain factors and delta>=0, delta+rho>=0 on blown factors. Under the stated sufficient cone-preserving parameter conditions a>=delta and c>=rho, the residual lies in the same cone. The order gap is the sum of nonnegative blockwise effective-rank losses. Equality holds precisely when every initially H-active block retains H, and every initially Q-only block retains Q. These parameter conditions do not characterize all nef residual backgrounds or all alternative cone representations. The nonclosed backgrounds tH+gQ and removals sE realize every strict gap 1 through m-1 at s=t, with equality for s<t. Actual divisorial removal is distinguished from minimal perturbative multiplicities, which are zero for these smooth nef backgrounds.
