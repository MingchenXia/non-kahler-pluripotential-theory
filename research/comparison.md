## Additional Hermitian theory

Chapters 2–5 develop torsion, mass variation, the energy obstruction, Gauduchon duality and the topology determined by finite linear trace. Chapters 9–14 develop smooth, bounded and prescribed-type equations, supporting probabilities, entropy and selection. Their hypotheses do not impose either metric mass property throughout the book. Positive-exponent potential uniqueness, zero-exponent scalar uniqueness and selection of one limit remain distinct.

Chapter 11 includes both nonuniqueness and positive criteria. The new Section 11.6 proves bounded uniqueness for a smooth nonnegative density whose positive set is connected and dense. Section 14.12 proves whole-family vanishing-exponent selection for fixed nonzero Lp data, p > 1, in ordinary minimal type on a smooth big background. Neither result is a general uniqueness theorem for all critical solutions.

Chapter 25 adds variational Gauduchon volume, current duality, attainment criteria, sharp determinant concentration, and the closed-core nef-boundary subtraction theorem. Chapter 27 treats ordinary closed classes, exceptional loci, divisorial constraints, elliptic examples and completeness. Chapter 28 treats Lee–Gauduchon geometry and its stated invariant-volume theorem. Closed Bott–Chern classes, nonclosed backgrounds and twisted classes retain different invariants. The excluded Nakamaye sources are historical material, not an active chapter or premise newly certified by this reconciliation.
